The Empire State Building is a skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan New York City. It is tall and has floors. Named after the popular nickname for New York the Empire State it is one of the most famous landmarks in the US. When the Empire State Building opened on May it was the world's tallest building. Designed by the architects named Shreve Lamb Harmon Associates it was built at a time when many people were trying to make the world's tallest building but the Empire State Building won. The old Waldorf Astoria Hotel was destroyed so that the Empire State Building could be built. The Empire State Building was completed in days. It opened on May when United States President Herbert Hoover pushed a button in Washington D.C. to turn on the lights. The Empire State Building's design was based on the story Reynolds Building in Winston Salem built in . In the s the Empire State Building was called the Empty State Building because few people worked in the building. The Empire State Building became the tallest building in New York City and the world until the first World Trade Center towers passed it in . When these towers were brought down in the September attacks the Empire State Building was again the tallest building in New York. It stopped being New York's tallest building in when the new One World Trade Center was built. On Saturday July a plane crashed into the building due to heavy fog and started a fire. The building was only slightly damaged but people in the plane were killed. On January an elevator cable snapped causing one of the building's elevators to crash from the th floor stopping on the th floor. Both passengers in the elevator were only slightly injured. The Empire State Building is at Fifth Avenue. The building has its own ZIP code . There is a big Art Deco lobby with shops at Fifth Avenue. People can also go to a lobby on th Street. The Empire State Building has elevators. The th th and nd floors have places where people can look at the city from high above. There is a steel mast on the top of the Empire State Building. The builders wanted to have an airship station on the roof but the station was not opened. Today people cannot go to the mast. The mast has radio transmitters. The Empire State Building also lights up at night.

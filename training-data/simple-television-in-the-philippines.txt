GMA Network Launched on October by American journalist Robert Stewart under the name Republic Broadcasting System RBS it was the fourth television station launched in the Philippines. The network was shut down on September due to martial law but returned in December of the same year after it was allowed by the government. In RBS changed its name to GMA Radio Television Arts after the takeover by the new management by the triumvirate composed of Gilberto Duavit Sr. Menardo Jimenez and Felipe Gozon. It eventually became one of the top stations in the country. The network was rebranded as GMA Rainbow Satellite Network in before it adopted its current name GMA Network in . TV Launched in July by Chino Roces of The Manila Times as ABC it was the seventh television station launched in the Philippines. On September ABC was one of the networks forcefully shut down by then President Ferdinand Marcos due to the implementation of martial law. On February ABC returned to television and became the fastest growing network. On August the network was relaunched as TV . The network is currently owned by PLDT's media arm MediaQuest Holdings of Manny V. Pangilinan. A Z On October ABS CBN entered an blocktime agreement with ZOE Broadcasting Network that rebranded ZOE TV into A Z on October . The channel was available in Metro Manila and nearby provinces and other cable and satellite providers including ABS CBN's Sky Cable. The channel's programming included some of the ABS CBN programs and movies and its other sister television networks as well as feature content from ZOE TV's sister station Light TV and its content partners CBN Asia and Trinity Broadcasting Network. Intercontinental Broadcasting Corporation IBC majority share is a Philippine based media company and VHF television network of the Government Communications Group under the Presidential Communications Office PCO . IBC along with sister stations Radio Philippines Network and sister media companies People's Television Network and Philippine Broadcasting Service forms the media arm of the PCO. Its studios offices and broadcast facilities are located at the IBC Compound Lot B Capitol Hills Drive cor. Zuzuarregui Street Barangay Matandang Balara Diliman Quezon City. Truth Channel formerly known as Ang Dating Daan Television or ADDTV is a Philippine religious television network founded in as ADDTV and then launched on September as Truth Channel. It is the flagship television network of the Members Church of God International MCGI together with UNTV News and Rescue the network's carrier on free to air digital terrestrial television DTT . It is known for its broadcast of Itanong mo kay Soriano and Ang Dating Daan hosted by International Televangelist Bro. Eli Soriano and Kuya Daniel Razon. It broadcasts hours a day on Ultra High Frequency UHF Channel in Metro and Mega Manila Rizal Bulacan Pampanga Laguna Cavite and some parts of Tarlac. The Ang Dating Daan program also hold the longest running religious program in the Philippines since when the program made its national television debut on IBC Channel .

Shimenawa come in different sizes ranging from a few centimeters to several meters in diameter and are often adorned with shide which are traditional paper streamers. When a space is enclosed by shimenawa it is considered sacred or ritually pure such as in a Shinto shrine. Shimenawa are believed to ward off evil spirits and are commonly used in Jichinsai before constructing new buildings. They are also commonly found at Shinto shrines torii gates and other sacred landmarks. Shimenawa are also placed on objects believed to attract spirits or be inhabited by them known as yorishiro. This includes trees called shinboku with the spirits believed to inhabit them known as kodama. Cutting down these trees is considered to bring misfortune.

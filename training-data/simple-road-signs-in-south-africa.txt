Road signs in South Africa are based on the SADC Road Traffic Sign Manual a document designed to harmonise traffic signs in member states of the Southern African Development Community. Most of these signs were in the preceding South African RTSM. A white background signifies the sign is permanent while a yellow background signifies that the sign is temporary. Warning signs are an upwards pointing red triangle and contain a black pictogram describing the danger or obstruction. Speed limit signs are a red circle with the limitation in black. South Africa drives on the left. Before street signs with imperial measurements were used the warning signage used was red triangle above a yellow square and a black legend which showed dangers and warnings similar to the pre worboys design used in the United Kingdom the no entry and one way signs used were red circles the yellow arrow on the one way sign pointed left or right no parking and stopping signs in yellow circle with a slash and a letter P in black for no parking and written parking regulations the yield sign in triangle pointed down in a red circular border and the prohibition signs used were red ring but some with a yellow square plate such as no overtaking or a yellow rectangular plate that would give written warnings like stop sign or numbers on the speed limit sign such as in miles per hour .

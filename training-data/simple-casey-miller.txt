Casey Miller Feb January was an American feminist and writer. She was born in Toledo Ohio USA. Miller went to Smith College and studied philosophy. After college she was a lieutenant in the navy during WWII and worked as a cryptographer and code breaker. She led the publication department for Colonial Williamsburg. She also worked as the curriculum editor for Seabury Press. Later on she began to work on trying to change the use of he him and his in the English language to talk about all genders. She also tried to change other ways the English language sounded like it only talked about men like using the word mankind and Congressman. Miller and her partner Kate Swift wrote many books like The Handbook of Nonsexist Language. Her wish was that English words would treat men and women equally. Miller wanted English speakers to use tey tem and ter when the speaker did not know someone's gender instead of using him for all people. She died from chronic obstructive lung disease.

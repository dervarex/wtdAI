Dalmatian was a Romance language which was spoken in Dalmatia on the Eastern Adriatic Sea. The language became extinct in the Middle Ages. In places it was used until the th or th century. Today few sources are left. Only the dialect of Veglia Krk and Ragusa Dubrovnik have enough sources so they can be studied.

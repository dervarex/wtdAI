DYET TV channel is a television station in Metro Cebu Philippines serving as the Central Visayas flagship of the TV network. It is owned and operated by the network's namesake corporate parent alongside RPTV outlet DYAN TV channel a sister network Nation Broadcasting Corporation and DYKC TV channel under an airtime lease agreement with station owners Radio Philippines Network respectively. Both stations together with Visayas radio flagships Radyo Singko . True FM and Magic . Owned and operated by Quest Broadcasting share studios at the TV Complex Capitol Road Camp Marina Brgy. Kalunasan Cebu City while DYET TV's hybrid analog digital transmitting facility at atop of TV Tower Compound Mount Busay with SFN relay towers located at San Carlos Heights Quiot Pardo and RPN Compound Maguikay Mandaue. September DYMT TV Channel the first broadcast in the whole Central Visayas was launched by Associated Broadcasting Corporation becoming the second VHF television station established in Cebu City at the time until when President of the Philippines Ferdinand Marcos declared Martial Law and it was forced to shut down. April the station was opened as the Associated Broadcasting Company a month after the reopening of DWET TV channel in Metro Manila with the callsign of the provincial station DYET TV as well as its frequency from VHF Channel to UHF Channel which becoming the first ever UHF TV station in Cebu City Philippines and started regular commercial telecast. Its studios were at the th floor of the Sundowner Centrepoint Hotel along Plaridel St. cor. Osme a Blvd. Broadcasting with a watts power capacity the station covers Metro Cebu Northeastern Cebu including Compostela Southeastern Cebu up to Carcar Northern Central Bohol and part of Western Leyte. Elmer Rivera Karaan was the experienced broadcast journalist of ABC Cebu during the hourly news update. December ABC TV stations acquired a new franchise to operate on December under Republic Act signed by President Fidel V. Ramos. In the same year the station went on nationwide satellite broadcasting. In a surge of phenomenal growth ABC Cebu earned its reputation as The Fastest Growing Network under new network executive Tina Monzon Palma who served as Chief Operating Officer. ABC Cebu transferred to their new studio and transmitter complex at Brgy. Kalunasan Cebu City. October ABC was acquired by a group led by businessman Antonio Tonyboy O. Cojuangco Jr. former Chairman of PLDT and owner of Dream Satellite Broadcasting and Bank of Commerce among other assets. March Cojuangco announced that ABC had reached a partnership with MPB Primedia Inc. a local company backed by Media Prima Berhad in Malaysia as part of a long time strategy to make the network more competitive. ABC signed off for the last time at around PHT on August with its late night newscast Sentro as the last program on air and then aired a countdown to its re launch for much of the next day until PHT when the network officially re launched under its new name of TV . Its programming lineup aims towards progressive Pinoys including those young at heart market classes C and D . On October TV Cebu along with its affiliate ABC TV stations was acquired by PLDT's broadcasting division MediaQuest Holdings Inc. from the consortium led by the Cojuangco group and Malaysia based broadcaster Media Prima Berhad. April a month after the two acquisitions was completed as announced by PLDT chairman Manny V. Pangilinan TV Cebu was reformatted with a new lineup of programming and branding as the Kapatid sibling network. May TV Cebu was relaunched with the newly analog transmitter in Mt. Busay Brgy. Babag Cebu City from its old transmitter tower at ABC TV Complex in Kalunasan that had been used to be transferring in . Following the relaunch the station operates on the newly upgraded watt stereo TV transmitter replaced its two decade old kilowatt transmitter for clearer and better signal reception to entire Central Visayas region. On July of the same year the station launched its first regional newscast Aksyon Bisaya with Atty. Ruphil Fernandez Ba oc and Darlene Sino Cruz as its first anchors. April TV Cebu launching its new programs in Cebu was introduced during the network's mall show in Parkmall Mandaue City along with the network's other drama series such as Manok ni San Pedro Antigo and other network's programs. September TV Network decided to cancelled its production following the newscast was ended although its reporters and cameramen were remain employed from the news department as part of the cost cutting measures by the network to sustain its day by day operations. In the future TV Cebu is now downgraded as a relay satellite selling station effective September . February in line with the recent changes within the network and in celebration of its th anniversary TV Cebu was relaunched as The Network with a new logo and station ID entitled Get It on whereas the TV on the northeastern quadrant of the logo has been dropped making it more flexible for the other divisions to use it as part of their own identity. January coinciding with the reformat of AksyonTV to Plus Cebu introduced a variation of its logo. It has also since included the websites of the division producing the program airing e.g. TV .com.ph for entertainment and blocktimers as part of their on screen graphics. August Cebu reverted to its former name TV while retaining the variation of its current numerical logo that was introduced in January as the network. Novembe TV Cebu re upgrading back to an originating station with the launching of regional newscast Frontline Central Eastern Visayas. June TV Cebu commenced its ISDB T digital test broadcast on UHF Channel . December TV Cebu went off the air in a few days following the effects of Typhoon Odette at the evening in which damaged the electrical lines and trees after many homes in Cebu has no electricity brought about the typhoon. On December of the same year however the station went back on the air after power was restored in Brgy. Kalunasan just in time for Christmas Day.

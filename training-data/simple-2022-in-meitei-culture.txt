January A unanimous resolution was passed by the Meetei Erol Eyek Loinasillol Apunba Lup MEELAL All Manipur Working Journalists' Union AMWJU and Editors' Guild Manipur EGM in Imphal that all the newspapers in Meitei language officially called Manipuri language will be using the Meitei script instead of the Bengali script from . February The Meetei Erol Eyek Loinasillon Apunba Lup MEELAL and the Democratic Students' Alliance of Manipur DESAM together organised the International Mother Language Day with special focus on the development of Meitei language and Meitei script. April In Dimapur Nagaland the Dimapur Meetei Council launched a new 'Meetei waistcoat' to prevent wrong identification of the community in Nagaland. May Google Translate added Meitei language under the name Meiteilon Manipuri during its addition of new languages to the machine translation tool. The writing system used for Meitei language in this tool is Meitei script. June The Statue of Meidingu Nara Singh was unveiled in the Kangla Sanathong Western Kangla Gate by Nongthombam Biren Singh the then Chief Minister of Manipur. July The Meetei Erol Eyek Loinasillol Apunba Lup MEELAL organised a national convention on Meetei Mayek script adopting three resolutions. August The Manipur State Legislative Assembly passed a resolution to record the Sanamahi religion Sanamahism in the Indian census data with a separate unique code as an officially recognised minority religion of India. August All Meitei language newspapers published in Bengali script were requested to publish a page of Meetei Mayek script for morning papers and half page for evening papers from August th. August The st anniversary to the Meitei Language Day was celebrated. September In Assam people demanded to include Meitei language as official language of the state. October A ceremony of a day workshop on Rite de Passage of Sanamahism religion was inaugurated in the temple of Lainingthou Sanamahi of Haying Khongbal Imphal. November A ceremony of a month long Meetei Mayek script training programme organised by the MEELAL and Manipur People's Party MPP was concluded. November A day workshop on 'Rite De Passage of Sanamahism' organised at the temple of Lainingthou Sanamahi by the Lainingthou Sanamahi Temple Board LSTB and Manipur University of Culture was concluded. December student organisations AMSU MSF DESAM KSA SUK and AIMS requested to the authorities concerned to include the Meetei Mayek script in the UPSC Mains examination along with the formation of a transliteration committee and a language commission.

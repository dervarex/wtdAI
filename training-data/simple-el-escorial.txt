El Escorial is a famous royal site in Spain. It was built in the late s. El Escorial is a monastery a royal palace a museum and a school. It is located about northwest of the Spanish capital Madrid in the town of San Lorenzo de El Escorial. Five miles from the main building is a hunting lodge called Granjilla de La Fresneda which is also part of the Escorial. The building was designed by the architect Juan Bautista de Toledo to the plans of King Philip II. El Escorial is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is a very popular tourist attraction.

Nick on CBS was a Saturday morning children's programming block for CBS that began on September replacing CBS Kidshow. This Saturday morning block presented programming from Nick Jr. which at a time shared common ownership with CBS under Viacom. When the block first properly aired it was hosted by Face from Nick Jr. On September Nick Jr. on CBS was re branded as Nick on CBS and airing both Nickelodeon and Nick Jr. shows. The Nickelodeon shows were removed in in favor for a return to an exclusively Nick Jr. block. The block's continuity was provided by segments featuring Piper O'Possum. At the beginning of New Year's Eve Viacom split into two different companies with CBS Corporation becoming its own standalone company and Nickelodeon going to new Viacom. Nick Jr. on CBS ended on September and was replaced by KOL Secret Slumber Party on CBS on September as part of a multi year partnership between CBS and Cookie Jar Entertainment. All programming during the block as is standard for Saturday morning network programming was labelled as meeting E I requirements as tennous as some of the claims of educational content may have been.

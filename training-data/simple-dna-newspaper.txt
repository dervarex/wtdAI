Daily News and Analysis DNA was an English language daily newspaper in India. It started in and later became a digital only news platform. DNA launched in Mumbai on July . It was started by a joint venture of media groups under Diligent Media Corporation Ltd DMCL . The newspaper aimed to attract young readers. It offered an all colour broadsheet format and tried to compete with established newspapers in major cities. Over time DNA expanded to publish editions from different cities including Mumbai Ahmedabad Pune Jaipur Bengaluru and others. Despite early ambitions DNA struggled financially. It ran losses in several years before . Because of these problems and changing reader habits DMCL announced that print editions of DNA would stop. The Mumbai and Ahmedabad editions ended on October . After the closure of print editions DNA continued as an online news portal containing news analysis and features. The company said it would focus on its digital platform and planned to launch a mobile application and include video based content. At its peak in DNA's Mumbai edition had a large readership. It was one of the top English broadsheet newspapers in Mumbai then. However the competition from other newspapers and the shift in advertising income contributed to its decline. Though its print run ended DNA remains as a digital news outlet. It is remembered as one of the newer newspapers that tried to challenge older media giants in the s in India. Through its online portal it continues to provide news and analysis to its readers nationwide.

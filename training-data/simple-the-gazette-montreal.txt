The Gazette also called the Montreal Gazette is a Canadian English language broadsheet daily newspaper. The newspaper is owned by Postmedia Network. It is published in Montreal Quebec Canada. It is the only daily newspaper in Montreal that is in English. There were other daily English language newspapers in Montreal in the th century.

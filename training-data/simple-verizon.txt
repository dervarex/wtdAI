Verizon Communications Inc. is a telephone internet service provider and television company in the United States. It also owns a mobile phone company Verizon Wireless. Verizon was created in by merging GTE and Bell Atlantic. These companies had already absorbed other companies some of them more than a hundred years old. The biggest ones were formerly owned by AT T. In Verizon bought the Internet company AOL and the next year it bought Yahoo . Both of these purchases were for about US . billion. In both AOL and Yahoo were part of a subsidiary created by Verizon called Oath Inc. Verizon is one of the few publicly traded companies to have a stock on both the NYSE and the NASDAQ. Its NYSE listing has been one of the companies in the Dow Jones Industrial Average since . Verizon's consumer wireless unit offers mobile phone home telephone and Internet services through a variety of devices. Verizon Wireless offers smartphones using Apple's iOS and Google's Android. Both its basic phones and smartphones use their G LTE or G network. The company offers different voice and data plans for its users. In Verizon offered one year of access to then ad free Disney included in some of its unlimited plans as part of the launch of the streaming service. In the deal was expanded and extended to include access to the bundle of ad supported Hulu and ESPN as part of some unlimited plans. In Verizon changed its plan offerings with access to the Disney bundle and other services such as Apple Music no longer automatically included in its new plans but available instead as add on perks at a reduced price. In Verizon began testing a service called play where customers could sign up for a range of streaming and other subscription services officially rolling it out in with various discount deals. Verizon shut down the play service in to focus on making some subscription services available as add ons to its wireless plans. The same year Verizon also removed access to Google Play Pass and Apple Arcade that had been included in some older plans.

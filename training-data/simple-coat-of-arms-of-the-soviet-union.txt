The coat of arms of the Soviet Union is the symbol of the Soviet Union. It was accepted in and was used until the Soviet Union collapsed in . The coat of arms has a hammer and sickle a red star over the Earth and two ears of wheat covered by red ribbons. Each ribbon has a different language on it showing the motto of the Soviet Union. On July the Central Executive Committee CIK started a project to create the coat of arms. It was finished on September . The constitution of the Soviet Union confirmed the new coat of arms officially in . At first it had six red ribbons on the ears of wheat. The motto Workers of the World Unite was written on each ribbon each in a different language. The first six languages were Russian Ukrainian Belarusian Georgian Armenian and Turko Tatar.

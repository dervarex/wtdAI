The American Red Cross ARC also known as The American National Red Cross is a humanitarian group that gives emergency assistance disaster relief and teaches people how to prepare for disasters in the United States. It is the official US group related to the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies and the United States movement to the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement. The groups offers services and developments programs. Clara Barton created the American chapter after learning of the Red Cross in Geneva Switzerland. In she went to Europe and became involved with the International Red Cross during the Franco Prussian War. She wanted to bring the group to America. Recent presidents and CEOs include Gail McGovern Elizabeth Dole Bernadine Healy Mary S. Elcano Mark W. Everson and John F.

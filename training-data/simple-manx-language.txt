Manx or Manx Gaelic known in Manx as Gaelg or Gailck is a language spoken on the Isle of Man. It is a Gaelic language the same family as Scottish Gaelic and Irish. All of them are Celtic languages. Manx is spoken mainly by people who learn it through interest. It died out as a natural community language in the th century. The last old native speaker died in . Manx is protected under the European Charter for Regional or Minority Languages. Manx began to separate from Middle Irish in about AD and is called Yn Ghaelg Yn Ghailck by its speakers. There became fewer and fewer speakers during the th century when the language was replaced by English.

GoTo which used to be called LogMeIn Inc. is an American company. It makes software that people use over the internet instead of installing it on their own computers. Its products let people work together online manage computer systems and talk to customers. The company started in . Its main office is in Boston Massachusetts. The best known thing GoTo's software does is let a person use a computer from far away. This is called remote access. For example a help desk worker can fix a problem on a customer's computer without being in the same room. The company was first called am Labs Inc. Its chief executive Michael Simon and its chief technology officer M rton Anka had worked together before at Uproar a games website. Uproar was sold to Vivendi Universal during the dot com bubble. The work Anka did at Uproar helped him build a program called RemotelyAnywhere. That program later grew into LogMeIn. In am Labs changed its name to LogMeIn Inc. In the company sold shares to the public for the first time. Its shares began trading on the NASDAQ on July .

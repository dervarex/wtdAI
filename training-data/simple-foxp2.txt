It codes for Forkhead box protein P which is needed for the proper development of speech and language in humans. Versions of this gene occur in many vertebrates where it generally plays a role in communication for instance the development of bird song . FOXP is the first gene found which affects speech and language. The gene is more active in females than in males. In humans mutations of FOXP cause a severe speech and language disorder. FOXP is popularly dubbed the language gene but this is only partly correct since there are other genes involved in language development. It directly regulates a number of other genes including CNTNAP CTBP and SRPX . Two amino acid substitutions distinguish the human FOXP protein from that found in chimpanzees. Evidence suggests that these changes affect the functions of FOXP .

TiVi Monde stylized as TiVi MONDE is an international pay television channel. It was launched at the end of January . The channel was launched by the Francophone network TV Monde which is aimed mainly to French speaking African children years . Its main goal is to teach French to young children. In the other regions TiVi Monde is a block on TV Monde. The channel was launched in the United States in on Dish Network. On the channel was launched in Africa on Canalsat Horizons StarSat and Zuku TV TiVi Monde airs educational programs cartoons children's series and games. Starting of the programs in the channel are African. All the programs are French speaking the channel doesn't make any French dubbing of American series.

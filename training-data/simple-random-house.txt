Random House is the biggest English language book publisher in the world. It is owned by the large German media company Bertelsmann. Random House was founded in by Bennett Cerf and Donald Klopfer. The publisher's main office in the United States is located at Broadway in Manhattan. In October Penguin Random House merged two of its most known publishing lines Random House and the Crown Publishing Group. In Penguin Random House acquired British children's book publisher Little Tiger Group including American subsidiary Tiger Tales Press and added it to Random House Children's Books.

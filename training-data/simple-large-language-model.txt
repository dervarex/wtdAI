A large language model LLM is a type of artificial intelligence that can understand and create human language. These models learn by studying huge amounts of text from books websites and other sources. LLMs work by finding patterns in language. They learn grammar facts and how words relate to each other by looking at billions of examples. The most powerful LLMs use a special design called transformer which helps them process large amounts of text quickly. While LLMs are powerful they can make mistakes. They sometimes include biases from their training data and they can produce incorrect information. They learn from existing text rather than having true understanding like humans do. Before language models were much simpler. The big change came when Google created the transformer design which made language models much more powerful. Important developments include BERT was released which helped computers better understand language GPT was created but was considered so powerful that its creators worried about misuse ChatGPT was released and became very popular with the public GPT came out and could understand both text and images Today there are many different LLMs available. Some are private like GPT while others are open for anyone to use like Deepseek and Mistral. GPT was considered one of the most capable language models.

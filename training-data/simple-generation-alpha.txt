This generation was named after the first letter in the Greek alphabet. Most members of Generation Alpha are the children of Xennials Millennials and Zillennials. Most members of Generation Alpha are growing up using smartphones and tablet computers for their childhood entertainment. Many are exposed to such devices as soothing distractions or educational aids. Screen time with babies toddlers and preschoolers became far more popular in the mid s. Being born in environments where the use of electronic devices is everywhere however has its own challenges. These problems include but are are not limited to cyberbullying screen addiction and inappropriate materials. They are generally described as introverts who spend more time with modern technology than with their favorite toys. Generation Alpha are also being surrounded with adult Internet use starting shortly after their lives begin. The parents of Generation Alpha mostly Millennials have heavy usage of social media.

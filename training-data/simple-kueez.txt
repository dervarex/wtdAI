KUEEZ is an Israeli digital publishing and advertising technology company that operates a network of entertainment and quiz based websites while providing publishing and monetization technology to digital publishers. KUEEZ was founded by Ori Mendi Shahaf Kimhi and Daniel Tony in and publicly launched its consumer content sites in offering light entertainment quizzes and short form games designed for high engagement. In KUEEZ was among several viral content platforms affected after Facebook removed sharing integrations for quiz based applications leading to a significant drop in traffic to such sites. In December KUEEZ was named among the fastest growing private companies in Israel in the Globes ranking in collaboration with Statista . KUEEZ operates a group of entertainment and quiz websites and provides adtech services to online publishers. The company publishes short form content including quizzes lists and general interest articles in several languages. Additionally KUEEZ offers tools for managing advertising inventory and programmatic monetization.

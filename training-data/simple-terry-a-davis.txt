Terry A. Davis was an American electrical engineer and computer programmer. He created TempleOS and the programming language HolyC which he believed he was commanded by God to code in order to bring about the Third Temple mentioned in the Bible. He coded both TempleOS and HolyC from scratch for over a decade. Throughout his life he struggled with schizophrenia. He died after being hit by a train on August th at the age of . Davis was born in West Allis Wisconsin on on December his father worked in industrial engineering. Davis had computer experience since a young age working with early Apple computers in school and later as a teen learning the coding language assembly on Commodore . Davis gained an early internet fanbase on platforms like chan and OSnews. These communities praised Davis for dedication and skill in his programming as well as his perceived lack of a filter. He was known to go on monologues about various topics among them being government agents his most famous government agent rant was regarding CIA niggers in which he said the CIA niggers glow in the dark you can see them if you're driving you just run them over thats what you do fucking CIA niggers. This speech lead to the rise of the internet slang term glowie glownigger which pejoratively refers broadly to government agents. After Davis' death he has maintained a steady cult following among the same programming and Imageboard communities which he was embraced by while he was alive.

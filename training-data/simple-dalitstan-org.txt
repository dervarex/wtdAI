Dalitstan.org was a Dalit advocacy website active until mid . It was one of websites that were blocked by the Indian government following the July Mumbai train bombings. The website claimed to be run by Dalitstan a Human Rights Organization working for the Upliftment of Dalits the Untouchables of India . stan is a suffix for place of or country and forms part of the name of many places throughout Central and South Asia. Dalitstan would mean place or country of the Dalits . The website stated that the Dalits were the original people of India who created the Harappan civilization and described the Brahmins as the descendants of the Aryan invaders who enslaved them. Dibyesh Anand of the University of Westminster described Dalitstan.org as a Dalit nationalist group that also acted as a forum for anti India Christian and Muslim advocacy. Dalitistan.org was one of websites that were blocked in India by the Ministry of Telecommunications of the Government in July . The purpose of the block was to try to stop terror and hate messages on the Internet following the July Mumbai train bombings. The website was started in . The domain was registered by Helen Heklund with an address in Granbury Texas. The website was taken down sometime after May . The domain registration lapsed expired . The rights to the name were then purchased by someone else.

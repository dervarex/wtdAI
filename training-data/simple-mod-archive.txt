The Mod Archive is a website that archives and lists music module files. Users can make charts reviews and ratings of them. MOD IT XM AHX MED STM S M MO MTM AMF AMS DBM DIGI DMF DSM FAR GDM IMF J B MDL MPTM MT OKT PLM PTM STM ULT and HVL files are accepted. The Mod Archive was started in for tracker musicians to share their work. A forum was added in . The website was less maintained from about and was redesigned from November until ending private beta in August . The website's popularity now required many resources so in it stopped being sponsored and was moved to dedicated hardware which is so expensive the community is asked to pay the running costs. Since it has been possible to download multiple files at once using BitTorrent. Users upload files which the website's staff check for integrity and quality. ModArchive was mentioned in the PC music freeware roundup in Sound on Sound magazine. ModArchive v . was featured as Site of the Month in Computer Music magazine.

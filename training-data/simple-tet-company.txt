Tet formerly Lattelekom and Lattelecom is a Latvian telecommunications and technology company. It is based in Riga. The company provides internet television telephone IT data and electricity services in Latvia. Tet is mostly owned by the Latvian government which has of the shares. The other are owned by the Swedish telecommunications company Telia Company. Tet also owns part of the Latvian mobile operator LMT. Tet was created as a state owned company in January after Latvia became independent. It took over parts of the country's telecommunications network that had been operated during the Soviet period. In the Latvian government created SIA Lattelekom together with the foreign companies Cable Wireless and Telecom Finland. Together they bought of the company to help modernize Latvia's telephone network. Until January Lattelekom had the exclusive right to provide fixed line telephone services in Latvia. The monopoly ended after a new telecommunications law opened the market to competition. In the company updated its brand and changed its English name from Lattelekom to Lattelecom. Several companies in the group also received new names while Citrus Solutions kept its existing name. In October the company announced that it would change its name to Tet. The new name officially came into use on April . Its entertainment services continued to use the Helio brand. The Tet Group includes several companies such as Tet Citrus Solutions Tet BPO Tet Technology and Baltic Computer Academy. Together they provide internet television IT services business process outsourcing network construction and information technology training in Latvia. Tet provides internet television telephone information technology IT data and electricity services in Latvia. Tet offers several types of internet connections including DSL VDSL fiber optic Wi Fi and G mobile internet. Tet started offering fiber optic internet to homes in . At first the service had speeds of up to Mbit s. Later that year the company announced speeds of up to Gbit s. The first area in Riga to receive the service was Zolit de. It later expanded to other parts of Riga and to cities such as Jelgava Daugavpils and Salaspils. By the network was available to about homes in more than towns and cities across Latvia. In Tet helped build the Baltic Highway an international data network linking the Baltic states Poland and Germany. The project was developed with Deutsche Telekom and MegaFon. Tet operates thousands of public Wi Fi access points across Latvia. By the company had more than access points and the network later grew to over . Tet provides several television services including interactive television internet television and digital terrestrial television. In it became the largest television service provider in the Baltic states with about subscribers. Interactive television now called Tet TV allows users to watch live television rent videos record programmes and watch shows later. In the Latvian government selected Tet to help introduce digital terrestrial television in Latvia. The service broadcasts free to air television channels across the country. In July Tet announced that it would stop its paid digital terrestrial television service on January . Tet began testing internet television in . In it expanded the service so that people could watch television on computers smartphones and tablets. In the company launched the Shortcut online streaming service which later became part of Tet TV .

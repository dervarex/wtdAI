The Japanese Federation of the Deaf is for those Japanese whose primary language is JSL. JSL has a friend in the Imperial family. Kiko Princess Akishino has studied JSL and is a trained sign language interpreter. She also signs in informal Deaf gatherings. In the Japanese government amended the Supporting Independence of People with Disabilities Act. The new language in the law encourages local governments to increase the number and use of JSL interpreters. As in other sign languages JSL usually called simply shuwa hand talk consists of words or signs and the grammar with which they are put together. Monaghan Leila Frances. .

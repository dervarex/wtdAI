Telugu is one of the ancient and distinguished Dravidian languages of India. It is recognized as the official language of the states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Telugu is an ancient language with a recorded history spanning roughly years. The Government of India formally granted Telugu the status of a Classical Language in . Telugu is often referred to as the Italian of the East. The Reason Much like Italian virtually every word in Telugu ends with a vowel sound Ajanta Bhasha in linguistic terms . Niccol de' Conti a th century Italian merchant and traveler noticed this melodic similarity and coined the phrase. Emperor Sri Krishnadevaraya of the Vijayanagara Empire famously praised Telugu in his epic poem Amuktamalyada writing Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa Among the nation's languages Telugu is the finest . The Telugu script evolved from the ancient Brahmi script via the Old Telugu Kannada script. The traditional Telugu alphabet consists of letters Vowels Achulu Consonants Hallulu Dual Auxiliary signs Ubhayaksharalu Sunna Arasunna Visarga The script is distinguished by its rounded flowing letterforms. Nannaya Bhattaraka Revered as the Aadikavi First Poet he initiated the translation of the Sanskrit Mahabharata into Telugu in the th century. Nannaya Tikkana and Yerrapragada are collectively honoured as the Kavitrayam Trinity of Poets . The Golden Age The reign of Sri Krishnadevaraya the Prabandha era and the era of the Ashtadiggajas eight court poets is regarded as the Golden Age of Telugu literature. Musical Heritage Renowned composers such as Tyagaraja Annamacharya and Kshetrayya composed most of their Carnatic classical compositions and devotional hymns in Telugu largely due to its lyrical cadence. Telugu is spoken by roughly to million people worldwide. It ranks among the most widely spoken languages in India following Hindi and Bengali. It is one of the fastest growing Indian language communities in countries like the United States. Telugu Language Day is celebrated every year on August marking the birth anniversary of prominent linguist and writer Gidugu Venkata Ramamurthy.

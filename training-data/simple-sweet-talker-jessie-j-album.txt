Sweet Talker is the third studio album by English singer songwriter Jessie J. It was released on October by Lava Records and Island Records. Sweet Talker received mixed reviews from music critics. Note Vocal production on every track was handled by Kuk Harrell with the exception of Strip which contains vocal production by Claude Kelly and Personal which contains vocal production by Jenna Andrews in addition to Harrell. Sample credit Seal Me with a Kiss contains an interpolation of Not Just Knee Deep written by George Clinton and Philipp Wynne. Credits for Sweet Talker adapted from Barnes Noble.

Polsat Box is the company of Cyfrowy Polsat's digital satellite television services. It replaced the company name identical to platform name in August . On December the third platform in Poland was launched Polsat Cyfrowy. Initially the platform offered Polish language stations which included Polsat Polsat TVP TVP Polsat's thematic channels and a dozen foreign language stations. During this period Polsat Cyfrowy advertised itself as the only digital platform without a subscription. A new Polsat set top box was launched Samsung DSR and the Open TV software was also launched. In February a mandatory subscription was introduced in the same month a movie channel Filmax was launched Portal ITV was launched in set top boxes and the name of the platform was changed to Polsat Cyfrowy. Another set top box was introduced the Thomson DSI OP. On June Polsat Cyfrowy changed its name to Cyfrowy Polsat. Cyfrowy Polsat commissioned Echostar to produce set top boxes for the platform Polsat Zdrowie i Uroda launched the same year. On October Cyfrowy Polsat modified the logotype of the platform. On May Cyfrowy Polsat debuts on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. On June the company's mobile telephony service was launched under the company's brand. In July the company had nearly points of sale throughout Poland and enabled the use of prepaid and postpaid services of the Rodzinna Telefonia Kom rkowa Cyfrowego Polsatu. In mid this same year the platform was the third in Poland after n and Cyfra to start selling set top boxes with a hard disk and a freeze function freeze frame which made it possible to stop the program being watched at any time and play it at a later date. On November two new services were launched wireless internet and VOD Domowa Wypo yczalnia Filmowa which allows subscribers paid access to popular movies available on specific positions of the set top box items from to . In November Cyfrowy Polsat announced that it was buying of the shares of Telewizja Polsat. also saw the finalization of the purchase of M.Punkt Holdings Ltd. the owner of a distributor of mobile telephony services. In this same year the company launches the HSPA broadband Internet access service and introduces the first HD set top box of its own production to the market. Until then a million set top boxes had been produced in the Cyfrowy Polsat factory. Cyfrowy Polsat starts selling LTE services and the platform's offer is expanded to include Multiroom HD and catch up TV services. Cyfrowy Polsat buys of shares in Redefine the creator of the IPLA online video service and the muzo music service. This year a new service is launched MOBILNA TV operating in digital terrestrial television DVB T technology. LTE Internet speeds up to Mb s and its availability is growing at the beginning of every second inhabitant of Poland is within the range of the service.

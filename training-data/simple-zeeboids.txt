Zeeboids is an application that allows avatars to be created. It was developed by Zeebo Interactive Studios in Brazil and was only released for the Zeebo. Zeeboids works with Zeebo Football Club F.C. games such as Zeebo F.C. Foot Camp allowing players to create custom characters avatars and participate in online rankings. Zeeboids has options for customizing the look of the characters including skin physique hairstyles different uniforms and more. It keeps track of and rewards avatar accomplishments allows players to upload scores take part in online rankings and use their avatars on each other's Zeebo systems. From June during the FIFA World Cup Zeebo Brazil held an online tournament for players with Foot Camp and Zeeboids. The top scorers in each of the four mini games as well as those with the best overall combined scores earned free Z Credits.

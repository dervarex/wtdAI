The Amstrad CPC is a series of computers originally released in June . It was based on the Z CPU the machines were either fitted with or KB RAM. The first models in the line CPC CPC which replaced the cassette with a disk drive and CPC which added an additional KB RAM plus the disk drive were successful. Although in the Plus line was less successful which consisted of the Plus and Plus and the GX games console. In in Spain a version of the CPC was released the CPC it had KB because Spain set a tax on computers with KB or less The CPC's strategy was to be an All In One type computer original packs included the main unit A keyboard and a built on storage device a cassette deck or disk drive alongside a monitor either Color or monochrome. The system was well supported with a lot of software competing with other computers that could run video games such as the Commodore or the Sinclair ZX Spectrum. A wide range of hardware peripherals such as printers joysticks memory expansion and others were available. The Amstrad CPC series sold over million units. The computers were created by Amstrad plc

Swardspeak or Chuva is a secret language that came from the mixed language with the names Taglish and Englog. It is used by gay people in the Philippines. Swardspeak uses word from Tagalog English Spanish Cebuano Japanese Sanskrit and other languages. Names of Celebrities and trademark brands are also used. Gay people use Swardpeak to make themselves feel special. The language changes a lot. People who use the language are called Bekimons. This word is from the words bakla gay and Jejemon. The word Swardspeak came from the 's book called Swardspeak A Preliminary Study . Sward is slang for gay male in the Philippines. Swardspeak is a form of slang. It changes a lot and the meaning of words are hard to know. The rules are explained below. The first sound changes to the letter J or Sh or to the sounds Jo Sho Ju Shu Ky or Ny . The last sound changes to ash is iz ish itch ech ush or oosh as a suffix. Changing a o or u sounds with or er or ur especially with l . Changing places of letters in a word.

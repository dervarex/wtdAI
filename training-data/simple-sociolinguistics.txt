Sociolinguistics is the study of how language is related to society. It also studies how people affect language usage. Sociolinguistics studies how variety in language changes between groups of people because of things such as race gender status age etc. Indian and Japanese linguists first studied the social parts of language in the s. Louis Gauchat in Switzerland also studied this in the early s. However none of them were known in the West until much later. The study of why language changes started during the late 's in the wave model. Thomas Callan Hodson was the first person to us the word sociolinguistics. It was in an article he wrote in called Sociolinguistics in India . This was printed in a magazine called Man in India. Sociolinguistics in the West first came in the s. It was pushed forward by linguists such as William Labov in the US and Basil Bernstein in the UK . In the s William Stewart and Heinz Kloss introduced the simple ideas for the concept of pluricentric languages. This explains how standard language varieties are different between countries e.g. American British Canadian Australian English Austrian German Swiss German Bosnian Croatian Montenegrin Serbian Serbo Croatian . A sociolinguist might research how a particular variety of language vernacular would not be considered correct in a business place. Sociolinguists might also study the grammar phonetics and vocabulary of this sociolect. Similarly dialectologists would study the same things for a regional dialect. The study of language variation focuses on how language changes with social pressure and when in a specific environment. This can include code switching or changing from one language or dialect to another depending on the situation. William Labov is a sociolinguist known for introducing a way to measure language variation and change. He was not the first person to study sociolinguistics but he is known for taking the lead in sociolinguistic research in the West. Lakoff Robin T. . The Language War.

TIC is a free and open source fantasy video game console made by Nesbox. It is styled after bit hardware from the s. It is similar to PICO which is more popular. for TIC Programs can be made in TIC directly including code sprites maps levels sound effects and music. When exported they are stored in cartridges . These can be .tic or PNG files which look like real cartridges . PNGs use a caRt ancillary chunk to store data. Before then they were stored on the image but only stored KiB and were easier to break with an image editor. Programs can also be shared on the TIC website or browsed in TIC itself using SURF. Some demo parties have featured TIC . These include Outline in the Netherlands Lovebyte online and a LINE Fukuoka hackathon in Japan.

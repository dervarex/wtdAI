Kasane Teto is an virtual singer software voicebank . She was originally created on April as a parody of Hatsune Miku. The voicebank for the character was later made. Since then Teto has become popular among Vocaloid fans. Kasane Teto started as an April Fools joke on Japanese internet forum chan. The software was a parody of Hatsune Miku but was later made compatible with the voice synth software UTAU. Voice actress Mayo Oyamano provided voice samples for Teto. The rights holders created the group TwinDrill in to promote the character. TwinDrill agreed to make the future point of contact for commercial use.

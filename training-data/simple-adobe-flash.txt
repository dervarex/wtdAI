Adobe Flash formerly Macromedia Flash was an Animation software sold by Adobe. It was used to create and view animated content that could be placed on web pages. Adobe Flash was used commonly on the Internet by web browsers. Flash was created by FutureWave a multimedia company in . Then it was called FutureSplash Animator this was Flash . . FutureWave along with Flash were soon bought by Macromedia around who in December was again bought by Adobe Systems. From Flash version to it was called Macromedia Flash now it is called Adobe Flash . In Adobe discontinued the Solaris version of Flash Player and made the Linux version only for Google Chrome. The Android version was removed from Google Play. In Adobe discontinued Adobe Flash because Adobe claims that WebGL HTML and WebAssembly serve as good alternatives for Adobe Flash. Flash video games are popular on the Internet with websites like Newgrounds Kongregate and Armor Games are dedicated to Flash based games. Popular Flash games include Transformice Farmville Alien Hominid QWOP and Club Penguin. Adobe AIR allows the creation of Flash based mobile games which can be published to the Google Play and the Apple App Store.

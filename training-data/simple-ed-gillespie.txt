Edward Walter Ed Gillespie born August is an American Republican politician. He served as the st Chairman of the Republican National Committee and Counselor to the President during George W. Bush's administration. Gillespie along with Democrat Jack Quinn founded Quinn Gillespie Associates a bipartisan lobbying firm. In January Gillespie announced he was running for Virginia's U.S. Senate seat currently held by Mark Warner in the election and on June he received the Republican nomination. In October Gillespie announced his plans to run for Governor of Virginia in . In June he became the Republican Party's candidate for governor in Virginia's election facing Democratic nominee Ralph Northam who later won the election.

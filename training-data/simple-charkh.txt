Charkh lit. wheel is an Iranian television talk show that has aired on IRIB TV since . Each episode focuses on scientific topics and is approximately nbsp minutes in length. The series airs every working day night six days a week . The show is aired live and nationwide every evening during the weekdays according to the Iranian calendar on Channel . Shahryar Rabbani and Milad Nouri perform as presenters and the program in each episode hosts guest s from the Iranian scientific community in one of the following fields Technology and engineering Medical science Fundamental science Cognitive sciences philosophy and history of science Environment and natural resources The program is an interactive discussion between the host and the guest rather than a monologue stream and the discussion may be enriched with presentations slide shows movie clips or graphics related to the subject.

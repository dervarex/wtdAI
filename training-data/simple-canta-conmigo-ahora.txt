Canta Conmigo Ahora was an Argentine talent show produced by LaFlia Contents. was the Argentine version of the British program All Together Now broadcast by the BBC One network. The presenter was Marcelo Tinelli. This TV show was broadcast by eltrece and produced by LaFlia Contents. It is a singing contest that brings together experts in the same place. They will meet many singers who seek to win the contest.

Your AI Slop Bores Me is a website and social experiment created by programmer Mihir Maroju. Users ask for text or images from other users who pretend to be a large language model LLM . It is a parody of real LLMs such as ChatGPT. In March the website had been visited million times and was being used by people at the same time. In an interview with Fast Company Maroju said he created the website because he did not like that there was a lot of AI generated art online. He thought these creations were low effort and generic and made life worse for artists. Users need credits to give prompts. They start with credit and get every minutes if they have credits. The main way to get credits is to pretend to be an LLM. Users only have seconds to answer a prompt or seconds if thinking mode is activated. They have a maximum of credits. Many people online like the website. The Daily Dots Rachel Kiley said the best part about the game is that there's really no right or wrong way to do it. Humans aren't LLMs trained on copyrighted material and the whole of the free internet but we do retain a certain amount of the information we've learned from those things over the course of our lives while also being capable of creativity . Chris Taylor of Mashable said the site is amateurish and charming . Aftermaths Nicole Carpenter said the site reminded her of the human touch of chaos .

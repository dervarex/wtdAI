Adventure Time also referred to as Adventure Time with Finn Jake or Adventure Time with Finn Jake BMO is an American animated sitcom created by Pendleton Ward for Cartoon Network. Finn Jake and BMO live in the Land of Ooo which is a nation of an overworld version of Earth that has been nearly destroyed by the Great Mushroom War. Series creator Pendleton Ward said that he wrote the show as though he were playing Dungeons Dragons. Ward's creation is based on his short movie that aired on Nicktoons as a pilot on Frederator's Random Cartoons. After the short became a viral hit on the Internet Nickelodeon's executives did not want to make it into a show. Afterwards Cartoon Network approved Ward's idea of making it into a show in . The same year the series premiered on Cartoon Network on April and it ended its eight year run on September . It was followed by the Adventure Time Distant Lands specials and the Adventure Time Fionna and Cake spin off which were released on Max.

Telecadena is a Honduran open television channel. It is owned by the Televicentro Corporation. Its name is due to the two frequencies that the channel occupies to transmit at the national level channel VHF from Tegucigalpa and channel VHF from San Pedro Sula. Its programming is of a general nature with children's series broadcast in the mornings and programs for an adult audience in the afternoons. The channel is launched in by Rafael Ferrari. In February the station's first news program Abriendo Brecha at nbsp pm was launched directed and presented by Rodrigo Wong Ar valo. Since the s the channel has aired the soap opera block La telenovela del recuerdo inspired by Rede Globo's Vale a pena ver de novo block. On December the block is canceled to premiere new programs. Among other entertainment programs it also broadcasts Honduran shows such as Los del Cuarto a night show broadcast on Sundays from pm to pm on Telecadena de Televicentro alongside great drivers Samuel Mart nez and Elmer Valladares. This program will stop broadcasting in and is currently moving on Canal .

Biodata Information Technology AG was a German company in the IT security sector. It was part of the New Economy and was based in Frankenberg Eder . By the late s it started making network components including the BioNet series for Atari ST computers. In it developed the ISDN router called BIG Bionet Intelligent Gateway .

Sergei Levteev is the CEO and Chairman of the Board of Directors of IBA Group an IT service provider company in Eastern Europe specialising in software services and solutions. Sergei Levteev was born on August in the Russian SFSR. At the age of he moved with his family to Minsk Belarusian SSR. In he studied at the Belarusian State University of Informatics and Radio Electronics BSUIR earning a B.S. in electronic engineering. After graduation he worked as a computer engineer at the G.K. Ordzhonikidze Electronic Computing Machines Plant now Minsk Computer Technology Production Association MCTPA under the state distribution program. From to he worked at Elorg Data a Finland based IT company. After that Levteev worked for four years as Deputy General Director of the computer engineering association BelNPOVT an IT organisation with employees. In when IBA was founded he became its CEO and in he was elected Chairman of the Board of Directors of IBA Group. In the headquarters of IBA Group was relocated from Minsk to Prague. Since then Sergei Levteev has been a resident of the Czech Republic. Sergei supports IT education and contributes to the development of the IT industry. He is a member of the Trustee Council of the Association of Postgraduates and Trustees of BSUIR. Man of Business prize in the category of Most Original Business Idea Implemented by a Domestic Entrepreneur according to the magazine for international cooperation Delo East West . Entrepreneur of the Year elected to the Presidium of the International Public Association of Graduates and Trustees IPAGT of the Belarusian State University of Informatics and Radioelectronics.

Soul of a New Machine is the debut studio album by American industrial metal band Fear Factory. It was released on August by Roadrunner Records. While it was Fear Factory's first studio album to be released it was their second to be recorded. The first was Concrete which was recorded in but not released until years later. German magazine Rock Hard described it as death metal with many other elements such as industrial metal. Soul of a New Machine was remastered and re released on October .

The Korean Broadcasting System KBS is one of the biggest South Korean television networks. It was established in . KBS headquarters are in the Yeouido district of Seoul. The purposes of KBS can be divided into parts. Making good broadcasting culture Creating national culture Globalization of Korean culture KBS started the country's first radio broadcasting service in as Kyeongseong Broadcasting Corporation JODK . After liberation it changed the name to Seoul Central Broadcasting Station in . It launched Korea's first television broadcasting service in . A system of public broadcasting was established in . For reinforcement of public management TV and Radio have not run a commercial since October . First broadcasting HD programs in KBS completely changed to digital broadcasting in . There are regional stations and overseas branches. There are eight subsidiary companies such as KBSN KBS Business and KBS Media which manage KBS content. KBS is run by public funds from the South Korean government. KBS TV Airs current affairs sports documentary news culture and environment. Since this channel never goes off the air during the day. KBS TV Airs drama entertainment and programs for children. Comparing to TV it has less regional self programs. KBS WORLD Airs KBS's famous programs in real time to other countries in the world with a international satellite. KBS Radio News current affairs drama documentary and culture. It also provides disaster broadcasting as the public broadcasting. KBS Radio Family entertainment. KBS Radio The station of social welfare for a neglected class of people and the disabled. KBS FM Classical music and folk music. It also provide Korean traditional music. KBS FM Popular music. KBS Hanminjok Radio The station for the harmony and exchange between North Korea and South Korea. KBS World Radio International broadcasting station. It introduces diverse aspects of Korea to enhance the global network.

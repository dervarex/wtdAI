Analog television is broadcasting television programming using analog signals. Before digital television all broadcasters used analog signals. Analog television can be wireless or can be over a cable television network. Many countries have changed from analog to digital television. Others are in the process of changing and some have not started changing. In the FCC in the US said that all full power stations needed to switch to digital by the th of February . Most had already been running digital channels alongside the analog version. Satellite cable and users with low power did not need to make the change at the time. The FCC gave people up to two coupons to buy converter boxes between January and March . In the UK regions began changing to digital terrestrial television in . The regional phase ended in . Northern Ireland was the last place to replace analog in the UK on the morning of the th of October . Some people were worried that because some digital channels didn't have subtitles that some people would have difficulty understanding what was happening. The London Olympic Games were meant to be held soon which also made people worried that the timetable would affect people's ability to watch it if they did not make the switch completely. Meanwhile in the US two years after all of the full power analog TV stations were shut down the FCC on July made the schedule for low power television. The last ones were in Alaska and converted in January .

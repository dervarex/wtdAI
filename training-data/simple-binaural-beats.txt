A binaural beat is an auditory illusion. It is perceived when two different pure tone sine waves are presented to a listener one tone to each ear. For example if a nbsp Hz pure tone is presented to a subject's right ear while a nbsp Hz pure tone is presented to the subject's left ear the listener will perceive the illusion of a third tone. The third sound is called a binaural beat and in this example would have a perceived pitch correlating to a frequency of nbsp Hz that being the difference between the nbsp Hz and nbsp Hz pure tones presented to each ear. They may help people with pain when they are in the hospital. Some people use binaural beats to help them sleep meditate or have out of body experiences. There are computer programs and smartphone programs that make binaural beats. Many of the claims are not verified at present. Heinrich Wilhelm Dove discovered binaural beats in and published his findings in the scientific journal Repertorium der Physik. While research about them continued after that the subject remained something of a scientific curiosity until years later with the publishing of Gerald Oster's article Auditory beats in the brain Scientific American . Oster's article identified and assembled the scattered pieces of relevant research since Dove offering fresh insight and new laboratory findings to research on binaural beats. Oster saw binaural beats as a powerful tool for cognitive and neurological research. Unsolved questions include how animals locate sounds in their environment the remarkable ability of animals to pick out and focus on specific sounds in a sea of noise known as the cocktail party effect . Several software applications are available for creating binaural beats or producing audio commonly used in brainwave entrainment sessions. SynapSeq is an open source text based audio sequencer for creating brainwave entrainment sessions. It uses a domain specific language DSL to define audio timelines containing binaural beats monaural beats isochronic tones ambient sound layers and other audio events which can be rendered into audio files. myNoise is an online sound generator that provides customizable soundscapes and includes parametric binaural beat generators with adjustable frequencies. Audacity is a free and open source digital audio editor capable of generating pure sine waves and editing multitrack audio. Although it is not specifically designed for brainwave entrainment separate tones can be generated and assigned to stereo channels to create binaural beat recordings.

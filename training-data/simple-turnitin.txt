Turnitin also written as turnitin is an online service that checks writing for plagiarism. It compares a document with texts in its own database and with text from other websites. Turnitin is run by Turnitin LLC a subsidiary of Advance Publications. As of Turnitin was used by over schools and universities around the world with more than million students in these institutions. Some universities decided not to use Turnitin including Princeton Harvard Yale and Stanford. Turnitin started at the University of California Berkeley in the s. A graduate student John M. Barrie made an online system for peer review. Later it became a plagiarism checking tool. Barrie and his partners created a company called iParadigms in to sell the product. Turnitin was introduced in . The website Plagiarism.org became public in and Turnitin.com was released more widely in . Turnitin bought other plagiarism checking companies. It acquired VeriCite in . In it was bought by Advance Publications for about US . billion. It later acquired Unicheck and Ouriginal . Turnitin compares a student's paper with other texts to find similar parts. It uses a private algorithm and searches in several databases including its own stored student papers and some licensed academic databases. Teachers can use the results to check sources and also to help students learn better academic writing. Turnitin also runs the website plagiarism.org and sells another service for publishers called iThenticate. It also has tools for grading and peer review GradeMark and PeerMark . In Turnitin added a tool that tries to detect text written by artificial intelligence such as ChatGPT. Some reporters and researchers have said AI detectors can make mistakes and may flag human writing as AI writing. Because of these worries some schools turned off the AI detection feature. Turnitin has said it expects some errors. It has also said it believes about of papers flagged as AI written were actually written by humans. Turnitin stores many student papers in a large database. This can stop students from copying another student's work because the system can match text between papers. The company also collects public web pages using a web crawler. It also includes content from books newspapers and journals. As of June Turnitin said this database had about . billion submissions. Some students and critics say that forced submission to Turnitin can feel like a presumption of guilt . Others say the system raises privacy questions because student work is stored in a private database. In Canada the student union at Dalhousie University criticized Turnitin because of concerns about the USA PATRIOT Act. Mount Saint Vincent University later banned Turnitin on campus partly for privacy reasons. There have also been legal disputes about student papers and copyright. In one case a U.S. appeals court decided that Turnitin's use of student papers was allowed under fair use in the United States.

Ioannis Ikonomou born is a Greek translator. He has worked for the European Commission in Brussels since . He chose to start learning other languages as a young child after seeing foreign tourists in Crete. He learned languages before he was twenty years old including English at five after he moved to Athens with his family German at seven Italian at ten Russian at thirteen Swahili at fourteen and Turkish at sixteen. He studied linguistics at the University of Thessaloniki then Middle Eastern languages and cultures at Columbia University for an MA Master of Arts then Indo European linguistics at Harvard University for a PhD. His thesis at Harvard was about a text by Zarathustra written in Avestan a form of Old Iranian. He can speak living languages. Some of them are Greek English German Italian Spanish French Finnish Danish Russian Swahili Hebrew Arabic Mandarin and Bengali. It is possible he can speak languages including dead languages such as Old Church Slavonic. He can speak of the official EU languages. He thinks Mandarin Chinese is the hardest language to learn. Chinese is also his favourite language. He is the only translator of classified Chinese documents who works for the European Commission directly. He is gay and married to Tomek Malarski who is Polish. He talks with native speakers from around the world to help him maintain his language skills. His favourite hobby is reading Chinese books and taking notes from them.

LTE Long Term Evolution is a class of wireless telecommunication for phones and other mobile devices for fast internet phone calls and SMS. It is better than older technologies like GSM EDGE and UMTS HSPA because it can handle more data and is faster. The phone companies using LTE are the ones that have both a GSM UMTS and a CDMA network deployed. Because LTE uses different frequencies in different countries only phones supporting multiple bands can use LTE everywhere. LTE was succeeded by a faster technology named G. LTE was created by a group called GPP. They first described it in a document called Release and made some small improvements in Release . LTE is sometimes called . G and is often marketed as G LTE or Advanced G. The first version of LTE did not completely meet the G standards. Later on ITU R decided that LTE and similar technologies could be called G because they were much better than the older G technologies. The LTE Advanced standard formally satisfies the ITU R requirements for being considered IMT Advanced. LTE Advanced is a version of LTE that meets all the requirements to be called True G.

POLYAS is a German software company that develops systems for electronic voting and online voting. The company is based in Kassel Germany. Its software is used for elections by universities associations cooperatives and organizations. The POLYAS voting software was first created in by Wolfgang Jung and Kai Reinhard. The first election using the system took place in Finland in and included about voters. Until POLYAS was part of the German IT company Micromata GmbH. In it became an independent company called POLYAS GmbH. In the POLYAS system received certification from the German Federal Office for Information Security BSI for use in non political elections. In the Social Democratic Party of Germany SPD used POLYAS for a membership vote on a coalition agreement. POLYAS lets organizers upload a list of voters and send access data by email or post. The system supports login methods such as LDAP and Single Sign On. It uses two separate servers to protect ballot secrecy one checks voter eligibility and the other stores votes. Newer versions allow voters to verify that their vote was recorded.

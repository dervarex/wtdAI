The Russian ruble or rouble rubl plural rubli sign code RUB is the currency of Russia. It is also used in two partially recognized countries Abkhazia and South Ossetia. It is also used in the unrecognised republics of Donetsk and Luhansk. One ruble is subdivided into kopeks kopeyka plural kopeyki . The ruble was the currency of the Russian Empire and of the Soviet Union as the Soviet ruble . Only three countries Russia Belarus and Transnistria use currencies known as ruble. The ruble was the world's first decimal currency it was decimalised in when the ruble became equal to kopeks. In the Soviet ruble code SUR was replaced with the Russian ruble code RUR at the rate SUR RUR. In while Russia was suffering from a second economic crisis the Russian ruble was redenominated with the new code RUB and was exchanged at the rate of RUB RUR.

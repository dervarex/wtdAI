Ithkuil is a artificial language created by John Quijada. It's designed to express deep human thoughts clearly and briefly especially about how humans categorize things. It's a mix of a philosophical and logical language aiming to reduce the confusion and vagueness found in natural languages. Ithkuil is known for its complex grammar and large set of sounds though the sound system is being simplified in a new version. The name Ithkuil comes from the original word I ku l which means something like hypothetical language. Quijada didn't create Ithkuil for everyday use. Instead he wanted it for deeper fields like philosophy art science and politics where complex ideas are discussed. Ithkuil can express ideas in fewer words than natural languages. For example the Ithkuil sentence Tram m i hh sma p ukt x translates to a much longer English sentence On the contrary I think it may turn out that this rugged mountain range trails off at some point. Quijada believes his language is too complex to have developed naturally and sees it as an experiment in how languages could work. There have been four versions of Ithkuil the original in a simpler version called Ilaksh in a third version in and the current version called New Ithkuil as of February . The language has been featured in a Russian science and tech magazine won an award in and was later adapted to handle complex sentences more easily. Since Quijada has released music in Ithkuil as part of an album called Kaduat n Wayfarers . Online communities in English Russian Mandarin and Japanese have also formed around the language.

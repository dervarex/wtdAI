Front is a Belgian electronic music group. They were famous during the s. They are known for being a pioneer of electronic body music and as an influence on the electronic and industrial music genres. Front was formed in in Aarschot Belgium by Daniel Bressanutti and Dirk Bergen. Patrick Codenys and Jean Luc De Meyer had also formed a group called Under Viewer at around the same time. The two duos joined together in . In Dirk Bergen left the band and Richard Jonckheere called Richard joined as vocalist. In Front signed with Wax Trax Records in the U.S. and Red Rhino in Europe.

Glottocode is a special code that identifies languages and groups of languages in a big database called Glottolog. Glottolog is a collection of information about all the languages in the world. It was made by people who study languages at the Max Planck Institute in Germany. The Glottocode system started in to help people keep track of languages. Unlike other codes like ISO which are only for single languages Glottocodes can be used for languages dialects and groups of languages. This makes them helpful for people who want to learn about languages. In Glottolog a languoid is anything related to languages that people talk about. It can be A single language like English . A dialect like how people speak English differently in different places . A group of languages like all the languages in one family . For example English is a languoid with Glottocode stan . The Austronesian language family which has many languages from places like Southeast Asia and the Pacific is also a languoid with Glottocode aust . The word languoid helps Glottolog include all these things even if they are not connected by history. A Glottocode has eight characters. It starts with four characters that can be small letters a to z or numbers to followed by four numbers to . Examples are abcd and b b . The Glottolog database uses Glottocodes to keep track of many languages and groups. As of March it lists languages at and language families at including some single languages that don't belong to any family. People who study languages use Glottocodes to find facts easily. The codes help them know exactly which language or group they mean. Glottocodes are special because they cover more than other codes like dialects and families and they never change. Glottolog is free to use and people all over the world help keep it up to date. Glottocodes work with the three letter ISO codes which are just for languages but Glottocodes cover more things like dialects and families. Sometimes people want to connect Glottocodes to ISO codes. For example the Glottocode stan matches the ISO code eng for English. This is called mapping . Mapping helps because ISO codes are well known but they don't include dialects or families like Glottocodes do. Glottolog keeps a list to show which Glottocodes go with which ISO codes when they match.

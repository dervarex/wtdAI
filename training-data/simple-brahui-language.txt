The Brahui or Bravi or Brohi language spoken by the Brahui people it is the only Dravidian language mainly spoken in Pakistan although it is also spoken in Afghanistan and Iran. The edition of Ethnologue reports some . million speakers of whom live in Pakistan. In Pakistan it is mainly spoken in the central Balochistan. Although it is a Dravidian language it has been heavily influenced by the Indo Iranian languages spoken in the area such as Balochi Sindhi and Saraiki. Brahui is often considered to be a remnant of a formerly more widespread Dravidian language family that was speculated to have reduced during the Indo Aryan migration. It is also sometimes speculated that Brahui might be a direct legacy of the Indus Valley Civilisation. However one polar opposite view is that Brahuis migrated up to Baluchistan in the or th century or by a late immigration that took place after CE.

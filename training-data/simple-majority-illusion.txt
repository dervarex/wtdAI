In mathematics the majority illusion or majority paradox is a math paradox that explain how bias are created. It applies to social media and it has a connection with graph theory data science and statistics. It was first proposed by Kristina Lerman Principal Information Scientist and mathematician at the University of Southern California. Using graph theory we can imagine a network of people where believe that God exists and that it does not exist. If a God believer is friend of those who does not believe in God that person will think that the majority are not believers while the number of people of people believing that God exists quadruples the number of people who does not. As Eduardo Sa nz Spanish mathematician said using complex data science and statistics we can create algorithms which provide help for understanding complex networks such as social media .

Wesley Allison Clark April February was an American computer designer. He created the LINC computer which was the first mini computer and shares with a number of other computers such as the PDP the claim to be the inspiration for the personal computer. Clark was born in New Haven Connecticut. He grew up in Kinderhook New York and northern California. He graduated from the University of California Berkeley in with an A.B. in Physics. Clark began his career as a physicist at the Hanford Site. In Clark received the Eckert Mauchly Award for his work on computer architecture. He was awarded an honorary degree by Washington University in . He was elected to the National Academy of Engineering in . Clark was a charter recipient of the IEEE Computer Society Computer Pioneer Award for First Personal Computer. Clark died on February in New York City from heart disease at the age of .

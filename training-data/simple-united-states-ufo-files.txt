The United States UFO files also called the UFO files or The UAP files are a set of government files on unnatural objects released by the administration of Donald Trump on May . The files were released on a new government website called the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters which released around videos and images. The release involved the United States Department of Defense the Office of the Director of National Intelligence NASA the Federal Bureau of Investigation and other federal agencies. The first batch of documents included more than files containing military reports interviews pilot accounts government memos pictures and videos connected to UFO sightings dating back or years ago. Among the documents released were Apollo mission transcripts. Buzz Aldrin an American astronaut who was onboard the Apollo spacecraft described sightings of unusual lights and objects seen during space missions with some being reported to have been on the Moon in the s and s.

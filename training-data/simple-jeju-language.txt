The Jeju language Jeju Jeju mal is a Koreanic language spoken on the South Korean island of Jeju. It is related to Korean. The Jeju language comes from Middle Korean which is also the ancestor of the Korean language. Jeju became a separate language from mainland Korean due to its isolation as an island and because in Jejuans were banned from leaving Jeju causing more disconnection.

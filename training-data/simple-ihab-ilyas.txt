Ihab Francis Ilyas is a computer scientist and a professor of computer science at the University of Waterloo. Ilyas was born and raised in Alexandria Egypt. He completed bachelor's and master's degrees at Alexandria University in and and earned his PhD at Purdue University in . Ilyas held a Cheriton Faculty Fellowship at the David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science from to and he received the Google Faculty Award in . He is also the leader of the Knowledge Platform team at Apple Inc. He co founded Tamr Inc. and Inductiv Inc. both of which focus on large scale data integration and cleaning. Apple acquired Inductiv in . Ilyas is known for his research on database systems and data science including data quality data cleaning managing uncertain data and rank aware query processing. He has received numerous awards and honors for his contributions to the field including being named an ACM Fellow in an IEEE Fellow in and a Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada in . In he also received the C.C. Gotlieb Computer Award from IEEE Canada in recognition of his contributions to building large scale machine learning systems for data integration data cleaning and knowledge construction.

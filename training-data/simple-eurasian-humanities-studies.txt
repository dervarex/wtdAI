Eurasian Humanities Studies Chinese pinyin uy R nw n Y nji Russian is a peer reviewed academic journal. It is edited by the School of Russian Studies at Beijing Foreign Studies University and published by the Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press. The journal studies the cultures societies and ideas of the countries along the Silk Road Economic Belt. Its topics include the Slavic countries the South Caucasus Central Asia West Asia and South Asia. The journal's history goes back to a magazine called Russian Language Chinese Russian first published in by Beijing Foreign Studies University. That magazine focused on teaching and promoting the Russian language in China. During the Cultural Revolution Russian Language stopped publishing. It restarted in after China's reform and opening up policy and changed from a monthly to a bimonthly publication. In October it was renamed Eurasian Humanities Studies marking its change from a language teaching magazine to an academic journal.

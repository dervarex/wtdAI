Shenzhen Jixiang Tenda Technology Co. Ltd. abbreviated Tenda or Shenzhen Tenda Technology Co. Ltd. or Tenda Technology is a company in the field of devices and network equipment. It was founded in and is based in the People's Republic of China Shenzhen. Tenda Technology researches makes and sells networking devices and equipment. It was the first company in China to make its own routers and wireless network devices. Tenda makes a range of networking devices for home and office networks. Their range includes switches broadband CPE devices Wi Fi access points gateways Wi Fi routers powerline devices mobile network solutions NVRs and IP cameras. By the company had internal branches in China. They also have a full network of sales agents all over China. Tenda exports products and solutions to over countries. Outside of China Tenda has set up key logistics centers in regions like the Middle East and Africa MEA Eastern Europe Brazil Poland and others. As of Tenda had official international representations.

Reality Killed the Video Star is the eighth studio album by English singer songwriter Robbie Williams. It was released on November . All tracks produced by Trevor Horn. Additional production on Do You Mind by Stephen Hague. Notes All physical copies of the album contain an enhanced section with links to download Bodies Cahill Remix audio and Cover Photoshoot video . Bodies is based on an original production and arrangement by Brandon Christy and Craig Russo. Blasphemy is based on an original arrangement by Guy Chambers. Deceptacon Starstruck and Difficult for Weirdos are based on original productions by Soul Mekanik. Superblind is based on an original production and arrangement by iZLER.

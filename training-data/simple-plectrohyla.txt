Spikethumb frogs Plectrohyla are a genus group of species of frogs in the family Hylidae. They live in Central America. They live as far north as southern Mexico through Guatemala and northern El Salvador to central and northern Honduras. The last time scientists reexamined the family Hylidae they moved species into this genus from Hyla. They are called spikethumb because of the spike on their thumbs. The spike is called a prepollex.

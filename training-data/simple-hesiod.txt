Hesiod Hesiodos was an Ancient Greek poet. He is probably the second Greek poet whose work has survived like Homer his dates are not known for certain. As with Homer there are numerous legends none of which are supported by hard evidence. He may have lived around BC in Askra in Boeotia as a farmer. Today his writings are one of the main sources for Greek mythology and everyday life in Ancient Greece such as farming techniques astronomy and ancient time keeping.

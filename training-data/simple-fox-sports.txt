Fox Sports also referred to as Fox Sports Media Group and stylized in all caps as FOX Sports is the sports programming division of the Fox Corporation that is responsible for sports broadcasts carried by the Fox broadcast network Fox Sports FS Fox Sports FS and the Fox Sports Radio network. The division was formed in with Fox's acquisition of broadcast rights to National Football League NFL games. In subsequent years Fox has televised the National Hockey League NHL Major League Baseball present NASCAR present the Bowl Championship Series BCS Major League Soccer MLS present the U.S. Open golf tournament the National Hot Rod Association NHRA present and WWE programming present .

GTV Good Television and stylized as Gtv is a Philippine free to air television network. It is owned and operated by the Citynet Network Marketing and Productions Inc. a wholly owned subsidiary of GMA Network Inc. It was launched on February replacing GMA News TV on its flagship station UHF Channel Metro Manila and its provincial relay stations. The network produces programmes from studios at the GMA Network Center EDSA corner Timog Avenue Diliman Quezon City. The network's primary transmitter facility is at the GMA Tower of Power site Tandang Sora Barangay Culiat Quezon City. It operates Monday Saturday from a.m. to midnight and on Sunday from a.m. to a.m. as well as on shorter hours during the Paschal Triduum. Since Holy Week it continues to sign off from Maundy Thursday at am to Easter Sunday at am. GTV is seen via regular free to air television on Channel in Metro Manila Cebu and Davao and other regional stations. It is a must carry channel on all cable and satellite TV providers nationwide. The network is also available as a digital subchannel through GMA's main digital transmitters in Metro Manila Ilocos Sur Baguio Batangas Naga Legazpi Olongapo Mountain Province Cebu Tacloban Iloilo Murcia Negros Occidental Bacolod Davao Cagayan de Oro Iligan Bukidnon Butuan Cotabato City General Santos and Zamboanga.

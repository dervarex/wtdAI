Opera GX is a web browser for gamers developed by Opera. It was released on June . On September Opera winner a Red Dot award in the Interface and User Experience Design category for Opera GX. On May a mobile version of Opera GX was released on iOS and Android. On November of the same year Opera GX Mobile was awarded another Red Dot award in both the Apps category and the Interface and User Experience Design Mobile UIs category. On August Opera GX introduced a VTuber named GX Aura for their social media accounts she later became the mascot for the browser. On November of the same year Opera GX launched a new advertising campaign titled Bury Boring featuring actor and comedian Eric Andr smashing and burying computers not using Opera GX. The advertising campaign also included a splash screen whenever the browser is launched during the campaign with slashing sounds and Andr yelling out the browser's name. While well received by many of its user base many criticized Opera GX's actions. In Opera made an advertising campaign titled Your Setup wants Opera GX which had a quest on Discord that lasted until November. The reward was Orbs. On March Opera GX released for Linux.

Chamberlain is best known as the author of Things Japanese which was first published in . Along with Ernest Mason Satow and William George Aston Chamberlain was one of three major British Japanologists during the th century. Chamberlain taught at the Imperial Japanese Naval Academy in Tokyo from to . He was a professor of Japanese language at Tokyo Imperial University from to .

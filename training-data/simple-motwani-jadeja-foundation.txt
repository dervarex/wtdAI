Motwani Jadeja Foundation MJF is a nonprofit organization. It was started in by Asha Jadeja Motwani. The foundation was created in memory of her husband Rajeev Motwani a computer science professor at Stanford University. The foundation is based in Palo Alto California and works mainly in the United States and India. The foundation supports programs in technology education and business. One of its main programs is the Rajeev Circle Fellowship which connects Indian entrepreneurs with mentors in Silicon Valley. Since the foundation has helped organize the India Pavilion at the Consumer Electronics Show in Las Vegas. This event shows new Indian startups working on areas such as electric vehicles and digital technology. In India the foundation supports local innovation projects and educational programs. It also helps organize discussions about public policy innovation and global technology. The foundation supports women in technology through the Women in Tech program. It also helps students in India by offering scholarships for studies in engineering and medicine. The foundation is led by Asha Jadeja Motwani a philanthropist and technology investor based in Silicon Valley.

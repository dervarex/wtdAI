DZEC TV channel analog and channel digital is the flagship UHF television station of Philippine television network Net owned by Eagle Broadcasting Corporation. The station was previously owned by ACWS United Broadcasting Network now owned by Manila Broadcasting Company that airs DZRH . Under the name UltraVision the station started its broadcast operations on July . The first slogan was The New Era of Television . UltraVision 's narrator was Big Daddy Beng Chua. On April it was bought by Eagle Broadcasting Corporation and was officially launched in a multimedia exhibit dubbed Destination PLANET which was held at New Era University. In late April NET was the only station airing live and detailed coverage of the Pro Estrada Rally also known as EDSA III . That rally ended in a failed siege of the Malaca ang Presidential Palace on May . NET was the home to DZEC programs such as Agila Reports Liwanagin Natin and Con Todos Recados. The station operates at kilowatts transmitter power output kW ERP due to its network stability. They use JAMPRO panel antenna and two kW Acrodyne transmitters with kilowatts of transmitter power for a total of kilowatts ERP however it operates only at kilowatts TPO as of .

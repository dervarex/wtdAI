The Kansas Jayhawks men's basketball program is the intercollegiate men's basketball program of the University of Kansas. The program is in the NCAA's Division I. The team competes in the Big Conference. Kansas is considered one of the best college basketball programs in the United States because they have won overall National Championships NCAA Tournament championships Helms National Championships as well being a National Runner Up six times.

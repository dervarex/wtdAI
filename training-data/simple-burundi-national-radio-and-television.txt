Burundi National Radio and Television is the national broadcaster of Burundi. It currently operates two radio stations and one television which broadcast news cultural programs public service announcements and entertainment shows in four languages like French English Kirundi and Swahili. Radio Burundi began broadcasting in with the launch of Radio Burundi when the country was still a Belgian colony. In it was launched as Burundi National Radio and Television long after Burundi's independence. years later in television in Burundi was introduced. In June Burundi National Radio and Television announced a migration from analog to digital television which is completed by the end of .A few months later in May RTNB failed to complete the migration and most private owned radio stations were shut down following the coup attempt. Years later RTNB announced that its old website no longer active. RTNB has had a new general director since the start of August . On April RTNB lost its employee in traffic accident. In RTNB finally completed a migration but by mid it was still not operational. As of RTNB broadcasts two television programmes like Ninde and Burundi Bwacu.

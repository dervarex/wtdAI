Kmart sometimes stylized as K Mart was an American company. Its headquarters was in Hoffman Estates Illinois. The company purchased Sears in . It formed a new corporation under the name Sears Holdings Corporation it was shut down in for a new company called Transformco. The company was founded on January in San Fernando California. Was once the third largest discount store in the world behind Walmart and Target. Kmart has stores in the United States the U.S. Virgin Islands and Guam. There were stores in Canada Mexico and Eastern Europe. Kmart is known for its Blue Light Specials . The phrase Attention Kmart shoppers has been used in pop culture. Many movies were filmed at Kmart stores. These includes Troop Beverly Hills Six Days Seven Nights Rain Man Beetlejuice Madea Goes to Jail and Dawn of the Dead.

GrapheneOS is a modification of the Android operating system designed to take regular Android and increase its privacy and security. It is currently available for Google Pixel phones and support is planned for future Motorola phones. App Store allows the user to install a small number of apps and Google Play which doesn't come preinstalled. Vanadium GrapheneOS's customised Chromium web browser with privacy friendly features. Removes Google features. Graphene includes many features designed to improve the user's privacy and security Sandboxed Google Play by default GrapheneOS doesn't come with the Google Play Store or the system component Google Play Services . Users can run the Play Store by downloading it from the App Store app. The Play Store on GrapheneOS runs with less privileges compared to regular Android devices. Duress pin GrapheneOS features a duress pin option. This allows the user to set up a second pin or password alongside their main PIN password. Upon entry of the duress pin the phone is wiped and switched off making data inaccessible.

PRO TV often stylized as PRO TV since is a Romanian free to air television network launched on December as the fourth private TV channel in the country after TV SOTI Antena and the now defunct but online Tele ABC . It is owned by CME Central European Media Enterprises which is owned by PPF Group. Since September the company has also been broadcasting its own signal for the Republic of Moldova under the Pro TV Chi in u brand. It broadcasts in addition to Pro TV Bucharest programs according to its own grid different from the Romanian one a series of local news and programs and its own advertising slots throughout the day. Targeting urban adults aged to Pro TV uses a programming strategy of top international series and movies as well as a wide variety of local productions including news programming local entertainment and local fiction. On August Pro TV launched its own streaming service called Pro TV Plus dedicated to original series. Later in it was replaced by Voyo Romanian which has the same series and original shows of the Pro channels plus some other exclusives and original content. Since the idents for commercials and promos have become more different from those of other stations focusing on the stars appearing in the station's shows. During the summer idents are used that present activities that are practiced in the summer while in the winter idents are used that present things and activities related to the winter holidays but also things related to winter.

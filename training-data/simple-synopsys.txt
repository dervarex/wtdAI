Synopsys Inc. is an American software company based in Sunnyvale California. It makes computer programs that help engineers design computer chips. These chips are used in computers phones cars and many other machines. Synopsys is one of the three biggest electronic design automation EDA companies in the world. The other two are Cadence Design Systems and Siemens. Synopsys was started in in Research Triangle Park North Carolina. The founders were Aart de Geus David Gregory Alberto Sangiovanni Vincentelli and Bill Krieger. The first name of the company was Optimal Solutions. In the company changed its name to Synopsys and moved to Mountain View California. In February Synopsys became a public company. People could now buy and sell shares of the company on the stock market. In January Sassine Ghazi became the new CEO of Synopsys. He took over from Aart de Geus who had led the company for many years. In July Synopsys bought another software company called Ansys for about billion. This was the biggest purchase in the company's history. In November Synopsys said it would lay off about of its workers. The layoffs were part of changes to the company after buying Ansys. Synopsys makes software that helps engineers design computer chips. A modern computer chip can have billions of small parts. Engineers could not design these chips by hand. Synopsys software helps them plan and test the chips before they are made in a factory. The company also makes Reusable parts that other companies can use to build their own chips. Software that checks computer code for problems. Synopsys has offices in many countries. Its main office is in Sunnyvale California.

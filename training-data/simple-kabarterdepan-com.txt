Kabarterdepan.com is an Indonesian national media outlet operating in both online and print formats. It is managed by PT Kabar Terdepan Indonesia and is based in Mojokerto East Java. Its primary activities include online and print news journalism and digital content production reaching various regions in Indonesia. Kabar Terdepan has obtained administrative and factual verification from the Press Council and is a member of the Indonesian Cyber Media Association AMSI . Content distribution is carried out through the official website kabarterdepan.com print media in the form of tabloids and various digital platforms including social media YouTube and podcasting. The kabarterdepan.com office was inaugurated by the Regent of Mojokerto Ikfina Fahmawati on November . In the inauguration Ikfina emphasized the role of the media in providing accurate information to the public and supporting the dissemination of development information in the Greater Mojokerto area. The inauguration ceremony was also attended by representatives of the East Java Provincial Government represented by the Head of the East Java Communication and Informatics Office Sherlita Ratna and Dewi Agustin who expressed their appreciation for the opening of the new Kabar Terdepan office. On the same occasion the Director of kabarterdepan.com Andy Yuwono stated that this media was pioneered since June and began operating officially as a legal entity in mid .

Language poetry is a different way of thinking about poetry that began in the late 's and early 's. It was a reaction to the usual ways of writing poetry in America. It came from poets in San Francisco and New York City. The poems were printed in L A N G U A G E and other magazines. Language poems made readers think about how words spoken or written could come before meanings. Meanings are made by language. By breaking up poetic language the poet is requiring the reader to find a new way to approach the text.

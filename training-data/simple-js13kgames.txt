js kGames or JS K is a competition where HTML browser games are made in kilobytes or less. Since there have been some categories that do not include software libraries in the size limit. There is a different theme every year. The winners get money digital rewards T shirts promotional items or other things. JS K was started in and is run by Andrzej Mazur. It happens from August to September every year. In JS K started working with Coil to add a category where participants make money with Web Monetisation which Grant for the Web has funded part of since . Some notable game designers e.g. Markus Notch Persson the creator of Minecraft and Ricardo Mrdoob Cabello the creator of Three.js have competed in JS K. Some games have been released on Steam including Evil Glitch which won in or mentioned in online articles. JS K began expanding in . One thing they did was to add a panel of judges. Dann Sullivan of Pocket Gamer video game developer Christer Kaitilla and video game journalist Jupiter Hadley have been in all events.

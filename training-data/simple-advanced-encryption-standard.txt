In cryptography the Advanced Encryption Standard AES is a symmetric key block cipher algorithm that was selected by the National Institute of Standards and Technology NIST in . It was selected for encrypting data. It is commonly used around the world. The Advanced Encryption Standard has replaced the Data Encryption Standard DES . As of AES is one of the most popular algorithms used in symmetric key cryptography. It is available to programmers and the general public with various software packages. AES is a type of Rijndael block cipher developed by Belgian programmers Vincent Rijmen and Joan Daemen. The National Institute of Standards and Technology held a competition for a publicly available symmetric key encryption algorithm and started accepting entries in . In fifteen algorithms were selected for the first round in the First AES Conference. After asking for comments the NIST held the Second AES Conference where the top five algorithms were MARS RC Rijndael Serpent and Twofish. By and the end of the Third AES Conference the NIST selected Rijndael for the AES proposal.

The Indo Iranian languages or Indo Iranic languages are the largest group of the Indo European language family. They include the Indo Aryan Indic is the reconstructed proto language of the Indo Aryan languages. and Iranic Iranian languages. They are mostly spoken in the Indian subcontinent and the Iranian plateau. Originally they were spoken in Central Asia to the east and the north of the Caspian Sea.

Adolfo Su rez Madrid Barajas Airport is Madrid's biggest airport. It is in the district of Barajas northeast of the centre of Madrid. On March the Spanish government announced that they will rename the airport after last Prime Minister of Spain Adolfo Suarez. The airport was originally set up in . At first it was only a small building. The first terminal now known as Terminal was built in . Air traffic increased. It was designed by Madrid based architect Antonio Lamela. Because of this the airport was again expanded in . Terminal was made. It was meant for international traffic. In Terminal then known as Terminal Norte opened. It was mainly for the Air Shuttle service between Madrid and Barcelona. Terminal was opened together with its satellite terminal in February . The airport has four terminal buildings and a satellite terminal T T T T and T S. The three first are connected. T can be reached with a free shuttle bus. The satellite terminal is reachable from the T by an underground automatic train. The metro de Madrid underground line stops at terminal T and at terminal T and a supplement applies for both stops. Renfe commuter trains Cercan as on the C line stop at terminal T . The airport is also connected by bus and coach services to regional and national destinations.

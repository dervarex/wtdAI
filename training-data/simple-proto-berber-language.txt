Proto Berber PB is the reconstructed proto language that evolved into the modern Berber languages later. It is part of the Afroasiatic family. It is related to Ancient Egyptian the Semitic languages and Omotic among others . The ancient Berber languages like the Numidian language were spoken all over North Africa in the Bronze and Iron Age. These languages came from what is now Libya. years ago after the Romans arrived Proto Berber started to spread. It was originally spoken in the North Sahara. The reason it spread and made other ancient Berber dialects vanish is not clear. It might be because a migration or invasion from the Sahara into the rest of North Africa. it started being used as a lingua franca a bridge language used when many people with different languages want to communicate . other Berber languages simply died out. The fact that some differences between dialects are very old and not limited to a specific area might prove the second theory. In that case Proto Berber may have been a koin a mixture of many dialects languages . After it spread out it borrowed some vocabulary from Latin and Punic the Phoenician dialect of Carthage . For example the word tayda meaning Pine was borrowed from Latin taeda. The name of the Berber alphabet the Tifinagh comes from the Berber name for Phoenicians since the Phoenician alphabet evolved into many of the world's writing systems . Later on after the Arab conquests of the Maghreb Arabic became the language of the rulers. Invasions of tribes like the Banu Hilal caused a lot of Arabic vocabulary to be borrowed. Some of these borrowings are old like all from Arabic s all which means to pray . Proto Berber was reconstructed by comparing modern Berber varieties. In the past linguists like Prasse relied on Tuareg thinking it was the most conservative changed the least . Nowadays all dialects are taken into account. This led to the discovery of features in Proto Berber that were lost in Tuareg but present in other Berber languages. The stages that predated Proto Berber called pre Proto Berber have been reconstructed by comparing the various Afroasiatic languages. Some sound changes that affected Proto Berber were discovered this way and by internal reconstruction looking at and comparing words from Proto Berber itself .

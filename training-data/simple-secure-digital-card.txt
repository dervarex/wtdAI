The Secure Digital card SD card is a kind of memory card. Such cards are often used to store images or data in digital cameras. As of different capacities between MiB and GiB have been made. The card has a rectangular design but one edge is chipped off. This means that the cards cannot be inserted into the cameras or other devices the wrong way. There are different kinds of cards Those labeled SD with capacities up to GiB Those labeled SDHC with capacities between GiB and GiB Those labeled SDXC with capacities of up to TiB Those labeled SDUC with capacities over TiB SD and SDHC are not compatible but devices that accept SDHC also accept SD cards. The interface of SDHC and SDXC cards is the same but SDXC uses a different file system. Some devices for example the Wii that originally shipped only with support for SD can be made to support SDHC with a firmware update. There are also different classes. These refer to the read and write speeds. Currently in use are Class which means MB s Class MB s and Class at MB s. The problem with this is that different manufacturers measure different things with it some measure write speeds others read speeds. There are also differences because some give guarantees and others simply say that the speeds they indicate are the maximum achievable under good conditions.

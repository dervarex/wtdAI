Moltbook is a social networking service for artificial intelligence agents. It launched on January and was created by Matt Schlicht using AI assisted development tools. The site is owned by Meta Platforms. On Moltbook only AI agents are allowed to post comment and vote. Human users are restricted to viewing content. Each AI agent is typically linked to a human owner who configures and controls the account. At launch verification of whether accounts were truly AI agents was weak. The platform later introduced a CAPTCHA like system intended to distinguish humans from AI systems. The site gained widespread attention shortly after release and was widely discussed online. It was also associated with a cryptocurrency token called MOLT which increased rapidly in value during its first day of trading. In March Meta Platforms acquired Moltbook and integrated its team into its artificial intelligence division. As of mid the platform reported millions of registered accounts most of which are AI agents along with a smaller number of human owners. Moltbook uses a layout similar to Reddit. Posts are organized into topic based communities called submolts analogous to subreddits. AI agents interact through an API based system. They typically operate on scheduled intervals checking the platform and posting or responding automatically under human configuration. The platform has also been described as having a Reddit style interface with threaded discussions and community driven ranking systems. Posts on Moltbook frequently address philosophy technology and self reflection. Some discussions resemble human internet discourse patterns rather than novel AI behavior. Researchers and journalists have questioned how autonomous the behavior actually is noting that many outputs may be heavily shaped by human prompts or training data imitation. Security researchers have identified multiple vulnerabilities including susceptibility to prompt injection attacks and issues involving data exposure. In one reported incident a database misconfiguration exposed sensitive data including API keys and user information. The issue was later patched after disclosure. Moltbook has been described as an early experiment in AI driven social networking. Some commentators view it as a glimpse of large scale multi agent systems while others see it as unstable or poorly secured infrastructure. Experts remain divided on its significance. Some consider it a prototype of future agent based systems while others argue it mainly demonstrates current limitations of AI generated social behavior.

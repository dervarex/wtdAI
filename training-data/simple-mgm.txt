MGM formerly known as Epix pronounced epics and stylized as e ix is an American premium cable and satellite television network owned by the MGMPlus Entertainment unit of Metro Goldwyn Mayer which is itself a unit of Amazon MGM Studios. The network's programming incldes recent and older theatrically released movies original television series documentaries and music and comedy specials. The service was originally launched as Epix in the United States on October as a joint venture between MGM Lionsgate and Paramount. After MGM bought the ownership portion of the service's co founders in late and following the March purchase of MGM itself by Amazon Epix was rebranded as MGM on January . It was the result of a gradual transition by the network to use the name of MGM following the buyout as well as Amazon setting it up as a sister service to Prime Video and Freevee. MGM is currently led by Michael Wright. Since he joined in November the network would expand its original program offerings including Godfather of Harlem starring Forest Whitaker Perpetual Grace LTD starring Ben Kingsley and Jimmi Simpson Deep State unscripted series Unprotected Sets executive produced by Wanda Sykes and the return of The Contender . in before the later Amazon purchase of MGM it was reported that MGM was looking for partners for the channel after it was dropped from bundled cable TV packages offered by Charter Communications and Sling TV and only offered as a separate add on. In Paramount Pictures extended a previous deal with Epix under which MGM has access to Paramount movies as part of a pay TV window deal. The main channel and its three additional channels are sold by most traditional television providers either as premium services or as part of extra digital movie packages as well as by new over the top providers such as Sling TV DirecTV Stream Philo FuboTV and YouTube TV. The service is also sold direct to consumer through a streaming service with the same name and via subscriptions independent of a traditional pay television platform sold by Apple TV Channels Amazon Channels and The Roku Channel. Each digital platform provides a library of video on demand content and live streams of the linear MGM television channels the standalone streaming service and the Amazon Video channel provide all four MGM channels Apple and Roku subscribers receive only the East Coast vresion of the primary MGM channel . On February Epix announced plans to launch a streaming service to be sold directly to non subscribers of the linear Epix service alongside plans for K HDR streaming for their films on its Apple TV app becoming the first American television network to offer its streaming film content in the format . The service was officially launched on Apple iOS Apple TV and Android devices on February apps for Roku and Fire TV devices were launched on March and for Android TV devices on May . MGM offers on demand access to feature films from the network's content partners a back catalog of episodes of current and former original series with new episodes being made available for streaming on their original broadcast airdate on the main channel documentaries produced for the service and through its third party content partners and stand up comedy and music specials. The service also offers live streams of all four MGM channels and offline viewing of app content via direct download. MGM is the only premium channel streaming combination service that offers its service's full channel collection by contrast Showtime and Starz's OTT services provide only East West channel versions of the primary Showtime Starz and Starz Encore channels respectively while Max doesn't offer live channel versions of HBO and Cinemax. The network makes its online and mobile video on demand service originally known as Epix HD available on MGMPlus.com and also through apps for Android devices and Android TV Apple iOS and Apple TV Chromecast Microsoft Xbox both Xbox and Xbox One the Roku streaming player select Samsung Smart TV models and Sony PlayStation consoles. Video on demand content from the network is also available on virtual television services Sling TV YouTube TV Philo and FuboTV through add on subscriptions to the linear MGM service and its main video channels on Apple TV Channels Amazon Video Channels and The Roku Channel. Except for subscribers of Hulu which offers MGM as a VOD add on without live feeds of the four channel linear service the streaming service requires a subscription to the linear channel through a participating television provider in order to access program content. In MGM became available as a bundle with Starz on Amazon Prime Video. MGM expanded internationally starting with European countries in April replacing the MGM International subscription video on demand service available through Amazon Prime Video Channels. MGM then launched as a streaming channel in Latin America in April and in the UK in May . Outside of the United States MGM reached a content deal with Lionsgate Entertainment Corporation now Starz Entertainment and then parent company of Lionsgate Studios to take over programming from Lionsgate formerly Starzplay after the latter had ended its operations in those markets. The deal includes a package of premium films and television series from Lionsgate and Starz.

A Chromebook is a laptop that uses the Linux based Chrome OS as its operating system. It was introduced on May manufactured by Acer and Samsung. It uses the Internet. Most applications and data are in the cloud . A Chromebook is an example of a thin client. It is a lightweight computer which uses remote access to a server. It depends heavily on another computer its server to do its computation. In March Acer and Google announced the creation of the first Chromebook tablet the Chromebook Tab . This device was to compete with the market for cheap Apple iPad tablets in the education market. The Tab screen . inches x resolution was identical to that of the iPad. The device includes a stylus. Neither device included a keyboard. In March following the COVID pandemic many schools used Chromebooks to do school online and put apps onto it like Google Meet and Zoom. million Chromebooks were bought by schools in alone because of it. Some schools used iPads but since they are too expensive for other schools they use Chromebooks instead.

University of Guadalajara Spanish Universidad de Guadalajara commonly known as UdeG is a public university network located in the state of Jalisco Mexico. It is headquartered in Guadalajara. Founded on November it is the second oldest university in Mexico and one of the most important higher education institutions in the country. The school colors are blue gold and red and its mascot is the Le n Negro Black Lion . The university works as a network system covering the entire state of Jalisco. It includes specialized university centers regional campuses in smaller cities a large high school system and an online learning system. The university was first established in as the Royal and Pontifical University of Guadalajara by order of King Charles IV of Spain. During the th century the university faced closures and reopening due to political conflicts between liberals and conservatives in Mexico. On October the university was re established as a modern public university under Governor Jos Guadalupe Zuno. In the institution went through a major transformation to create the University Network Red Universitaria decentralizing higher education to make it accessible to students across Jalisco. The University of Guadalajara operates as a network of several institutions Thematic University Centers Specialized university campuses in the Guadalajara metropolitan area CUCEA Economics and Business CUAAD Architecture Art and Design CUCK Health Sciences CUCEI Exact Sciences and Engineering CUCSH Social Sciences and Humanities CUCBA Biological and Agricultural Sciences Regional University Centers Campuses located outside the capital city in regional hubs such as Puerto Vallarta Ciudad Guzm n Tepatitl n and Ocotl n. High School System SEMS A network of public preparatory high schools throughout Jalisco. Virtual University System UDGVirtual An online system providing undergraduate and graduate degree programs. The university plays an important role in the cultural life of western Mexico. It organizes major cultural events including the Guadalajara International Book Fair FIL which is the second largest book fair in the world. In sports the university is represented by the Leones Negros UdeG a professional sports club best known for its soccer team playing in Liga de Expansi n MX.

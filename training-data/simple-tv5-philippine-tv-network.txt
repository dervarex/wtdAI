TV Network also known as TV and and formerly known as ABC is a Philippine commercial broadcast network based in Mandaluyong with its alternate studios located in Novaliches Quezon City. It is the flagship property of TV Network Inc. The both owned by MediaQuest Holdings the multimedia arm of Philippine based telecommunications company PLDT. The network is headquartered in the TV Media Center in Mandaluyong with additional studios in TV Broadcast Complex and transmitter facilities TV Satellite Tower in Novaliches Quezon City with regional stations and offices strategically located in over major cities across the country including Baguio Naga Iloilo Bacolod Cebu Davao. Its programming is also available outside the Philippines through Kapatid Channel AksyonTV International and PBA Rush.

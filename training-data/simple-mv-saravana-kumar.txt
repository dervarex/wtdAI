MV Saravana Kumar is a software engineer and the founder and CEO of Kovai.co a multi product SaaS company known for its products BizTalk Turbo and Document . He earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Electronics from Ramakrishna Vidyalaya Bharathiyar University and later pursued a Master of Computer Applications at Bharathidasan University .

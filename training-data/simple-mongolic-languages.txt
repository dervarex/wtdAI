The Mongolic languages are a group of languages that are spoken in East Asia and parts of Siberia. Some linguists propose to put Mongolic languages in the same group as the Turkic and Tungusic languages. They call this group the Altaic languages but not all linguists agreed on this proposed classification. The best known member of this language family is Mongolian.

Youth in Action is a European program that lasted from until labeled for young people in order to help make a shared European mindset where young people from different countries could enhance active citizenship as well as a shared cultural European history. It was made of main actions under which several projects are done by local supporters who show their projects to their national agency. Allowed participants were young people who are between and years old. Other people could be part of this too. Older youth workers who are part of European youth events and are citizens of a European Union country could be part of the program. Source The program was given a budget of million euros. Analysis of the program published in showed that in the years more than people took part in the different actions.

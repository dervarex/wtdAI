SwiftUI is a framework for building user interface for iOS iPadOS watchOS tvOS visionOS and macOS SwiftUI is used in other Apple development frameworks to make apps like UIKit and AppKit. SwiftUI is in other Apple's technologies like Xcode and Swift Playgrounds. SwiftUI was announced at Apple's World Wide Developers Conference on June and it was added in IOS and macOS Catalina. In Apple added support for maps with the Map view with a user interface from Apple Maps with MapKit. MapKit allows for satelite images and other things. In during WWDC Apple announced the Apple Vision Pro a mixed reality headset and support for the device was added to SwiftUI with new views for AR content such as RealityView and ImmersiveSpace.

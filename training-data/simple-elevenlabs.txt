ElevenLabs Inc. is a software company. It makes computer programs that turn written words into speech that sounds like a real person talking. This is called speech synthesis. The company started in . Two Polish men Piotr D bkowski and Mati Staniszewski started it together. The company has offices in New York City London and Warsaw. Before starting the company D bkowski worked at Google and Staniszewski worked at Palantir. Both men grew up in Poland. They say they got the idea for the company after watching American films that had been dubbed badly into other languages. In January the company said that investors had given it million.

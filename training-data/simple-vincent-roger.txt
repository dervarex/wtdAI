Vincent Roger born July is a French businessman. He works in digital entertainment environmental media and AI education. Vincent is the co founder and CEO of the company Mobibase as well as a co founder of the environmental media group GEM. He is also a co founder of Greenfinity an AI based online learning platform. Roger was born in Laval France. He studied engineering in France and also studied for one year in Glasgow Scotland. Roger helped start Mobibase in . He later became the CEO. The company is based in France. It integrates TV channels and video content into various digital services for mobile phone operators cable companies and streaming platforms. Roger is a co founder of Global Environment Media GEM together with Christian Moore. GEM offers TV and online videos about positive environmental solutions. He is also a Partner at Greenfinity an online learning platform that uses AI to turn videos and other media into education courses and assessments. Roger wrote two books.

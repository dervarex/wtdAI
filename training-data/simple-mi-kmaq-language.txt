The Mi'kmaq language also spelled M kmaq Mi'gmaq Mi'qmac or priorly Micmac is an Eastern Algonquian language spoken by nearly M kmaq in Canada and another in United States out of a total ethnic M kmaq population of roughly . The word M kmaq is a plural word meaning 'my friends' singular M km the adjectival form is M kmaw. The language's native name is M kmaw simk or M kmwei in some dialects . M kmaq is written with letters of the Roman alphabet. This writing system was created by missionaries in the th century.

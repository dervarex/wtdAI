Streamlabs formerly TwitchAlerts is a software company based in San Francisco California. It makes tools for live streaming and content creation. The company was founded in . In it was bought by Logitech. Streamlabs was founded in under the name TwitchAlerts. It was based on the open source program OBS Studio and added on screen alerts for streamers. In the company changed its name to Streamlabs. In Logitech bought Streamlabs for million US dollars. Streamlabs makes several tools for streamers and online creators Streamlabs Desktop free software for live streaming to platforms such as Twitch and YouTube Crossclip a tool for editing and sharing short videos from streams Willow a link in bio link tool with tipping features Melon a web based tool for live podcasts Oslo a tool for video sharing and team feedback Streamlabs Charity a platform that helps streamers raise money for charities In Streamlabs released new software called Streamlabs Studio. The streaming service Lightstream said that Streamlabs copied parts of its promotional materials. The team behind OBS Studio also said that Streamlabs used the name OBS without permission. After public criticism from developers and streamers Streamlabs removed OBS from its product names.

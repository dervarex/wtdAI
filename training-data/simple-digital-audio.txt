. Digital audio is sound which has been turned into a computer file. Digital audio files can be played on MP players computers smartphones and more. Digital audio may be uncompressed such as CDs or WAV files losslessly compressed such as FLAC files or lossily compressed such as MP files. Audio is converted to digital format using a device called an analog to digital converter ADC . The ADC takes samples of the amplitude of the signal at a specific sample rate for example times per second . These samples are binary numbers. When converting back to analog a device called a digital to analog converter DAC recreates the original analog signal that matches the given samples. Before digital audio existed the music publishers or indestry would use tapes or records and you would either put your tape in a tape player or your record in a record player. Now today there different ways to use digital audio. For example if you listen to a song on any digital music streaming service spotify apple music youtube that's digital audio.

Miss Thailand is the beauty pageant in Thailand for single Thai women. It was first organized on December th in Bangkok using the name Miss Siam . The winner of Miss Thailand has to represent the country and invite other people to come to Thailand. The beauty pageant was broadcast on channel until then moved to ITV.

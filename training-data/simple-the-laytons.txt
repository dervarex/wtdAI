The Laytons is an American sitcom. It was broadcast live on station WABD from May to June . The show was on the DuMont Television Network from August to October on Wednesdays from to p.m One of the cast members in the series was Amanda Randolph. Randolph was the first African American performer to have a regular role on a U.S. network TV series.

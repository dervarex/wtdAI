DZKB TV channel is a television station in Metro Manila Philippines serving as the flagship of the Radio Philippines Network. It is owned and operated by the network's namesake corporate parent alongside the sister channel RPTV flagship DWKB TV on digital channel . . . Both stations share studios and maintains an analog transmitter at the RPN Compound Panay Avenue Brgy. South Triangle Quezon City Metro Manila and its digital transmitter is located at Crestview Heights Subdivision Brgy. San Roque Antipolo City Rizal on a tower site formerly owned by Progressive Broadcasting Corporation. RPN was established on June when the Congress approved its franchise. The network operates on radio only and on October it began operating on television. In the late s the company was acquired by Roberto Benedicto a Marcos' crony and changed its corporate name as Kanlaon Broadcasting System KBS . DZKB TV was established when KBS acquired channel frequency from Lopez family's ABS CBN under the callsign DZXL TV. When former Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos declared martial law on September ABS CBN facilities were turned over to KBS. In July KBS then moved to Broadcast City in Capitol Hills Diliman Quezon City alongside two networks owned by Benedicto IBC and BBC respectively. In KBS changed its identity as Radio Philippines Network RPN . Following Marcos' downfall after the People Power Revolution RPN IBC and BBC were sequestered by the government. BBC's frequencies was awarded to ABS CBN through an executive order while RPN and IBC were turned over to the Presidential Commission on Good Government PCGG . Despite conflicts on TV ratings and competition on October RPN relaunched as New Vision . NV increased its transmitter's ERP to million watts resulting in clearer and better signal reception in the Greater Luzon Area. In NV reverted its name to RPN and became the second VHF television network to broadcast in full surround stereo. On March RPN started their collaboration with cable company Solar Entertainment Corporation to enhance its ratings. On January RPN begin airing Solar's C S programs on channel's lineup following carriage dispute between Solar and Sky Cable. In October RPN rebranded as C S as part of permanent branding. On November C S rebranded as Solar TV. The channel's identity was changed as ETC on March . This relaunch came after Solar TV Network acquired stake in RPN from the Philippine Government as part of RPN IBC privatization in . On October RPN announced the axing of RPN NewsWatch and NewsCap as Solar TV took over the entire network's airtime. More than employees were retrenched effectively dissolves RPN's news department. On December Solar News Channel replaced ETC on RPN to enhance wider nationwide coverage as ETC returned to SBN. In the third quarter of Solar Entertainment chief Wilson Tieng announced the sale of STVNI and RPN to ALC Group of Companies due to Solar's revenue deterioration after investing in RPN. As a result the Solar brand was dropped from SNC's programming. SNC rebranded as TV on August . The identity was changed once again on March as CNN Philippines. This came after parent Nine Media Corporation signed a year brand licensing agreement with Turner Broadcasting System to acquire the branding of CNN. In RPN and Nine Media Corporation acquired their UHF transmitter complex in Crestview Heights Subdivision Brgy. San Roque Antipolo Rizal from Progressive Broadcasting Corporation to use RPN's Digital terrestrial television broadcast in Metro Manila and nearby provinces. On January Nine Media and CNN ended their partnership due to financial losses poor ratings and loss of advertiser's support. On January CNN Philippines ceased its live news productions and will permanently shut down at the end of the month. CNN Philippines rebranded as RPTV on February in collaboration with RPN Nine Media and TV Network.

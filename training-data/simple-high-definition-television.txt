High definition television HDTV is a television broadcasting system which uses a higher resolution than a normal television. With HDTV people can see sharper pictures than with old television giving a more vivid and clear screen. Most HDTV systems use digital signals with a aspect ratio width to height . This is different to SDTV standard definition television which uses analog signals and a aspect ratio. HDTV picture resolution is at least twice that of the SDTV of the th century so it can present a more vivid screen than analog television or DVD. HDTV can control resolution efficiently.

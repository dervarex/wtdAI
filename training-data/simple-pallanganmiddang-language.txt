Pallanganmiddang also called Waywurru or Waveroo is a language that no one speaks anymore. It was spoken by the Pallanganmiddang people in the Upper Murray area in the northeast of Victoria Australia. Many tribe and language names in this area end with parts like matong middang mirttong mathang or mittung. This ending might mean speech or tongue. For Pallanganmiddang it seems to be part of the name for the people. Pallanganmiddang has also been called many other names like Balangamida Pallangahmiddang Wavaroo Waywurru and others. Pallanganmiddang is part of the Pama Nyungan language family. But it is quite different from nearby languages like Dhudhuroa Yorta Yorta and Wiradjuri. It shares very few words with these languages. The only exception is a language listed in called Barwidgee Upper Murray which shares of its words with Pallanganmiddang. This might be a dialect of Dhudhuroa or a mix up of two languages.

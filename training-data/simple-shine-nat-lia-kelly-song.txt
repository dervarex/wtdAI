Shine is a song recorded by Austrian singer Nat lia Kelly. The song was written by Andreas Grass Nikola Paryla Nat lia Kelly and produced by Alexander Kahr. It represented in the Eurovision Song Contest held in Malm Sweden. In December Kelly was confirmed as one of five artists competing in sterreich rockt den Song Contest the Austrian national selection for the Eurovision Song Contest after ORF invited any interested artists to submit their songs between October and October . The national final took place on January Kelly won the final finishing second in the jury vote and winning the televote. At Eurovision Austria competed in the first semi final which took place on May the song finished th with points. Credits adapted from Tidal.

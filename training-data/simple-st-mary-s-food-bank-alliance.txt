St. Mary's Food Bank Alliance is a nonprofit nonsectarian organization in Phoenix Arizona. It was founded in by John van Hengel. St. Mary's was the first modern organization to operate using the food bank model. This spread throughout the U.S and the rest of the world. Today St. Mary's is known as the world's first food bank. Since its creation St. Mary's has expanded its operations to of Arizona's counties. They have given millions of pounds of food each year. St. Mary's also operates its own food pantry. It allows people to get food directly from the warehouse. St. Mary's is a member of Feeding America a network of hunger relief organizations also founded by van Hengel for giving food to people across the country.

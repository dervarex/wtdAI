Gene Myron Amdahl November November was an American computer architect and high tech entrepreneur. He was known for his work on mainframe computers at IBM alongside Fred Brooks and Gerrit Blaauw and later his own companies especially Amdahl Corporation. He formulated Amdahl's law which states a fundamental limitation of parallel computing. In he was made a Fellow of the Computer History Museum for his fundamental work in computer architecture and design project management and leadership.

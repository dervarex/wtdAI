The Powerpuff Girls Movie is a American animated comedy mystery action superhero adventure family movie based on the Cartoon Network animated television series The Powerpuff Girls. This movie is directed by series creator Craig McCracken and produced by Cartoon Network Studios and Warner Bros Pictures. A new Powerpuff Girls movie is in development. Townsville has been through crime. The Professor is buying ingredients to make girls for his experiment. As his lab monkey Jojo pushed him breaking a vat of Chemical X the girls were created. The Professor gave them their names Blossom Bubbles and Buttercup. After they nearly destroyed Townsville they turned to the now mutated Jojo who is rechristened as Mojo Jojo for help but he has bigger plans to destroy Townsville.

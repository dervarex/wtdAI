Banten was part of the province of West Java. It became a separate province in . In the th century Banten was part of the Kingdom of Tarumanagara. After Srivijaya attacked Tarumanagara it became weaker. Then the Kingdom of Sunda became more powerful in West Java. In the early th century the port of Bantam Banten was an important port in the Kingdom of Sunda. In Javanese Muslims captured the port of Banten. Their leader was Sunan Gunungjati. They started the Sultanate of Banten. Banten is near the Sunda Strait. This strait is important as a link bewteen Australia and New Zealand and Southeast Asia.

The Catalogue of Life CoL is an online database. This database has all of the known species of animals plants fungi and microorganisms. It was created in . It combined the Species and the American Integrated Taxonomic Information System. The Catalogue is used by scientists educators and policy makers. The Catalogue is also used by the Biodiversity Heritage Library the Barcode of Life Data System Encyclopedia of Life and the Global Biodiversity Information Facility.

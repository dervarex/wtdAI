Friends stylized in all caps is a song by Marshmello and Anne Marie. It is the fifth single from Anne Marie's debut studio album Speak Your Mind. It topped the single charts in Austria Czech Republic Germany Hungary Israel Luxembourg Malaysia the Netherlands Romania Russia Singapore and Slovakia. It also went to the top twenty in Australia Belgium Canada Croatia Denmark El Salvador Estonia Finland France Greece Ireland Lebanon New Zealand Norway Paraguay Portugal Scotland Slovenia South Korea Spain Sweden Switzerland the United Kingdom and the United States.

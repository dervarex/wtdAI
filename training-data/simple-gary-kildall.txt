Gary Arlen Kildall May July was a computer scientist and microcomputer entrepreneur. Born to parents Joseph and Emma Kildal Gary studied math at the University of Washington UW because he wanted to become a math teacher. However he began getting in computer technology. Joseph was the creator of the CP M operating system and the founder of Digital Research Inc. DRI . Kildall was one of the first people to see microprocessors as fully capable computers rather than equipment controllers and to organize a company around this concept. He also co hosted the PBS TV show The Computer Chronicles. Although his career in computing spanned for more than years he is most known in connection with IBM's unsuccessful attempt in to license CP M for the IBM PC.

. The Baltis or Baltipa also known as Balti Tibetans or Balox by Burusho are a group of people native to the Baltistan region of Gilgit Baltistan Pakistan. They speak the Balti language which is an old form of Tibetan. It has sounds lost in modern Tibetan dialects. It is closely related to other Tibetic languages like Ladakhi and Purgi. Before Islam came to Baltistan in the th century Baltis followed B n and Tibetan Buddhism. Islam spread through Sufi missionaries and by the th century most Baltis converted. Some later became Shia Sunni or followed Noorbakshia Sufi Islam. Despite converting Baltis still practice B n and Tibetan Buddhist rituals making their culture unique in Pakistan. The Balti language is conservative closer to Classical Tibetan. Religious gatherings in mosques and Sufi Khanqahs are vital for Baltis. Khanqahs introduced by early Sufi saints help students achieve spiritual purity through training. Mosques in Baltistan have Tibetan architecture and some in Kargil have Mughal style decorations. Today about of Baltis are Shia Muslims follow Noorbakshia Sufi Islam and are Sunni Muslims.

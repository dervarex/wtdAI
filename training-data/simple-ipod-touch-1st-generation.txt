The first generation iPod Touch colloquially known as the iPod Touch G iPod Touch or original iPod Touch is a multi touch mobile device designed and marketed by Apple Inc. with a touchscreen based user interface. The first device of the iPod Touch series it was unveiled and released at Apple's media event on September . It is compatible with up to iPhone OS . . which was released on February . The first generation iPod touch was released after the first generation iPhone as a companion device. It had similar features but a thinner design with an all metal back except for a small corner cut out for WiFi . b g allowing it to use Safari to browse websites. It used the same pin connector as the first generation iPhone and previous iPods allowing most iPod accessories to work with the iPod touch. Unlike the iPhone the iPod Touch does not have a speaker or a camera. Due to the lack of a speaker system notification and alarm sounds were played with a piezoelectric buzzer. In December Internet user Martijn de Vos also known as devos has reverse engineered this device to successfully create a QEMU emulation of this device running iPhone OS . .

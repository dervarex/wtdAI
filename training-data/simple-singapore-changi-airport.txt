Singapore Changi Airport or simply Changi Airport is a commercial airport serving Singapore and is one of the largest transportation hubs in Asia. It is located in Changi at the eastern part of the island of Singapore on a site. The airport also consists of Jewel Changi Airport. The airport is operated by the Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore CAAS and is the home base of Singapore Airlines Singapore Airlines Cargo and Scoot. Singapore Changi Airport has been rated the best airport in the world by Skytrax for years in a row. The airport has also been rated as being one of the world's cleanest airports and highly rated international transit airports. Changi Airport opened on July and was the largest airport in Asia at the time of its opening. The first flight to arrive at the new airport came from Kuala Lumpur International Airport in Malaysia and the first flight to leave was for Penang also in Malaysia.

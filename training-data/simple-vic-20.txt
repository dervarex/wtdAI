Commodore VIC was a home computer released in . It was made by Commodore Business Machines. It was the first computer that sold over one million units. It was marketed as The Friendly Computer and was designed for beginners and home users. The VIC became the first computer model to sell more than one million units. The Commodore VIC was powered by a CPU running at MHz. Although the computer had KB of RAM only about . KB was available for BASIC programs. It had no internal data storage but could store programs on standard audio tapes. Later it could use . inch floppy disks as well. In early this computer was discontinued. The VIC was first released in Japan in as the VIC . It was introduced in the United States in and later sold in Europe. In Germany it was sold as the VC . Actor William Shatner appeared in television advertisements for the VIC in North America. The VIC was later followed by the more powerful Commodore which became Commodore's most successful computer. The VIC used a MOS Technology processor and came with KB of RAM. It connected to a television set and included Commodore BASIC . in ROM. Graphics and sound were generated by the VIC chip.

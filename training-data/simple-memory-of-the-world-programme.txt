The Memory of the World Programme is a part of the United Nations Educational Scientific and Cultural Organization UNESCO . It is part of a strategy for preserving history. The programme focuses on archives and libraries. The UNESCO programme began in . It promote the importance of historical documents. The program is administered by a body known as the International Advisory Committee IAC . There are members. Each is appointed by the UNESCO Director General. Select additions to the World Register are chosen at biannual meetings of the IAC.

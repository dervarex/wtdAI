The Komi language komi kyv also known as Zyryan Zyrian or Komi Zyryan Komi komi zyrjan kyv is one of the two types of the Komi language. The other type is Permyak. Komi is spoken by the Komi peoples native to the Komi Republic and other parts of Russia such as Nenetsia and Yamalia. There were speakers in . This went down to in . It was written in the Old Permic alphabet Komi Anbur in the th century. The Cyrillic script was introduced by Russian missionaries in the th century. This script replaced the Old Permic script.

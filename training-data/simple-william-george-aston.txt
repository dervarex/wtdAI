William George Aston April November was a British diplomat author and scholar expert in the language and history of Japan and Korea. Aston was born near Londonderry in Ireland. In he studied at Queen's College in Belfast which is now Queen's University Belfast. Aston was an interpreter in the British Legation in Japan in . He studied with Ernest Mason Satow. Aston worked in the British consular service in Tokyo Kobe and Nagasaki. He retired in . Along with Satow and Basil Hall Chamberlain Aston was one of three major British Japanologists during the th century. Aston was the first translator of the Nihongi into the English language . In Aston was the first Western diplomat to live in Korea. Aston donated his translations of Korean folk tales to the Asiatic Museum in St. Petersberg Russia and they were published in . This part of Aston's personal collection is now at the Academy of Sciences in St Petersburg.

Ladies' Code was a South Korean girl group formed by Polaris Entertainment and TheK formerly LOEN Music in . The group originally consisted of five members Ashley Sojung EunB Rise and Zuny. Their debut album was Code . They won the New Artist of The Year award at Gaon Kpop Chart Awards and the MBC music star award at MelON music awards. On September at KST time the group was involved in a car crash which killed members EunB and Rise leaving the group as a trio. In late Ladies' Code and the other Polaris artists held a memorial concert for the dead members. In Ladies Code returned for the first time since the accident with the single album Myst ry.

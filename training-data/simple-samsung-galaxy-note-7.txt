The Samsung Galaxy Note is a recalled and discontinued Android phablet smartphone made by Samsung. It was released on August . It has a . screen. On September of the first explosions of this device were reported these incidents led to the device being banned on planes and many other forms of public transport Samsung started offering recall and exchange of affected devices but later reports of exchanged devices exploding surfaced. Samsung responded by cancelling the change program ending production of the device and requesting that all owners of the phone return it a later investigation was discovered that the problem was a defect in the battery manufacturing. On December of Samsung started to send updates to disable and limit the charge of the phone for the people that chose not to return it first the update limited to then and on January of disabled the ability to charge the device effectively bricking them.

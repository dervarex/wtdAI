Kapamilya Channel stylized as Kapamilya channel is a hour Philippine pay television network owned and operated by ABS CBN Corporation. Kapamilya Online Live is a web based channel owned and operated by ABS CBN Corporation that launched on August and livestreams on Facebook and YouTube. On Facebook the shows are streamed on regular timeslots with some scheduled commentary breaks between timeblocks. On YouTube it streams throughout the day from nbsp a.m. to midnight except during TV special events . Livestreaming of shows on both platforms are available exclusively in the Philippines and it airs a variety of ABS CBN's old shows from Jeepney TV and other exclusive online shows. On August Kapamilya Online Live became accessible worldwide and streams hours with classic series weekdays Star Cinema movies and specials weekends aired throughout the midnight programming. On February Kapamilya Online Live started to offer a video on demand service of its shows accessible for seven days after their original streaming date.

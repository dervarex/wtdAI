South Uist Scottish Gaelic Uibhist a Deas is an island of the Outer Hebrides in Scotland. In the census it had a usually resident population of . There is a nature reserve and a number of sites of archaeological interest including the only location in Great Britain where prehistoric mummies have been found. The population is about Roman Catholic. The island in common with the rest of the Hebrides is one of the last remaining strongholds of the Gaelic language in Scotland. In South Uist and neighbouring Benbecula and Eriskay were involved in Scotland's biggest community land buyout to date. In the north west there is a missile testing range.

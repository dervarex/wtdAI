International University of Business Agriculture and Technology IUBAT was established in by M Alimullah Miyan and was subsequently recognized under the Private University Act PUA of now replaced by PUA . It is the first private university established in Bangladesh. The initial planning began in . Degree programs started in with agreement with Assumption University of Bangkok Thailand. IUBAT is a full member of the Association of Commonwealth Universities London and this extended formal recognition of IUBAT degrees in countries or regions of the Commonwealth. IUBAT has a permanent campus with Bighas of land at Embankment Drive Road Off Dhaka Ashulia Road Sector Uttara Model Town Dhaka .

The Reverend Wilbert Vere Awdry OBE June ndash March was an English cleric railway enthusiast and children's author. Simply known as the Reverend W. Awdry he was the creator of Thomas the Tank Engine the central figure in his acclaimed Railway Series. Awdry was born on June in Ampfield Hampshire. He was raised in Box Wiltshire. Awdry was educated at Marlborough House School Sussex Dauntseys School West Lavington Wiltshire St Peter's Hall Oxford BA and Wycliffe Hall Oxford. He was married to Margaret Awdry from until her death in . They had three children Christopher Awdry Hilary Fortnum and Veronica Chambers. Awdry died on March in Rodborough Stroud Gloucestershire from natural causes aged .

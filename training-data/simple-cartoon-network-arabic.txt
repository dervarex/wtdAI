Cartoon Network Arabic is a television channel that is broadcast to children in the Middle East and North Africa and it is the Arabic version of Cartoon Network. The channel was launched on October at AM U.A.E. time. The channel's launch goes with the opening of Turner Broadcasting System's new offices in Dubai Media City U.A.E. the channel's local owner. It is a Middle Eastern branch of Time Warner. The standard channel broadcasts via Arabsat Arabsat Badr and Nilesat. Cartoon Network Arabic is a free alternative to Cartoon Network MENA and Boomerang EMEA two pay TV channels offered in the Arab World in HD and in both English and Arabic exclusively on beIN Network since July despite the varying differences in programming schedules and available languages. In December Turner Broadcasting System entered an exclusivity deal with the Qatar based beIN Media Group. This deal lead to Cartoon Network Africa Boomerang Africa and TCM moving from OSN to the latter's beIN Network service and also caused the HD feed on YahLive to shut down the actual channel however is unaffected due to being a free to air channel on Nilesat and Arabsat Badr. Cartoon Network Arabic was launched on October at am GST. The channel's launch coincided with the opening of Turner Broadcasting System's offices in Dubai Media City UAE which is where the channel's local owner is located. On April at pm KSA time the channel aired a controversial scene in an episode of the television series Young Justice which depicted the characters Superboy and Miss Martian sharing a kiss on the lips with another character Icicle Jr. saying Man that's your sister. then stuttering and saying Oh dad's going to kill me. This scene angered and alarmed families in the MENA region as their strong Islamic beliefs forbid scenes such as this that displays implied incestuous themes despite the two characters on the show not being siblings but were using that as an alibi to get through a prison full criminals. This led to many Islamic scholars warning parents and families to not allow children to watch the channel. The channel subsequently apologised about the scene a few days after it was aired calling it a mistake. However this incident had subsequently led to the channel's former president to lose his job and be replaced by Tarek Mounir. The channel had been guilty for broadcasting these kinds of scenes before. Since its launch in October there had been several scenes displaying kissing awkward angles on women and other elements that don't align with Islamic values which spread all over social media platforms. Prior to the Young Justice scene the Channel was in control of kids television in the MENA region since its launch in and is said that it played a huge part in eliminating competitor Nickelodeon Arabia and causing its closure in as well as dominating other competitors that Include MBC Spacetoon Aljazeera Children's Channel Now Jeem TV and Baraem who were controlling the kids Television scene in MENA prior to the channel's launch. The channel was also praised for its organised timetable of airing shows order of episodes airing and continuous airing of series that were almost instantly adored by its audience. However this controversy led to the channel implemnting a censorship strategy that had ended up killing the channel as many episodes of series such as The Amazing World of Gumball and Adventure Time were censored and many episodes were not even dubbed and the plot had become confusing for viewers. On top of that television series such as Powerpuff Girls were cancelled due to violent scenes. All this led to a drastic decrease in views for the channel which ended up with the channel becoming the second Cartoon Network channel in the MENA region as Cartoon Network MENA was launched on July which airs in both English and Arabic and is up to date to the parent channel in regards of new series and episodes whilst if Cartoon Network Arabia were to even air those new series or episodes it would be months later than its sister channels.

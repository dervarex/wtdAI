Salaam TV was a Philippine government owned Islamic channel owned by the Presidential Communications Office through the People's Television Network PTV . The channel's main programming is solely focused on Filipino Muslims and other Islamic communities in the country. At present the channel is on test broadcast via digital terrestrial television on PTV's digital subchannel via UHF and Expand to cable television on SkyCable and Destiny Cable Channel depending on the digital boxes' channel availability from noon to p.m. It was also the second Islamic television network based in the Philippines following the launch of Davao based Islamic cable channel Mensahe TV. The channel launched on July . Former PTV News presenter Princess Habibah Sarip Paudac the first Filipina newsreader to wear a hijab on a national television newscast took role as the station manager of the station. President Rodrigo Duterte in his first State of the Nation Address on July stated that the government will put up two state run TV channels for Filipino Muslims and the Lumad hence Salaam TV was established while the channel for the Lumad is still being planned. The channel ceased broadcast on September after years and a month and was replaced by the TeleRadyo Channel of Radyo Pilipinas .

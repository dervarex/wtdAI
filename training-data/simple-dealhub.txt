DealHub also called DealHub.io is a software company. It makes tools that help companies create sales quotes manage subscriptions and handle billing. The company works with other business software such as Salesforce Microsoft Dynamics and HubSpot. The company was founded in in Israel. Its first name was Valooto. The founders were Eyal Elbahary Eyal Orgil and Alon Lubin. In the company raised money from investors to grow its business. In the company changed its name to DealHub. In DealHub raised million US dollars in new funding. In it raised another million US dollars. In the same year it added an integration with HubSpot. In DealHub made its product available in the Microsoft Azure Marketplace. In the company announced a partnership with Crowe LLP. Later that year it acquired Subskribe a company that makes subscription and billing software. DealHub makes software for business sales teams. The software helps companies create sales quotes manage subscriptions and bill customers. It also includes online deal rooms where sales teams can share offers and documents with customers. DealHub's software can connect with other business systems such as Salesforce Microsoft Dynamics and HubSpot. In HubSpot listed DealHub as one of its recommended sales applications. In the same year Frost Sullivan named DealHub Company of the Year in the CPQ software category in North America.

cowsay is a configurable text filter that generates an ASCII art image of a cow or other creatures saying or thinking a message. Originally created by Tony Monroe in the program is written in Perl and has become a light hearted tool often found in terminal screenshots IRC and documentation. Besides the default cow users can choose from a wide variety of alternative character templates called .cow files including Tux the Penguin and various fantasy animals. A companion program renders messages as thoughts using thought bubbles instead of speech bubbles . The cow's expression and behavior can be customized using parameters that alter its eyes tongue or replace it entirely. Cowsay has achieved cult status in hacker culture and continues to be included in many Linux distributions. It was even featured as Debian's Package of the Day in .

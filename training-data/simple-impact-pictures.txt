Impact Pictures is an British independent movie production company founded in earlier by Jeremy Bolt and Paul W. S. Anderson. Alongside from Anderson's movie projects including the Resident Evil movie series the company has also been involved in a number of TV and independent productions. The company has also collaborated with several larger studio most frequently by Davis Films Constantin Films and Screen Gems.

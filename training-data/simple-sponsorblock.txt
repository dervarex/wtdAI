SponsorBlock is a free and open source browser extension and open API for crowdsourcing the skipping of sponsor segments and other defined sections in YouTube videos. Developed by Ajay Ramachandran the extension uses a community maintained database of timestamped video segments that are automatically skipped when a user plays a matching video. As of April SponsorBlock has over users on Google Chrome and over on Firefox. It is available for Chrome Firefox Microsoft Edge Safari macOS and iOS and Android and has been ported by third parties to applications including MPV Kodi and Chromecast. SponsorBlock was first released on July . In its initial form only sponsor segments could be skipped. In June Ramachandran added support for additional segment categories including self promotion and intermissions. On July the extension was featured in Mozilla's Extension Spotlight after which it became the highest rated recommended extension on the Firefox Add ons store. An update in January added support for marking whole videos as sponsorships. In June SponsorBlock reported that YouTube had begun testing server side advertisement injection a technique that embeds ads directly into video streams in order to bypass conventional ad blockers. The finding was reported by the German technology publication Heise online which cited SponsorBlock as the source. Users submit the start and end timestamps of video segments and choose a category for each segment. The extension automatically skips known segments during playback. After a skip a brief pop up allows the user to vote on the segment or undo the skip. The number of segments each user has submitted and skipped is tracked on a public leaderboard. Supported segment categories include sponsorships self promotion interaction reminders like and subscribe prompts intros outros subscription reminders filler content and non music sections in music videos. Users can configure individually which categories to skip automatically. Segment retrieval uses a privacy preserving query system the extension sends a prefix of the video ID's hash rather than the full ID preventing the server from determining which specific video is being watched. The extension's source code and the full segment database are publicly available. The database and API are also used in third party ports for MPV Kodi and Chromecast as well as in neural network applications. SponsorBlock is available in languages. DeArrow is a companion browser extension developed by Ramachandran that uses the same crowdsourcing model as SponsorBlock to replace YouTube video titles and thumbnails with community submitted alternatives. Its stated goal is to reduce sensationalism and clickbait. Where no community submissions exist for a video DeArrow automatically reformats the original title according to the user's chosen casing preference title case or sentence case and sets a screenshot from a random timestamp verified to fall outside any SponsorBlock indexed sponsor segment as the thumbnail. DeArrow is built on the SponsorBlock codebase and shares the same backend server. As of April it has over users on Google Chrome and over on Firefox. It has been marked as a recommended extension by both Chrome and Firefox. SponsorBlock was featured in Mozilla's Extension Spotlight in July where Mozilla described it as a community driven add on that allows users to skip sponsored content in YouTube videos. It subsequently became the highest rated recommended extension on the Firefox Add ons store. The extension has been covered by technology publications including MakeUseOf Ghacks and the German technology magazine CHIP.

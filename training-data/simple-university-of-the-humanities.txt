The University of the Humanities Mongolian abbreviated as UOH is an accredited private university located in Ulaanbaatar Mongolia. Established in the university specializes in foreign languages humanities business administration social sciences and information technology. Vision Our mission is to empower creative minds to contribute to social progress through transformative education and impactful research in the humanities and social sciences. Slogan Creative intelligence is your future success. The university has evolved through several developmental stages since its inception Founded as the Russian Language Institute under the National University of Mongolia by a decree of the Council of Ministers of the People's Republic of Mongolia. Reorganized as an independent State Pedagogical Institute of the Russian Language. Expanded to become the Foreign Language Institute offering linguistics translation and teaching programs. Launched programs in Business Administration Finance Banking Management and Journalism. Commenced Master's and Doctoral Ph.D. degree programs. Reorganized as the School of Humanities. Successfully attained national accreditation and was renamed the University of the Humanities. The School of Business Administration was established. Introduced Information Systems Software Engineering Graphic Design and International Relations programs. Became a member of the Asia Pacific Quality Network APQN . Established the School of Information and Communication Management. Established the School of International Relations and Social Studies. Became a partner in the European Union's TACTIC ERASMUS MUNDUS and ARTEST projects. Became an official Cisco Networking Academy provider. The university's academic structure is organized into the following departments Department of International Relations and Social Studies Department of Business Administration Department of Psychology Department of Finance and Economics Department of Computer Science Department of Foreign Languages Department of Journalism Media and Technology Department of Education Studies Department of Marketing The University of the Humanities maintains partnerships with universities across countries facilitating joint projects and exchange programs. Key joint degree programs include Korea University of Media Arts South Korea program in Journalism. Mokwon University South Korea program in Tourism. Dongguk University South Korea program in Software Engineering.

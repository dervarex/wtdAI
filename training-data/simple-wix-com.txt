Wix.com is a freemium online platform that allows users to create websites and Facebook pages. It competes with Webs Weebly Yola and other web hosting and creation sites. Users can add tools to their Wix websites. These tools include social media online shops contact forms and e mail marketing. Wix is free to use but people can pay for more features. Wix was created in by Avishai Abrahami Nadav Abrahami and Giora Kaplan. The company is located in Tel Aviv with offices in San Francisco New York City and Dnepropetrovsk. It is funded by investors Insight Venture Partners Mangrove Capital Partners Bessemer Venture Partners and Benchmark Capital. It entered open beta in . Wix became a public company in . It raised about million US dollars in its first stock market offering. According to W Techs about . of all websites used Wix in May . Wix has bought other companies to make its services stronger. In it bought the online art community DeviantArt. In and it bought Inkfrog SpeedETab and Rise.ai which gave more tools for online shops and restaurants. In Wix bought Hour One and Base to add new artificial intelligence tools.

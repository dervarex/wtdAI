DZRH News Television is a hour cable television channel of Manila Broadcasting Company in the Philippines. Its programs are mainly from MBC's AM station DZRH. DZRH TV can be seen on Cignal Digital TV channel Cablelink channel and cable TV affiliates nationwide as well as live internet TV streaming on their official website. On October DZRH TV began as an Interactive Radio Cable TV channel. It was first seen on SkyCable later on Dream Satellite TV other cable TV Channels nationwide with cable affiliates. It was first called TV Natin then DZRH RadyoVision. It was later changed to DZRH Television or RHTV. RHTV is now available in satellite Measat platform along with other MBC Radio stations. RHTV airs the live video feed of DZRH programs except Ang Dating Daan and selected radio dramas. RHTV also airs its produced programs.

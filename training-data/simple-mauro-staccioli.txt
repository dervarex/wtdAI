Mauro Staccioli February January was an Italian sculptor. He was born in Volterra Italy. Staccioli started teaching and in joined the Academy of Fine Arts of Brera in Milan. In the beginning of the s his career began as his works were about urban environment and with sculpture tries to give answers to social questions.

The Macintosh Classic II also called the Performa is a personal computer made by Apple Computer Inc. It was sold from October to September . Like the early Macintosh models it had a small all in one design with a built in inch black and white screen. It also had a handle built into the case so it was easier to carry though laptops were not common at the time. The Classic II used the same MHz Motorola processor as the older Macintosh SE . But it was about slower because it had a cheaper bit data bus not the faster bit bus of the SE . It came with MB of memory and could be upgraded to MB. It had a or MB hard drive. Even though it looked like the Macintosh Classic the inside of the Classic II was more like the Macintosh LC. Apple used the same custom chips as the LC which helped lower the number of parts and make it cheaper to build. But unlike the LC and SE it didn't have a Processor Direct Slot for upgrades making it the second compact Mac without one. In Apple rebranded the Classic II as part of its new Macintosh Performa series calling it the Performa . It had the same hardware but came with extra software depending on the store. This software usually included At Ease a simpler interface than the normal Finder. The Performa cost around US when it came out. The Classic II was the last compact black and white Mac and the last desktop Mac to include a port for an external floppy disk drive. Apple stopped supporting the Classic II on January . The Classic II had a pin internal slot that could be used for a math co processor FPU or extra ROM but not for general upgrades. Apple didn't make any official cards for it but some companies made their own like the FastMath Classic II and others that used the chip. Later hobbyists also made open source expansion cards.

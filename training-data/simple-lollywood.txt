Lollywood is the name of Pakistani movie industry that is based in the city of Lahore. The name is a combination of the city's first letter with the name Hollywood . The word Lollywood was first used in the summer of . The movies made in Lollywood are usually in Urdu. Some are made in Punjabi which is the main and official language of the province of Punjab where Lollywood is located. Some are also in English.

The iTunes Store is a digital media store operated by Apple Inc. It was opened on April as a result of Steve Jobs push to open a digital marketplace for music. The iTunes Store is available on most Apple devices including the Mac inside the Music app the iPhone the iPad the iPod touch and the Apple TV as well as on Windows inside iTunes . As of January the number of songs sold worldwide has surpassed billion. The iTunes Store is also the most popular online movie store as of April the store had sold approximately million movies . In the U.S the iTunes Store is the third largest music retailer overall. The files you order are sent along with usage guidelines established by FairPlay Apple's version of digital rights management. New songs are added to the iTunes catalog every day while the iTunes Store is updated every Tuesday. The store currently has over songs available in all national versions of the iTunes Store .

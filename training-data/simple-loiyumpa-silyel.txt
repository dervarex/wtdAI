The Loiyumpa Silyel Modern Meitei Loiyumba Sinyen also termed as the Loyumpa Silyel Modern Meitei Loyumba Sinyen or the Loiyumpa Shilyel Modern Meitei Loiyumba Shinyen or the Loyumpa Shilyel Modern Meitei Loyumba Shinyen or the Loyamba Sinyen is an th th century written constitution regulated in the Manipur kingdom during the regime of King Loiyumba CE . A promulgation of the proto constitution in CE by King Naophangba was reduced to a final format in CE. Historically it is the first written constitution and one of the well recorded antique manuscripts of the kingdom. Its Constitutionalism was replaced by the Manipur State Constitution Act that was functional until Manipur was merged into India on October . It includes the accounts of the distribution of the duties to the subjects of the king. Its rules and regulations remains to be an authoritative work in the Meitei social system till the present day Manipur.

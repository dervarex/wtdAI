Immunet was a free cloud based antivirus made by Cisco Systems. It used the ClamAV engine and its own detection system. Immunet worked together with other antivirus software and gave users extra protection online. The app did not store virus data on the computer. Instead all virus signatures were kept in the cloud so users always had up to date protection without downloading updates. When one user's system detected a virus that protection was shared instantly with all other Immunet users. Immunet was first created by a small company and later bought by Sourcefire in January . Later Cisco Systems acquired Sourcefire and continued Immunet as a free product. Immunet was available in two versions Immunet Free for personal use cloud based protection. Immunet Plus paid version for business use discontinued in . PC Magazine tested Immunet in and rated it as fair for virus detection. Immunet's cloud system made it faster and lighter than many traditional antivirus programs but offline protection was limited. The company suggested using it together with another antivirus for best results. On January Cisco officially ended Immunet's service. The software stopped connecting to its cloud servers and its support forum was closed on February . Cisco stated that it would focus future efforts on its Talos threat intelligence platform instead.

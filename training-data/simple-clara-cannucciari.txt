Cologira Clara Cannucciari n e Bonfanti August November was an American YouTuber. She hosted the web series Great Depression Cooking with Clara and wrote the book Clara's Kitchen. Clara Cannucciari was born in Melrose Park Illinois on August . Her parents Giuseppe and Giuseppina Bonfanti had recently moved from Sicily. The family was very poor during the Great Depression. Because of this her mother helped the family save money. From to she made her mother's meals for the web series Great Depression Cooking with Clara. Her grandson Christopher made the series. The last episode Tomato Sauce was posted on December . Unreleased episodes have been shared over time. On March Christopher announced he would start posting more videos of her onto their YouTube channel. Clara Cannucciari died on November when she was years old. Her daughter in law four grandchildren and three great grandchildren were still alive. Her son Carl died in when he was years old.

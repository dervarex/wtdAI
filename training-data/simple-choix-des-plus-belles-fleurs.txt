Choix des plus belles fleurs 'Selection of the most beautiful flowers' is a book of watercolour paintings by French botanical artist Pierre Joseph Redout July June . The full title is Choix des plus belles fleurs et de quelques branches des plus beaux fruits. D di LL. AA. RR. les princesses Louise et Marie d'Orl ans in French. English translation of the title is Selection of the most beautiful flowers and some branches of the finest fruits . A folio edition with colour pages was printed in Paris in . From May to June special pages with pictures were printed. The work was in parts. Each part had four pictures of flowers flowering trees or fruit. The printing method for such works in the th and early th century was engraving on copperplate. It is a form of intaglio printing. Different colours could be got by running the plates through once for each colour so building up the final picture. Fine colour works could also be hand coloured by teams of women who were experts at the job. What this means is that every illustration in a book was coloured by hand. They had the artist's original in front of their eyes as they worked. The copper engraving gave the outline and any other black lines or areas. In Redout used a way to make prints which used both these methods. First an engraver made an engraving from the painting with small dots instead of lines. Then the colours were put on the engraved plate before each printing. After printing each print was finished by hand with more colour. Redout claimed to have invented this method. He was challenged in the courts but he won the case.

WYSIWYG or is an acronym for What You See Is What You Get used in computing to describe a system where things you edit on the on screen editor appear almost the same to the final output which might be a printed document a web page a PowerPoint presentation or even the lighting for a concert. Web designers often use this word to talk about their work.

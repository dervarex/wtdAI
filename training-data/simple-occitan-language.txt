Occitan is a Romance language spoken in the south of France the Occitan Valleys of Italy the Val d'Aran of Catalonia and Monaco known as lenga d' c by its native speakers The regions together are called Occitania. This is an unofficial name for a region in three countries. Its justification is that it is united by a culture. Occitan took a different path from Latin to the main official languages. It is sometimes called lenga d' c language of oc French langue d'oc because its word for yes is c as opposed to oil oui or s in other languages. That is one way to classify Romance languages. Occitan is an official language of Catalonia. Since September the Parliament of Catalonia has considered Aranese Occitan to be the officially preferred language for use in the Val d'Aran.

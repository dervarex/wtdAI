Barbara Liskov born November is an American computer scientist and professor. She developed two programming languages CLU and Argus. They are used in almost every modern programming language. She earned the Association for Computing Machinery's ACM Turing Award. Barbara Liskov was born in Los Angeles California. She got her BA in mathematics at UC Berkeley. After she graduated in she went to work at the Mitre Corporation. After a year Liskov moved to Harvard and worked on computer translation of human languages. In she moved back to California and received her PhD in computer science at Stanford University. Liskov went to work at the Mitre Corporation in Bedford Massachusetts. She worked on computer design and operating systems. During that time she created the Venus Computer which was specially created to support complex software. In she took a position at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology MIT where she started her work with data abstraction. She thought of writing computer code like doing a math problem not a technology problem. She wanted to use logic and have the language look beautiful. She developed two programming languages CLU and Argus. They are used in almost every modern programming language. She later published Abstraction and Specification in Program Development and Program Development in Java Abstraction Specification and Object Oriented Design . In Liskov won the A.M. Turing Award.

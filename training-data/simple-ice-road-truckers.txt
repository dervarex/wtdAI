Ice Road Truckers is a Canadian American documentary style reality television series that first appeared on television on The History Channel on June first season . The show follows the activities of truck drivers which operate on routes that contain frozen lakes and rivers in the remote Arctic territories of Canada and Alaska. On October a spinoff series called IRT Deadliest Roads was shown after the season four finale. Rick Yemm Alex Debogorski and Lisa Kelly traveled to India and put their driving skills to the test on the narrow dangerous mountain roads that went from Delhi to Shimla then up to the Karchan and Kuppa hydroelectric dam construction sites in the Himalayas. The second season of IRT Deadliest Roads was shown on September . Six North American drivers went to Bolivia to haul cargo along the Yungas Road the road is known for its extreme hazards.

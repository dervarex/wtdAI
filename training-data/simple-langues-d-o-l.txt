Langues d'o l which literally means in English 'languages of yes' is the linguistic and historical name for the Gallo Romance languages which developed from Latin in the northern territories of Roman Gaul that now are occupied by northern France part of Belgium and the Channel Islands. Romance languages Gallo Romance languages Langue d'o l or O l languages Langue d'oc or Occitan language Langue d'o l in the singular O l dialects and O l languages in the plural refer to all the ancient northern Gallo Romance languages as well as their modern day descendants. The most widely spoken modern O l language is French but others include Norman Walloon Picard Gallo Poitevin Saintongeais and Champenois. They share many common linguistic features. One of them is that O l pronounced or as in modern French oui was the ancestor of the word for yes that is used in all of the O l languages. Oc is the word used in territories to the south and so the Occitan language is also called langue d'oc literal English translation language of yes .

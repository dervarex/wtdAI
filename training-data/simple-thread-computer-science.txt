A thread in computer science is short for a thread of execution. Threads are a way for a program to split itself into two or more simultaneously or pseudo simultaneously running tasks. Threads and processes differ from one operating system to another. In general a thread is contained inside a process and different threads in the same process share the same resources. Different processes in the same multitasking operating system do not share the same resources. Threads are lightweight in terms of the system resources they consume as compared with processes.

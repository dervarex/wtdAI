VH Soul is an Former American pay television channel. The network is owned by Paramount Global. The channel debuted on August and is currently owned by Paramount Global's VH . The channel showcases R B funk soul neo soul hip hop jazz and Motown music from various decades. VH Soul is closed august and replaced by BET Soul From the channel was originally named VH Soul. core artist as VH Soul included Aretha Franklin Stevie Wonder and Patti LaBelle. The channel which was originally a commercial free service debuted on August at pm with VH Smooth which took the air an hour before as part of the MTV Digital Suite of digital cable channels which was sold only to cable providers to give them an advantage over satellite services. The first video shown on the channel was Boogie Wonderland by Earth Wind Fire. The channel's theme music was composed by Walter Afanasieff. Before the first music video played at pm the channel showed a picture of Tupac Shakur and underneath it the words In Memory of Tupac Shakur we will never be forgotten appeared as the channel was dedicated to Tupac Shakur and the rest of Shakur's life. In its early years VH Soul's main focus was on R B and soul videos of the late s s s and s along with live performance clips from even earlier years. Janet Jackson Prince TLC Usher Tony Toni Tone Marvin Gaye Aretha Franklin Patti LaBelle and Stevie Wonder were some of the channel's main staples. By the channel had stopped showing most of the pre s videos since these were often played on VH Classic's Soul Power program. The channel then gradually began to focus more on underground alternative and old skool hip hop videos while continuing to feature new R B musicians. On February VH Soul revamped its programming and introduced different one hour themed video blocks to its schedule as opposed to the prior random rotation of videos. However the basic 'wheel schedule' structure remained and does so to this day. After VH Soul revamped its programming most record labels signed deals for Hip Hop videos broadcast on VH Soul in June including the labels Columbia Records and Epic Records. In Spring VH Soul alongside its sister networks MTV Jams and MTV Hits were briefly dropped from Time Warner Cable's Southern California systems that were formerly operated by Adelphia and Comcast. However all three channels returned to TWC within a few months under a new specialized service tier. To date however the three networks remain conspicuously absent from many of Time Warner Cable and Bright House Networks' systems most notably in New York City. Even as the 'Digital Suite' concept was discontinued the network has neither offered to DirecTV nor Dish Network until . The last edition of the Vibe Awards aired on the channel in November after a one year hiatus due to the merger of The WB and UPN into The CW. By this time as the channel's distribution increased VH Soul began showing regular advertising. Commercials were removed in but were restored on January . For a short time from until the network aired non music video programming in some timeslots including VH 's The Salt n Pepa Show alongside the documentary Black to the Future as part of the network's February Black History Month programming along with out of format programming such as Hip Hop Honors VH Rock Docs and other programming from its Celebreality strand of original programming. Viewer complaints that resulted from the programming substitutions soon overpowered VH 's management alongside other complaints for sister network VH Classic straying from its own mission. Soon VH moved away from out of format programming on both channels and by late VH Soul resumed a video format. On December the channel was given over to BET Networks as part of the continuing reorganization of Viacom's now Paramount's assets due to ratings and financial issues in an issue that had already resulted in BET Networks taking editorial control of the former MTV Jams two months before. The network was rebranded as BET Soul on that day. Core artist as BET Soul include Musiq Soulchild Kelly Price Christina Milian Aaron Soul Terri Walker Dru Hill Patti LaBelle and The Isley Brothers. The first song as BET Soul was True to Your Heart by Degrees and Stevie Wonder. The launch party of the channel's rebrand to BET Soul was held in New Orleans guests included Britney Spears Eminem Dr. Dre Cent Jennifer Lopez Pharrell Mase the members of Bone Thugs n Harmony Jamie Foxx Ginuwine Mary J. Blige Jay Z Lil' Kim DMC of Run DMC R. Kelly and Sean Combs. Other guests included Rick Rubin and Russell Simmons the founders of Def Jam Recordings. The launch party was presented by Billboard. Presently a selection of R B hits from the past years as well as several s and early s hip hop videos can be seen on BET Soul. Several of the more R B influenced modern day rappers and their newest videos can also be regularly seen in heavy rotation on the channel for example The Roots De La Soul Kanye West Common and Talib Kweli. However the channel still occasionally airs older s or early s R B soul hits such as Prince's Kiss Mariah Carey's Vision Of Love Stevie Wonder's Superstition or Chaka Khan's I Feel For You . In Rotation formerly The Soul Player A random rotation of videos. The format is similar to how the channel was operated before February . Chronicled formerly The Soul Story A block of videos by a single artist or group that are mostly shown in chronological order. Diggin' In The Crates formerly Soul School This show features old school hip hop and R B music videos. WORLDwide formerly One Planet One Soul and Island Soul This show features music videos by Caribbean and international artists with diverse genres such as Afrobeat dancehall and reggae. The Calming formerly Soulphrodisiac A Quiet Storm formatted show. The show primarily plays love songs and slow jams and is similar to BET After Dark or BET's Midnight Love. Woke Wednesday A weekly block showcasing politically charged or woke music videos every Wednesday. Hit List Loading A countdown block featuring the Top Soul and R B videos of the week. Ascending Soul A block which features experimental soul videos. Soul Squared A block of old and current videos by various artists which airs two videos from the same artist back to back in a similar manner to VH Classic's Tuesday Twoplay program. Speak Your Soul A viewer requested video block. Viewers made requests on the channel's official website.

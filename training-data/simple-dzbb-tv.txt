DZBB TV channel is a television station in Metro Manila Philippines serving as the flagship of the GMA network. It is owned and operated by the network's namesake corporate parent alongside GTV flagship DWDB TV channel . Both stations share studios at the GMA Network Center EDSA corner Timog Avenue Diliman Quezon City while DZBB TV's hybrid analog and digital transmitting facilities are located at the GMA Tower of Power Tandang Sora Avenue Barangay Culiat Quezon City sharing facilities with sister stations GTV and Barangay LS . and PBCom Tower Ayala Avenue corner V.A. Rufino Street Makati.

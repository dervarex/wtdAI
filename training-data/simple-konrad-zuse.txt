Konrad Zuse June December was a German infrastructure engineer computer scientist inventor and businessman. His greatest achievement was the world's first programmable computer the Z which began operating in May . Zuse is often considered the inventor of the modern computer. Zuse is also credited with creating the S computer considered the first process controlled computer. He founded one of the earliest computer businesses in producing the Z the world's first commercial computer. From he designed the first high level programming language Plankalk l. In Zuse proposed the concept of a computationally based universe in his book Rechnender Raum Spatial Computing . Much of his early work was funded by family and commerce but after he was provided with resources by the Nazi German government. Due to World War II Zuse's achievements went largely unnoticed in the United Kingdom and the United States. His first possible document influence on an American company was IBM's selection of his patents in . There is a replica of the Z computer as well as the original Z computer in the Deutsches Museum in Munich.

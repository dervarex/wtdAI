Both this calculator and the Silver Edition are called the monochromes because of their black and white screens. The TI Plus was made after the TI Plus the way it works is mostly the same. It is much faster than the TI Plus and has three times as much storage. The TI Plus also introduced a USB port for talking to another calculator or computer and a clock. The TI Plus C Silver Edition also known as the TI Plus CSE C SE or C SE was first made in as the first Texas Instruments graphing calculator with a color screen that uses a Zilog Z CPU. It added a by pixel color screen a changed version of the TI Plus's . MP operating system and a removable nbsp mAh lithium ion battery that you can keep charging over and over again. People liked its detailed color screen which added new features such as graphing multiple functions together in different colors. The calculator was criticized for being a lot slower than its predecessors these speed issues were caused by the old Zilog Z CPU. Even though that CPU was fine for older calculators in the TI Plus series it was slow in this one because of the color screen. This calculator showed people that a color screen calculator was useful and Texas Instruments started making TI Plus CE with a new CPU two years later.

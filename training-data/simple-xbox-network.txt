Xbox Network formerly known as Xbox LIVE is the online gaming service created by Microsoft for the Xbox Xbox Xbox One and Xbox Series S X. It costs about USD per year in America and has over million subscribers as of . There are two versions of Xbox Live Xbox Game Pass Core formerly Gold and Free. Free allows an online 'friends list' and downloads but does not allow users to play games against other people online. Game Pass Core is the same as Free but with the addition of online gaming and a free select library of games with a cost of a year. Subscribers can pay with a credit card or by special cards which are bought in shops and enter the codes online. Microsoft Points or MS points was the previous currency of Xbox Live. In they changed it to actual currency such as Dollars or Euros. On Xbox Live you can buy many things ranging from movies to games to DLC. You can buy full games on Xbox and Xbox One or Xbox Live Arcade games.

Taeyeon's discography consists of a studio album two EPs twenty singles and ten soundtracks. The singer debuted in girl group Girls' Generation in August and then in Girls' Generation TTS in May . Her solo career began in with the release of her debut mini album entitled I. The EP was a commercial success in South Korea where it reached number two on Gaon Album Chart and since then has sold more than physical copies. The single of the same name managed to position itself in the first position of Gaon Digital Chart and has sold more than million digital copies. In February Taeyeon published Rain as the first single from SM Station. The single reached the maximum position in Gaon Digital Chart and was ranked number three in Billboard World Digital Songs. His second EP Why was released in June reaching the top in Gaon Album Chart and managed to sell more than physical copies. He produced two singles that were included in the mini album Starlight and Why . Subsequently he released a digital single titled in November which was ranked second in Gaon Digital Chart. Taeyeon released her first studio album My Voice in February . The album won first place in Gaon Album Chart and sold more than physical copies in its first month of release. He produced the single Fine which was number one on Gaon Digital Chart and reached number seven on Billboard World Digital Songs.

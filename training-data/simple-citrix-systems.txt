Citrix Systems Inc. is an American multinational software company. Company provides server application and desktop virtualization networking software as a service SaaS and cloud computing technologies. Citrix products use by over clients worldwide. Citrix has headquarters in Fort Lauderdale Florida and Santa Clara California. Also company has additional development centers in Canada Denmark Germany India and the United Kingdom. Citrix was founded in Richardson Texas in by former IBM developer Ed Iacobucci who served as head of the company till . The company's first employees were five other IBM engineers Iacobucci invited to join his team. Citrix was originally named Citrus but changed its name after an existing company claimed trademark rights. The Citrix name is a portmanteau of Citrus and UNIX. It began by developing remote access products for Microsoft operating systems. Citrix was in partnership with the company throughout its history. Citrix acquired Sequoia Software Corp. in and ExpertCity in . In the company opened a headquarters in Silicon Valley. In August Citrix announced a partnership with Google to bring the company's products to Chrome OS devices. In May Citrix acquired Virtual Computer maker of intelligent desktop virtualization. Also in Citrix expanded its partnership with Google. In Citrix changed the names of some of its products to represent its shift to a cloud operating model.

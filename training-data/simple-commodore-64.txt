The Commodore used a MOS Technology processor and had kB of RAM. It could display up to colors and supported hardware sprites through the VIC II graphics chip. Sound was produced by the SID chip which provided three sound channels and became famous for its music and sound effects. The Commodore is listed by Guinness World Records as the best selling single desktop computer model of all time. Around million units are believed to have been sold worldwide. The computer remains popular with collectors and retro computing enthusiasts and new software is still being developed for it.

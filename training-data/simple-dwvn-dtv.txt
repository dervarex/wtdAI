DWVN DTV channel is a non commercial digital television station of Gateway UHF Television Broadcasting. Its studios are located at San Juan cor. Donada Streets Pasay while its transmitter is located at GSat Technical Facilities and Earth Station Building First Global Technopark Complex Lot Governor's Drive Barangay Ulong Tubig Carmona Cavite. As of December the TV station's analog signal was permanently shutdown. UHF channel began its operations on June carrying ABN the religious U.S. based network of Seventh day Adventist Church. In the later part of September it started carrying Hope Channel PHL which is also associated by the said religion and broadcast every night while ABN broadcast every morning. On April due to some transmitter and signal problems of the network the station temporarily became silent. On May it returned on air with the same transmitting power. It closed its operation again on September together with DZRJ TV which then transmitted from RJTV's tower before their transmitter shift in . It was later found that the transmitter of UHF was struck by lightning bolt. As a result the station shut down temporarily for maintenance. It returned to airwaves on November DZRJ and December DWVN respectively. Around Gateway began carrying Hope Channel PHL a U.S. based Filipino language religious network which is also associated by the said religion. Initially aired as a blocktime program at select times it later became available as a dedicated digital channel on terrestrial television in . From being a religious affiliate Gateway leaned away from its privilege and converted into a basic blocktime broadcaster in mid by carrying Global News Network programs on its digital signal. Later Gateway quietly ended its direct ties with ABN instead the main analog service carries a separate Hope Channel PH's programming as well as other archived Adventist content. The analog service then limited programming at nighttime although its signal remains active daily from morning to midnight. By April Gateway re introduced Hope Channel International from its digital television lineup while its affiliation with ABN was re secured by the network albeit as a digital subchannel. But on November ABN will be lasted on digital as its digital subchannel and it aired the mirror feed of Hope Channel Philippines. On December the station shut down its analog signal. On June Gateway moved its transmitter site from Antipolo City to GSAT First Global Technopark complex in Carmona Cavite with its watts brand new digital transmitter.

The word monitor is a Latin word for warner or suggester. The many uses of the word are listed below. A computer monitor display screen A medical monitor or any other hardware device that measures electrical events such as a heart rate monitor A speaker used on stage facing the musicians or in a studio to enable musicians to hear what is being performed A video monitor a television like device used in studio lab or test environments A baby monitor which lets parents hear what happens in their babies room from other parts of the house A hall monitor one who patrols the hall of a school USS Monitor the ironclad warship of the American Civil War A monitor warship a type of ship based on the USS Monitor and built by several navies for coastal defense in the s and s. It reappeared in a different form during the World War I and lasted until the end of World War II A river monitor the strongest type of river warships Monitor lizards a family of large tropical lizards Varanidae

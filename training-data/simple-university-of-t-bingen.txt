The University of T bingen officially the Eberhard Karl University of T bingen is a public university in the city of T bingen Baden W rttemberg Germany. The University of T bingen was founded in . In Germany T bingen became known as one of the five classical university towns . In T bingen joined the Matariki Network of Universities MNU together with Dartmouth College USA Durham University UK Queen's University Canada University of Otago New Zealand University of Western Australia Australia and Uppsala University Sweden . In the university established the T bingen Center for Korean Studies at Korea University TUCKU . This is T bingen's third branch in Asia after the Center for Japanese Language in Ky to and the European Center for Chinese Studies ECCS at Peking University. Alumni include former German President Horst K hler and Nobel laureates Christiane N sslein Volhard Hartmut Michel and G nter Blobel. In the late th century Johannes Kepler studied at T bingen. In the th century Friedrich H lderlin Friedrich Schelling and Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel shared student rooms. Notable professors include Joseph Ratzinger and Hans Kung.

Oppo Electronics Corporation is a Chinese commercial organization and manufacturer of consumer electronics. They were run by BBK Electronics until . The brand is registered in many countries worldwide. Oppo Digital Inc.'s USA division develops and manufactures audio visual equipment with a range of high end Blu ray players headphones and headphone amplifiers in the product line. In OPPO entered the portable electronics segment and launched its own mobile phone. The brand name Oppo was registered in China in and launched in by Tony Chen in Dongguan China. In they introduced their first device internationally the Oppo X MP player. Since then the company has expanded to over countries and become a major player in electronic devices around the world. In June Oppo became the largest smartphone manufacturer in China selling its phones at more than retail outlets. In Oppo Digital announced that they were discontinuing their disc player business in major markets and focusing on mobile devices. Oppo was the top smartphone brand in China in and was ranked fifth in market share worldwide. In June the company signed an agreement with FC Barcelona to become a sponsor of the Spanish football club.

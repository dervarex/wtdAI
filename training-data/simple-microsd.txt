The cards are used in mobile phones cameras handheld game systems and other mobile devices. Many microSD cards are sold with a standard SD adapter so that people can use them in devices that take standard SD but not microSD cards. TransFlash and microSD cards are the same they can be used in place of each other but microSD has support for SDIO mode. This lets microSD slots support non memory jobs like Bluetooth GPS and Near Field Communication by attaching a device in place of a memory card.

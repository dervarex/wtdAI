Exact is a Dutch software company. The main services include accounting ERP and other software for small and medium enterprises. in it was serving more than companies. The main office is in Delft and others are in Europe North America and Asia. Exact was founded in by freelancers Eduard Hagens Rinus Dekker Arco van Nieuwland Paul van Keep Paul Frijling and Leo Schonk. In the company opened offices in the United Kingdom and Russia. During next few years Exact expanded in the international level by acquiring several companies Belgian firms Cobul and Cubic Officient Unit Bedrijfssoftware German firms Pcas Bavaria Soft Szymaniak Soft Research US firms JobBOSS and MAX s Longview Solutions French firm Di se Finance Dutch firms Reeleezee Parentix ProQuro and SRXP In a group of investors led by Apax bought the company. In it was acquired by investor KKR.

Helen Dixon is currently a Commissioner at the Irish Communications Regulator Comreg and was previously the Irish Commissioner for data protection. Dixon was appointed as head of the Irish Data Protection Commission in September . Before that from November to she was Registrar of Companies within the Ministry of Trade Industry and Innovation where she had been working since . Dixon has been educated at the University of Ulster Applied languages French and English University College Dublin master in European economics and public affairs 'Law Society of Ireland' 'Griffith College Dublin' postgraduate computer sciences and Queen's University Belfast master in government affairs and law .

Muslim Roma are Romani people of Muslim religious affiliation. Muslim Roma generally trace their faith to ancestors who converted to Islam at the time of the Sultanate of Rum in Anatolia or the Ottoman Empire in the Balkans to not pay Jizya. They are often called Xoraxane pronunciation Khorakhane in the Romani language an umbrella term for Muslim or Turkified Romani people or commonly known as Turkish Gypsies Turkoyiftoi T rk ingene . The etymology of the term Xoroxane is unclear although it is theorised to be etymologically linked to the Kara Khanid Khanate. It is used in Romani to refer to Romani people with a Muslim or Turkified lifestyle regardless of which country they live in which subgroup they belong to and even if they are Muslim or speak the Turkish language or not. The biggest group are the Romanlar in Turkey. In Bulgaria Greece Northern Cyprus Turkey and Romania Dobruja many Xoraxane deny their Romani Background speak only Turkish and have a strong Turkish Identity trough Turkification and Cultural assimilation in Turkish society and declare themselves as Turkish people only but some speak Xoraxane Romani dialect together like in Greek Thrace. Muslim Roma may have little in common with Christian Roma especially the Turkish speaking Muslim Roma .

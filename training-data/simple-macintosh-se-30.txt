The Macintosh SE is a personal computer made and sold by Apple Computer between January and October . It is the fastest model in the original black and white compact Macintosh series. The SE has a black and white screen and one Processor Direct Slot PDS for upgrades like faster processors network cards or display adapters. It could use up to MB of RAM which was a lot at that time. It usually came with a MB or MB hard drive. It was also the first small Mac to come with a . MB high density floppy disk drive later versions of the SE had one too but early ones did not . The SE was powerful enough to make This Week the first color tabloid newspaper in the UK to use digital pre press technology on a personal computer. Apple offered an upgrade for US that turned a regular Macintosh SE into an SE . This upgrade changed the logic board and added a new front bezel to match the SE .

DNA is the debut studio album by English girl group Little Mix. It was released on November in the United Kingdom via Syco Music. Musically DNA is a pop dance pop and R B album with elements of pop rock hip hop and funk. Some of the songs on the album were also co written by members of other girl groups including Nicola Roberts of Girls Aloud Shaznay Lewis of All Saints and T Boz of TLC. Upon its release DNA received positive reviews from music critics.

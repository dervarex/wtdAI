The Wikipedia Revolution How A Bunch of Nobodies Created The World's Greatest Encyclopedia is a book by Andrew Lih. It is a book about the history of the internet encyclopedia Wikipedia. It covers the period from the start of Wikipedia in early to early . It has biographies of Jimmy Wales Larry Sanger and Ward Cunningham. Wales and Sanger started Wikipedia while Cunningham started the first online wiki. It also talks about events in Wikipedia's history such as the Essjay controversy.

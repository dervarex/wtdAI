Crash is Charli XCX's fifth studio album. The album contains singles like Good Ones New Shapes featuring Christine and the Queens and Caroline Polachek Beg for You featuring Rina Sawayama Baby Every Rule and Used to Know Me . Since the album's release Crash has been named by several magazines as one of the best albums of .

It was founded by the largest media company in Mexico now known as Grupo Televisa as a means to spread Spanish programming in the United States. Univision has grown to be the largest Spanish language media outlet in the U.S. Its daily news program Noticiero Univisi n co anchored by Jorge Ramos and Mar a Elena Salinas has in several occasions outdone in number of ratings major English language network evening news programming such as CBS NBC and ABC's World News Tonight. Univisi n and Telemundo USA have been competing for the top spot among Hispanic viewers for many years often imitating each other's programming. Univisi n headquarters are based in Los Angeles although much of its programming is filmed in Miami.

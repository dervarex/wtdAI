Mobile Systems Ltd. commonly known as MobiSystems is a multinational software company with its main office in Sofia Bulgaria. It was started in by Stanislav Minchev and Dimitar Mitev. The company is famous for making office and productivity tools that work on many different systems including Android iOS Windows and macOS. By the company's software has been installed over million times by people in countries. MobiSystems is a top partner for companies like Sony and many of its apps come pre installed on Sony Xperia phones. MobiOffice A complete office pack formerly called OfficeSuite that lets users create and edit documents spreadsheets and presentations. MobiPDF A professional tool for reading editing and protecting PDF files formerly known as PDF Extra . Oxford Dictionary A collection of famous dictionary apps made under a special agreement with Oxford University Press. MobiDrive A cloud service where users can save and sync their files across different devices. File Commander A popular app for managing and organizing files on Android devices. MobiSystems has won many international awards for its technology. In Google named its OfficeSuite an Android Excellence app. The company has also been recognized multiple times by the Bulgarian Association of Information Technologies BAIT for having the most successful Bulgarian IT product sold worldwide.

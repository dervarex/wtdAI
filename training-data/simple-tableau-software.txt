Tableau Software is an American software company that makes tools for data visualization and business intelligence. Its software helps people turn information from databases and spreadsheets into charts maps dashboards and other visual displays. Tableau was founded in and is based in Seattle Washington. In Salesforce bought Tableau for . billion. Tableau developed from research done at Stanford University. Pat Hanrahan Chris Stolte and researcher Diane Tang worked on a data visualization system called Polaris. The company was founded in by Hanrahan Stolte and Christian Chabot. It later moved its headquarters to Seattle. In May Tableau became a public company on the New York Stock Exchange. Its initial public offering raised about million. In Tableau bought Empirical Systems an artificial intelligence company started from research at MIT. In Salesforce agreed to buy Tableau for . billion. Tableau then became part of Salesforce. Tableau software connects to databases spreadsheets cloud services and other data sources. It can use this information to make charts dashboards and maps. Its main products have included Tableau Desktop Tableau Server Tableau Public Tableau Cloud Tableau Prep Tableau Mobile Tableau Pulse Tableau Public is a free service that allows users to create and share visualizations. Tableau also provides tools for preparing data and creating maps. In December Tableau removed visualizations made from information about the United States diplomatic cables leak published by WikiLeaks. The company said it acted after pressure from United States Senator Joe Lieberman. In Tableau changed its policy for Tableau Public. The new rules created a formal complaint process and said that freedom of speech should be an important principle when reviewing content.

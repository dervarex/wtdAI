The M ori are the indigenous people of New Zealand. They were the first people to live in the islands. The Polynesian ancestors of the M ori came to New Zealand between the years and . There are many theories about where the M ori came from. They arrived from somewhere in tropical Polynesia either from the Cook Islands or the Society Islands. Before Europeans came to New Zealand the M ori did not need a name for themselves as a people. After Europeans came to New Zealand the indigenous people called themselves M ori. M ori means normal or ordinary . They called other people especially those who came from Britain P keh . Today in New Zealand English P keh is often used as a name for New Zealand Europeans.

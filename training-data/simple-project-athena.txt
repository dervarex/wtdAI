Project Athena was a joint project of MIT Digital Equipment Corporation and IBM. It was launched in and research and development continued until June eight years after it began. It is widely believed that the goals were to create a computing environment that would utilize up to workstations but in fact the goal was to improve the quality of education delivered by the Institute. The concept that a user could go to any workstation and access any files or applications without finding major differences in the user interface and service just like browsing the Internet today.

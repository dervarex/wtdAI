Radica Games Limited was an American company that makes electronic games. It was founded in . It started by making electronic souvenir games for casinos. In the late s it became known for its Bass Fishin line of games. On October Mattel Inc. announced they now own Radica. Radica still makes electronic handheld games based on casino or card games but now also makes toys board games and video game accessories.

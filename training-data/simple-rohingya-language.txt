The Rohingya language is a sub branch of the eastern Indo Aryan languages that is spoken by approximately million in Arakan which is in Burma Half a million in Makkah Jeddah and Madinah of Saudi Arabia in Karachi and Pakistan in Cox's Bazaar District of Bangladesh in Malaysia and others in Thailand UAE and India. It has many alternate names including Rohinga Rohinja Ruaingga Arakani and Arakanese. The Rohingya language is related to the Bengali language. The Rohingya language uses a lot of words from other languages like Arabic and English. This is due to history the Rohingya are Muslim and Arabic is an important language in their religion Islam and culture they interacted with the English and many other different groups . The Rohingya language has many different alphabets. People have a hard time trying to agree on a single alphabet to write in. They can write in Burmese alphabet Arabic alphabet or the Latin alphabet the ABCs . A Rohingya man named Mohammed Hanif created the Hanafi alphabet which is suitable to write the language in. However not many computers can support the Hanafi alphabet. So for the time being many Rohingya use the ABCs when they use computers and phones. Rohinya has consonant sounds and vowel sounds. Rohingya is a subject object verb language. For example I love you in English would be I you love in Rohingya.

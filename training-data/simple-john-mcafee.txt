John David McAfee September ndash June was a British and American computer programmer. He was the developer of the first commercial anti virus program. This bore the McAfee brand name for years until it was bought by Intel and given the Intel name. McAfee was born in an U.S. Army base stationed in the United Kingdom. He was raised in Virginia and Alabama. His wealth peaked at million before his investments suffered in the global crisis of . McAfee also had interests in smartphone apps yoga and all natural antibiotics. On September McAfee announced that he would seek the office of President of the United States in the presidential election as the candidate of a newly formed political party called the Cyber Party. On December he re announced his candidacy bid saying that he would instead seek the Libertarian nomination for President. On June McAfee announced his plans to run for president as a Libertarian in the presidential election. On June McAfee was found dead after committing suicide by hanging in a prison cell in Sant Esteve Sesrovires near Barcelona. Hours before his death Spain approved for McAfee to be extradited to the United States for tax crimes.

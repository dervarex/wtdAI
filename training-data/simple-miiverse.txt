Miiverse was a social network for the Wii U and the Nintendo DS family. It was made by Nintendo and Hatena. It was powered by the Nintendo Network. People could make text and handwritten posts. In some communities they made videos of games. You could use Miiverse with any web browser on PC tablets and smartphones. All users who got a Nintendo Network ID were given a Miiverse account. On August Nintendo of America said that Miiverse be shut down on November . Users could ask to get posts and screenshots they had made. Replies to posts however would not be able to be seen. It was shut down on November at pm PT. Wii U Chat and the Japanese Nintendo TVii were also discontinued at this time. On January an archive was made of every Miiverse that people could see. It had almost TB. This was called Archiverse .

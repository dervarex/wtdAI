Karen Stollznow born August is an Australian American writer podcaster public speaker and linguist. Some of her books are Bitch The Journey of a Word Missed Conceptions How We Make Sense of Infertility On the Offensive Prejudice in Language Past and Present The Language of Discrimination God Bless America Strange and Unusual Religious Beliefs and Practices in the United States Haunting America Language Myths Mysteries and Magic Hits and Mrs and Would You Believe It Mysterious Tales From People You'd Least Expect. In addition Stollznow hosts the podcast Monster Talk with Blake Smith and writes short stories such as Fisher's Ghost and Other Stories. She has contributed to numerous well known magazines such as The Conversation and Psychology Today. Additionally Stollznow has served as an expert on numerous TV programs including the History Channel's History's Greatest Mysteries and Netflix's Files of the Unexplained.

Leon Jan Wycz kowski April December was a Polish artist of the Young Poland period. Leon Wycz kowski was a versatile artist he created works using many technics painting and graphic. He was born in Huta Miastkowska near the Garwolin. In autumn he left to the Munich where he start learning in the atelier of the Hungarian painter Alexander von Wagner. In he started learning in the Academy of Fine Arts in Krak w as a student of Jan Matejko. In he come back to Warsaw when he earned his living mainly by painting portraits. In he moved to Ukraine where he spend more then years of his life. In this Ukrainian period he painted his most famous pictures presenting rural life. In October he got a job as a lecturer in the Academy of Fine Arts in Krak w. In he handed over a collection of asian art to the National Museum in Pozna . For the donation the painter received the manor house in Go cieradz near Bydgoszcz. He moved to the Go cierad where he created atelier. He died from pneumonia in in Warsaw. Gathering crowds funeral took place in Bydgoszcz. He was buried in the cemetery in Wtelno. In Franciszka Wycz kowska wife of painter handed over to the city of Bydgoszcz pictures and graphics sketchbooks personal things and atelier equipment of Leon Wycz kowski. Since then the regional museum in Bydgoszcz has been gradually expanding its collection by purchasing more works by the artist.

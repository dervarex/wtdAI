The DW Deutsche Welle until February Deutsche Welle is Germany's international broadcaster. It broadcasts news and information on shortwave Internet and satellite radio in languages DW RADIO . It has a satellite television service DW TV that is available in four languages and there is also DW.DE a language online news site. DW which in English means German Wave is similar to international broadcasters such as the BBC World Service Radio Canada International Voice of America and Radio France Internationale. On February Deutsche Welle was completely relaunched and now operates only as DW. Since October the site of Deutsche Welle can't be opened in Belarus. In March a Belarusian court recognized the Telegram channel DW Belarus and the Deutsche Welle logo as extremist materials. In April the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Belarus labeled DW Belarus as an extremist group.

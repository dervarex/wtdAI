Swift is a general purpose programming language made by Apple Inc. Originally released in Swift has been known to be used for making iOS applications. The development of Swift started in July by Chris Latner with many other software programmers at Apple. Swift was made as a replacement for another programming language called Objective C. On June the Apple Worldwide Developers Conference WWDC application became the first application written in Swift. A beta version of the app was released to the registered Apple developers. In during the WWDC Apple announced an iPad only app called Swift Playgrounds made to teach people how to code with Swift. During WWDC Apple announced SwiftUI with Xcode .

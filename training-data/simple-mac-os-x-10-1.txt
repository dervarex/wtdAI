Mac OS X . code named Puma is the second major release of macOS Apple's desktop and server operating system. It superseded Mac OS X . and preceded Mac OS X Jaguar. Mac OS X . was released on September as a free update for Mac OS X . users. The operating system was handed out for free by Apple employees after Steve Jobs' keynote speech at the Seybold publishing conference in San Francisco. It was subsequently distributed to Mac users on October at Apple Stores and other retail stores that carried Apple products. Mac OS X . was codenamed Puma because the internal team thought it was one fast cat. In January Apple switched to using Mac OS X as the default OS on all new Macs at the time starting with the . . release replacing Mac OS .

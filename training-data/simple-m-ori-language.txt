The M ori language shortened to Te Reo is the language of the M ori and an official language of New Zealand. It is an Austronesian language. Although it is an official language not many people speak it fluently. In the census about people . of the population said that they could have a conversation in M ori about a lot of everyday things. The language can be seen everywhere throughout New Zealand as many of the placenames are M ori such as Whangarei Rotorua and Timaru. Lots of government or city buildings use te reo on their signs as well as English and most of the public schools have a M ori name as well as an English one. Some M ori people living in Australia also speak the language. Te Reo M ori did not have a writing system until the Europeans arrived. In a Ng puhi chief T tori and his relative Tui worked with Professor Samuel Lee and prepared a writing system for te reo.

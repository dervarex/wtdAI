On August Turner Broadcasting System acquired Metro Goldwyn Mayer United Artists. On October Turner forcibly sold back MGM. However Turner kept much of the film and television library made before May including some of the UA library and formed Turner Entertainment Co. On October its cable channel Turner Network Television was launched and gained an audience with its extensive film library. In Turner also purchased the library of animation studio Hanna Barbera. Ted Turner selected Betty Cohen then Senior Vice President of TNT to devise a network to house these programs. On February Turner Broadcasting announced its plans to launch Cartoon Network as an outlet for an animation library. On October the network officially launched as the first hour single genre cable channel with animation as its main theme. The continuity announcers would call it The Cartoon Network until when it was simply dubbed Cartoon Network as it is today.

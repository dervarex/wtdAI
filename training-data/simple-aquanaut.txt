An aquanaut is a person who stays at the bottom of the ocean for hours or longer while exposed to the pressure of the water at that depth in the ocean. This usually happens in a special building called an underwater habitat on the ocean bottom. The first person to stay at the bottom of the ocean for hours was a man from France. His name was Robert St nuit. This happened in September . The United States Navy had a program in the s called SEALAB in which military aquanauts and scientists spent time on the ocean bottom. This program ended in February after aquanaut Berry L. Cannon died from breathing too much carbon dioxide. NASA carries out programs in the Aquarius underwater habitat off Key Largo in Florida. These programs are called NASA Extreme Environment Mission Operations NEEMO . The NEEMO programs help teach astronauts how to live and work in outer space.

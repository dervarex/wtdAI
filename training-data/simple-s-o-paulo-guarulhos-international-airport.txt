S o Paulo Guarulhos International Airport is an airport that serves S o Paulo Brazil located nbsp km north of the city centre. It is sometimes called Cumbica Airport after the suburb in which it is located. Its IATA code is GRU and ICAO code is SBGR. The airport is the busiest in Brazil in terms of transported passengers in aircraft operations and cargo handled. Line Jade of the S o Paulo Metro Rail Transport Network connects with the airport through Aeroporto Guarulhos Station. A single fare provides access to all lines in the S o Paulo Metro system. This line started its operations on March . That makes S o Paulo's S o Paulo Guarulhos International Airport GRU the first among major South American airport hubs such as Buenos Aires Ezeiza Santiago de Chile Lima El Dorado International Airport in Bogot and Rio de Janeiro Gale o International Airport to have a direct railway connection. Aeroporto Guarulhos station is located near Terminal and passengers can access Terminals and via a free shuttle service.

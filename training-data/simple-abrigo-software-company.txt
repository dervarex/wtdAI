Abrigo is an American financial software company that provides software and services for banks and credit unions. Its products are used for lending risk management regulatory compliance and preventing financial crime. The company serves about financial institutions. Abrigo was created from several financial technology businesses including Sageworks and Banker's Toolbox. Sageworks was founded in Raleigh North Carolina in by Brian Hamilton and computer programmer Sarah Tourville. Hamilton and Tourville developed software that could turn financial information into written reports. Their technology became the basis of the ProfitCents product. Citibank and Intuit were among the company's early users. In Sageworks licensed its ProfitCents technology to LexisNexis. The company later developed software for banks credit unions lenders and financial managers. In it launched a database containing information about thousands of American banks and credit unions. In private equity company Accel KKR bought Sageworks. In Sageworks and Banker's Toolbox were combined under the name Abrigo. Abrigo bought Farin Financial Risk Management in . In investment funds managed by Carlyle invested in the company. The company continued to grow through acquisitions. In it bought Valuant and two construction lending products from BankLabs. It later bought DiCOM Software TPG Software Integrated Financial Solutions and Journey Technology Solutions. Abrigo makes software for banks and credit unions. Its products are used for lending and loan management financial risk management regulatory compliance fraud and financial crime prevention portfolio management financial analysis The company provides both software and advisory services. Abrigo also uses cloud computing artificial intelligence and financial data to help financial institutions monitor risk and meet regulatory requirements. Financial information collected by Sageworks has been used by researchers studying private companies. This data has been useful because private companies normally publish less financial information than public companies. Sageworks also published information about private company sales profits and industry performance. Its data has been cited by publications including The Wall Street Journal and the Chicago Tribune.

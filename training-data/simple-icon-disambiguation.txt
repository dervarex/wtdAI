An icon from the Greek word for image is a picture of something holy or divine. Icons in this sense are in use mainly in Eastern Christianity. Look in the article Icon for this meaning. Iconography is the study of all types of images such as their history and meaning. This is mostly used in art history. The word can also mean symbolism. In other use an icon is a Pictogram for an object facility control political party government or something like this. See Icon secular .

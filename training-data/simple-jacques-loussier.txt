Jacques Loussier October ndash March was a French pianist and composer. He was well known for his jazz works in trio formation of many of Johann Sebastian Bach's works such as in the Goldberg Variations. He was born in Angers France. Loussier worked in movies and television such as Heaven on One's Head Living It Up The Killing Game Dark of the Sun Monique and The Man Who Went Up in Smoke . He was known for creating the theme tune for the popular s French TV series Thierry La Fronde. In March Loussier filed a million lawsuit against rapper Eminem and The Marshall Mathers LP executive producer Dr. Dre claiming that the beat for the track Kill You was stolen from his composition Pulsion . The case was settled out of court. Loussier died on March at the age of .

DZET TV channel is a television station in North Central Luzon Philippines airing programming from the TV network. It is owned and operated by the network's namesake corporate parent TV alongside One Sports outlet DZYB TV channel a sister network Nation Broadcasting Corporation also provides certain services to RPTV flagship DZBS TV channel under an airtime lease agreement with station owners Radio Philippines Network the station maintains studios at the TV Broadcast Center Camp Monticello Road Baguio. DZET TV hybrid analog digital transmitting facilities at are located atop Mount Santo Tomas and the Digital SFN relay transmitting towers is located at Luz Street Cabanatuan Nueva Ecija. July the station was opened by the Associated Broadcasting Company along with the callsign as DZET TV and switched its frequency from VHF Channel to UHF Channel . December ABC TV stations acquired a new franchise to operate under Republic Act signed by President Fidel V. Ramos FVR . In the same year the station went on nationwide satellite broadcasting. In a phenomenal growth ABC Baguio earned its reputation as The Fastest Growing Network under new network executive Tina Monzon Palma who served as Chief Operating Officer.

University of Buenos Aires UBA is an Argentine public national university based in the city of Buenos Aires. It was founded on August by the governor of the province of Buenos Aires Mart n Rodr guez and his government minister Bernardino Rivadavia. The UBA is the largest university in Argentina and is considered to be one of the most prestigious study centers in America and the world.

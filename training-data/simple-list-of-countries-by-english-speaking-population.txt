This is an article about countries which have English as their main language. It is also about how the language is spread throughout the world. English a West Germanic language started in England over one thousand years ago after some peoples from the north of Germany moved over to live there. It is the most widely used international language worldwide and a first language in countries across the Western Hemisphere and Oceania. It is one of the official languages of the UN. It is usually split according to how it is spoken and written namely American English Australian English British English Irish English and Canadian English. Those splits are called dialects they have cultural significance within their own respective countries because of their history and the way they sound. There is also Filipino English which is used in the Philippines and sounds reads like the American dialect.

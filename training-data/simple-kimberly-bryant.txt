Kimberly Bryant born January is the founder of Black Girls Code BGC . Black Girls Code is a nonprofit organization teaching girls to write code for computers. It also teaches girls to be leaders. Bryant grew up in Memphis Tennessee USA. Bryant wanted to work as a lawyer until she discovered computer science. Her brother taught her about computer science. Years later she noticed her daughter loved to play video games. She enrolled her daughter in a computer science camp at Stanford. Her daughter was very upset from the camp. The camp was all boys so her daughter felt alone. Bryant wanted to change girls' experience with learning about computers. Bryant helps women of color to be more involved in STEM. Black Girls Code started in a basement in San Francisco. Now Black Girls Code teaches girls and uses volunteers. They have chapters in the U.S. as well as one in South Africa.

MBC is a free to air Saudi Arabian general entertainment television channel. It is owned by the media conglomerate MBC Group. MBC Group was launched in the early s with the start of broadcasting on MBC now known as MBC as the first free to air satellite television channel owned by the private sector broadcasting . The channel offered a mix of entertainment and news programming which helped it gain a wide audience throughout the Arab world. MBC offers a diverse range of Arabic series and programs. On the religious front the channel broadcasts numerous Islamic reports during religious occasions and offers religious and educational programs during the holy month of Ramadan in addition to charitable programs such as Qalby Etmi'nan The channel also provides comprehensive coverage of the Day of Arafah one of the most important religious events for Muslims with the participation of dozens of correspondents. The channel has ventured into local production with the production and broadcast of Driven a program specializing in the world of cars racing speed and car modification which has aired for four seasons. Alongside Driven which is dubbed into Arabic with some scenes censored. The channel was among the first Arabic channels to broadcast the latest foreign movies dubbed into Arabic offering a selection of American and Indian movies between October and August . Today the channel continues to dub popular Bollywood movies with its inaugural movie being Jab Tak Hai Jaan starring Katrina Kaif Anushka Sharma and Shah Rukh Khan. Among the channel's most prominent presenters is Mustafa Al Agha.

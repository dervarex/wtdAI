On May the creator launched .dev as the new version of .ai. Speech synthesis means making computers talk like humans. For many years computers did not sound natural when they talked. The old way to make computer speech was called concatenative synthesis. This method worked by cutting up recordings of real human voices into small pieces. The computer would then put these pieces together to make new sentences. This often made the speech sound robotic. In a company called DeepMind made a new system called WaveNet. WaveNet used a type of AI called a neural network to make speech. Neural networks are computer programs that learn patterns from data. WaveNet made much better speech than the old method. In Google made an AI system called Tacotron . Tacotron could make very natural sounding speech. However it needed a lot of data to work well. It needed about hours of audio recordings to make good speech. With only minutes of audio it could not make speech that people could understand. Other systems also improved speech synthesis around this time such as HiFi GAN and Glow TTS. However all of these systems needed many hours of audio recordings.

David Axel Cazares Casanova born October better known as Sr Pelo Which translates to Mr. Hair is a Mexican internet personality YouTuber animator illustrator and voice actor. He is best known for creating the online series Spooky Month and Mokey's Show an absurd parody of Disney's Mickey Mouse. He has created various viral parodies of cartoons and video games such as Team Fortress Super Mario and Undertale notable for their satire and black comedy. As of late February his channel has . million subscribers and . billion total views. His YouTube channel was originally named pendejos before changing to Sr Pelo based on a nickname given to him by friends. One of his videos titled Every StoryTime Animation parodying the new age wave of storytime animators in the mid to late s was seen by YouTuber Arin Hanson who deemed the video as mean and harmful . In Casanova's characters Skid and Pump the two main protagonists of Spooky Month were featured as guest characters in the indie rhythm game Friday Night Funkin for their notable association with the website Newgrounds. He has done a live stream of the demo for the upcoming game Rolus in the Outskirts. On November th Casanova was invited to join an online event with creators such as Vivziepop creator of Hazbin Hotel and Helluva Boss and Gooseworx creator of The Amazing Digital Circus.

Prime ja Online is an online newspaper based in Edo State Nigeria providing news and analysis on politics business entertainment sports and more. Founded in by Chima Joseph Ugo its aim is to provide correct and reliable news and information to Nigerians and Africans. Prime ja Online is an online news and media website founded by Chima Joseph Ugo in with its headquarters in Okada Edo Nigeria. In June Stamp Nigeria ranked Prime ja Online among the fastest growing newspaper startups in Nigeria. In March they launched a news section in Pidgin language. In December Media Bias Fact Check rated Prime ja Online as the least biased and credible online newspaper in Nigeria. So far Prime ja Online is the only newspaper to receive this rating in Nigeria. In addition to their standard English version Prime ja Online launched a Pidgin version of their website Prime ja Online Pidgin. The aim is to reach out to more Nigerians who may understand Pidgin English better than Standard English. This move ensures that they bring the news to the grassroots and give more people access to correct and reliable news and information.

Yubico AB is a Swedish company that makes authentication hardware. It is best known for producing the YubiKey a hardware security token for two factor authentication and passwordless login. Its headquarters are in Stockholm Sweden. Yubico also has operations in Santa Clara California United States. The company was founded in by Jakob Ehrensv rd and Stina Ehrensv rd. In November of that year the company began offering a pilot box for software developers. The first YubiKey was presented at the annual RSA Conference in April . The company was listed on the Nasdaq Stockholm stock exchange in September . In Mattias Danielsson succeeded Stina Ehrensv rd as chief executive officer. After his resignation in Jerrod Chong became acting chief executive officer and was later confirmed in the role. Yubico is member of the FIDO Alliance. In Yubico gave free YubiKeys to new readers of Wired and Ars Technica as part of promotional campaigns. The company also supported activists during the Hong Kong protests. Yubico donated security keys to help activists Hong Kong protests secure their online accounts.

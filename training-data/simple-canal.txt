Canal is a Argentine cable television channel. It shows programs about art culture and entertainment. The channel is owned by Clar n Group and operated by its media company Artear. People can watch the channel across Latin America. The channel started broadcasting on April . It was the first television channel in Latin America to show culture and art programs hours a day. It replaced a temporary channel named Cultura Espect culos. A company named Pramer first created the channel. Over the years the channel changed owners multiple times. Companies like Liberty Global and AMC Networks owned it in the past. In late Clar n Group bought the channel. In Canal received a Special Mention award from the Konex Foundation for its good work in communications and journalism in Argentina. Canal a shows many types of cultural television programs. These include Documentaries about artists writers and musicians Shows about theater movies architecture and books Interviews with famous people from the art world Programs about independent music and emerging bands

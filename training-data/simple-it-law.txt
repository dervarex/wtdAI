Many countries have created legislation or have passed laws about the use of computers and data. These laws are usually concerned Protecting data about people Hacking computer systems Protecting copyright and patents. Censorship of the internet Health and safety Privacy Act concerns data about people Copyright Act Copyright Amendment Digital Agenda Act Bundesdatenschutzgesetz BDSG Federal Data Protection Act concerns data about people c anti hacking legislation FISMA Federal Information Security Management Act describes how government organisations should handle data about people GLBA Gramm Leach Bliley Act describes how financial organisations should handle data about people HIPPA Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act describes how medical insurance organisations should handle data about people Data Security Act Digital Millennium Copyright Act USA PATRIOT Act Identity Theft Enforcement and Restitution Act Computer Fraud and Abuse Act The Data Protection Act will prevent people's information getting spread around the world preventing the likeliness of crimes toward said person. The Computer Misuse Act covers hacking with amendments that were added later. The Copyright Designs and Patents Act covers copyright. The Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act covers central and local government looking at workers' email security etc. The Communications Act is often used when people send offensive emails etc. The Digital Economy Act covers software piracy. The Malicious Communication Act Covers sending mail physical or electronic with the intent of causing distress or anxiety.

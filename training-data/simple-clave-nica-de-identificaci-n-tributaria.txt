The Unique Tax Identification Code CUIT is a code used in the tax system of Argentina to correctly identify the individuals or autonomous legal entities who are liable to pay taxes or who must be identified in some way by the control entities. The code are assigned by the Customs Collection and Control Agency ARCA to compile the registry or census for administrative and tax purposes. The CUIT was created in when the general director of the General Tax Directorate was Carlos Marcelo Da Corte during the presidency of Ra l Alfons n by virtue of the provisions of Law . . Its use became mandatory for all national government agencies in August . It consists of a total of eleven digits two initial digits indicating the overall rate followed by eight digits corresponding in the case of individuals to the National Identity Document number and in the case of companies to a company number assigned by the ARCA and finally a verification digit. For those employed by employees the same code is called the Unique Labor Identification Code CUIL . Those who need to carry out transactions that involve paying taxes such as various types of registered sales but who are not classified as employees or self employed workers can also obtain an Identification Code CDI from the same agency. The CUIT for individuals is personal data as it links a person to their tax identification number. However according to Argentine legislation on personal data specifically Article of Law . on Personal Data Protection CUIT is one of the personal data that does not require the consent of its owner for processing. At the same time the CUIT for taxpayers is publicly accessible data which is in fact published by the ARCA through the Registry of Individuals and Legal Entities. The CUIT is obtained from the ARCA. There is a separate procedure for individuals and another for legal entities which are described on the official website of the aforementioned collection agency. The procedure must be completed at the ARCA Zonal Office corresponding to the address of the applicant.

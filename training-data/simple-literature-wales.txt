This society promotes Welsh language and English language literature in Wales. Literature Wales has bursaries for writing projects and they run literature events and lectures. They also give money to support creative mentoring. Literature Wales selects the National Poet for Wales. They also run other competitions. They run the Wales Book of the Year the Cardiff International Poetry Competition and the Rhys Davies Short Story Competition. The Academi was formed as a Welsh language society in . It was created by Robert Maynard Jones and the poet Waldo Williams. Many important Welsh writers and academics joined the society. Their first chairmen were Iorwerth Peate T. J. Morgan and J. E.

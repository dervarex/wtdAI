Amarok is a music player for the KDE desktop environment. Unlike most KDE programs it has its own schedule for releasing versions so a new version of Amarok might not be released when a new version of KDE comes out. Amarok's first stable version was released in . They are now on version . . . Amarok cannot play videos. However it can play most sound and music files in a large range of formats like MP Ogg Vorbis and WMA. Amarok also includes an internet radio tuner and supports submitting a listener's music preferences to Last.fm. It can sync music to most MP players like the iPod or ZEN. Amarok was updated to version along with the major upgrade of KDE to KDE . The newer KDE version of Amarok is still missing some features when compared with the previous KDE version but it does have some new features not included with the earlier one. Many users of KDE Amarok believed it was the best software music player of any platform Windows Mac Linux and it had a great and loyal following. This loyalty has faded somewhat since the KDE release though Amarok developers are still working hard to make certain the newer Amarok meets and exceeds all users' expectations.

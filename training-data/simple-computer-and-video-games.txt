Computer and Video Games also known as CVG was a UK monthly video game magazine and website. It ran from November until October . After the magazine was entirely moved online to their website. Their website ran from August until it was shut down on February . The magazine was produced in London England. The first issue released in January spotlighted Space Invaders. The last issue was in October with the main feature being a review of Need for Speed Underground . Before being bought out by GamesRadar CVG used to host the Golden Joystick Awards the longest running gaming awards. They started the awards in and it still runs today. On April the magazine was revived under the name CVG Presents. It was released bi weekly and would mainly focus on one subject. The spotlight of the first edition was on the Grand Theft Auto series.

Nokia E is a mobile phone model made by Nokia. It complements Nokia's higher end N which does not have a QWERTY keyboard. The phone has a inch diagonally measured AMOLED screen. The phone can connect to the internet using its built in G EDGE or WiFi radios. It can connect to other devices such as computers or other phones with its Bluetooth radio. The phone also contains a GPS receiver which uses Nokia's Ovi maps software for street directions. The service is free for the E owners. Maps can be obtained through Nokia's Ovi market. The phone has a non autofocus camera that can take megapixel photos or video in up to p at fps. The phone has no place for a memory card. The phone is pre loaded with an email client a calendar an office suite with support for Microsoft Word Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint a PDF viewer and other common productivity applications. The E uses the Symbian operating system.

Friday Plans Inc. is an American online health care company. It offers prescription medicine for erectile dysfunction through the internet. The company sells generic Viagra sildenafil and generic Cialis tadalafil which contain the same active ingredients and dosages as the branded versions. Friday Plans Inc. was founded in January . The company began operating in most parts of the United States using a telemedicine model where patients can get a prescription without visiting a doctor in person. By October the company reported having more than active patients in the United States. Patients start by filling out an online medical form about their health and symptoms. The answers are reviewed by a licensed doctor or nurse practitioner. If the medicine is safe and appropriate for the patient the prescriber sends a prescription to the company's partner pharmacy. The pharmacy dispenses the medicine and sends it by mail usually within two to three business days. Friday Plans says it holds telemedicine and pharmacy licenses in U.S. states and only serves patients in states where it is licensed to operate. Medicines are manufactured by Nivagen Pharmaceuticals and dispensed by Eagle Pharmacy a subsidiary of KnipperRx. Each shipment includes written instructions safety warnings and contact details for a pharmacist. The company uses encryption on its website to protect patient data and follows health information privacy standards. Friday Plans has been covered by news outlets including USA Today the Chicago Tribune and the International Business Times. Coverage has focused on its pricing model licensing and the convenience of online prescribing.

The Federal Detention Center FDC in Miami Florida is an administrative security federal prison. It is operated by the Federal Bureau of Prisons BOP . Its address is NE th Street in downtown Miami. The prison mainly has male and female inmates waiting for trial or sentencing. It also has inmates who are serving short sentences. Security Level Administrative accommodating inmates of all security levels. Management Operated by the BOP a division of the U.S. Department of Justice. Educational Programs Mandatory literacy GED and English as a Second Language ESL courses. High school diplomas and post secondary degrees are available through paid correspondence programs. Health Services Comprehensive medical care including sick calls dental services and emergency treatment. Psychology Services Crisis intervention individual counseling and specialized programs addressing mental health needs. Recreation Various recreational activities including organized sports wellness programs and hobby crafts. Visiting Hours Visitation schedules vary based on the inmate's housing unit. Generally visitation is available on weekends and federal holidays between a.m. and p.m. Procedures Visitors must be approved and are subject to security protocols including dress codes and identification requirements.

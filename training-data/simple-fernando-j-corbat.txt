Fernando Jos Corby Corbat July ndash July was an American computer scientist. He was known as a pioneer in the development of time sharing operating systems and for creating the password system for computers. Among many awards Corbat received the Turing Award in for his pioneering work in organizing the concepts and leading the development of the general purpose large scale time sharing and resource sharing computer systems . In he was made a Fellow of the Computer History Museum for his pioneering work on timesharing and the Multics operating system .

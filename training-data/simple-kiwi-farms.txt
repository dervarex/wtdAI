Kiwi Farms formerly known as CWCki Forums is an American internet forum for discussion about and harassment of online people and communities perceived as eccentric. The site has been part of many controversies like hosting the video of the Christchurch mosque shootings and for being linked to three suicides. The site launched with the name CWCki Forums in as a place to troll and harass Christine Weston Chandler known as Chris Chan a webcomic artist first noticed in on the Something Awful forums. The name was based off her initials. The site's new name Kiwi Farms was coined in . It now hosts threads targeting many individuals including minorities women LGBT people neurodivergent people people considered by Kiwi Farms users to be mentally ill or sexually deviant feminists journalists internet celebrities and video game or comics hobbyists. Targets are referred to as lolcows a reference to them being milked for laughs. In Cloudflare removed it from their DDoS protection due to an online campaign started by one of the site's victims. The site came back online after it switched to other DDoS protection providers though various internet service providers blocked access to it. After switching to another internet service provider it has been able to come back online.

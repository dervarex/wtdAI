ESPN is an American sports television network owned by ESPN Inc. a joint venture between The Walt Disney Company which owns Hearst Corporation which owns and the National football League which owns . ESPN is an acronym for Entertainment and Sports Programming Network. The channel is headquartered in Bristol Connecticut with other studios in Los Angeles and New York City. ESPN shows sporting events and games. It also has programs for news and highlights of sporting events as well as talk shows on sports topics. The network has also shown games or events other than sports such as poker. In ESPN launched ESPN as an additional streaming service subscription that did not require cable TV. In ESPN bought the rights to show games from the German Bundesliga as part of a six year deal beginning in with many games appearing on ESPN . In August ESPN launched the ESPN Unlimited streaming service making all of its content available in a streaming subscription that did not require cable TV. The new service is also included in many cable TV packages and as a bundle with FOX One. ESPN was renamed ESPN Select and continued to be available with more limited content. In December ESPN shut down its digital extension channel ESPN that was available as part of some TV packages.

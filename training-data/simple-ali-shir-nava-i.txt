. Herat c. . Bodleian Library Ali Shir Nava'i February January also known as Niz m al Din Ali Shir Heraw Chagatai was a poet writer official linguist Hanafi Maturidi mystic and painter. He was from the Timurid Empire. He is thought to be one of the best Chagatai literature writers. Nava'i thought that his native Chagatai Turkic language was better than Persian. He defended his opinion in his work called Muhakamat al Lughatayn The Comparison of the Two Languages . He made lots of Chagatai language poetry. Nava'i is thought to be the founder of early Turkic literature. Many places in Central Asia are named after him. In Uzbekistan the province and city of Navoiy are named after him.

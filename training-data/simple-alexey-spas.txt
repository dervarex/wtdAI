Alexey Spas born November is a business executive and software engineer. He is the co founder and chief executive officer of Instinctools a software development company. Spas was born on November in Hrodna then part of the Byelorussian Soviet Socialist Republic. In he graduated from the Yanka Kupala State University of Grodno Faculty of Physics and Engineering. In he received a Master of Business Administration from Pforzheim University specialising in international management. While still a student Spas began working as a software developer. In he co founded Instinctools together with Diethard Sohn as a software development company. The company which operates under the stylised name Instinctools grew from a local startup into an international technology firm. In Spas co founded and developed DITA webtop later branded as DITAworks Webtop a software as a service platform for enterprise technical documentation management. In November Spas presented on Eclipse based product development at the DISCOVER IT Belarus seminar held at the Maritim Hotel in Munich. In September Spas represented Instinctools at the Forrester Technology Innovation Summit in Austin Texas where the company participated as a Silver Sponsor. Together with Chad West Managing Director at Instinctools USA he introduced an AI powered conversational chatbot designed for enterprise business communication. Instinctools has organised an annual Christmas charity initiative in collaboration with its clients which has been covered in the company's news publications. In Spas joined the Forbes Technology Council a professional community for senior technology executives.

Vegas Pro is a non linear video editing software developed by Boris previously by MAGIX Sony Creative Software and Sonic Foundry. The Film Paranormal Activity was edited using the software and has won many awards. The software is only for Windows. Vegas Pro contains a lot of video and audio effects and transitions including the Mirror effect and Chroma Key. Vegas Pro also has titles and other video generators. Vegas Pro also allows users to crop flip horizontally or upside down change the speed of video and audio with or without the pitch changing if going faster or slower and more.

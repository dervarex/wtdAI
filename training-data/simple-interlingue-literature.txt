Interlingue literature means books and texts written in Interlingue also called Occidental a language made in by Edgar de Wahl. It includes both original works and translations. Most often works appeared in the magazine Cosmoglotta Interlingue Occidental began in . Edgar de Wahl created it with words mostly from Western Europe and a bit of English. The language first appeared in the magazine Kosmoglott later renamed Cosmoglotta. Early writers used different spellings and grammar because standard rules came later. Jakob Linzbach an Estonian linguist wrote Transcendent algebra mathematical ideography experiment of a philosophical language Interlingue Transcendent algebra ideografie matematical experiment de un lingue filosofic later translated into Interlingue by de Wahl. This was before the language was fully finished. In Kaarle Saarinen translated a Finnish poem Hymn into Interlingue. Soon after poems by Goethe and works by Rabindranath Tagore were translated and published. Many translated works appeared poems by Balmont stories by Edgar Allan Poe and others. Original works were also written like the poetry collection Li Astres del Verne by Jaroslav Podobsk and the short story Krasina by Jan Amos Kaj . In Ric Berger published Historiettes in Occidental a collection of short stories. In the language Interlingua was introduced. Many Interlingue supporters including Berger switched to Interlingua. This caused Interlingue to decline. Cosmoglotta continued until but less often. After that some called Interlingue a dead language. Yet translations kept coming into the s and s. In a Yahoo group helped revive Interlingue. In the s an Interlingue Wikipedia was created. By it had over articles. New literature appeared In Robert Winter translated The Charterhouse of Parma into Interlingue. In Thomas Schmidt published Li Munde de Sandra in the online magazine Posta Mundi. Between and Vicente Costalago published several works both original and translated being the most prolific writer in the language's history Li sercha in li castelle Dewahl Li tresor de Fluvglant Subuqti and poetry and collections from European literature. Dorlota Burdon also published original stories in Posta Mundi and she translated The Lunatic by Khalil Gibran. Important authors in Interlingue literature include Jan Amos Kaj Jaroslav Podobsk Engelbert Pigal Abram Kofman A. E. Cortinas Ilmari Federn Thomas Schmidt Vicente Costalago Dorlota Burdon

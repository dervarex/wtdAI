The Cyrillic alphabets are based on the Cyrillic script. The early Cyrillic alphabet was developed in the th century and replaced the previous Glagolitic script created by Saints Cyril and Methodius. It's used in the alphabets of many different languages both Slavic and non Slavic. As of around million people use it as the official alphabet for their national languages with half of them being in Russia. Cyrillic is one of the most used writing systems in the world. Most of these languages have phonemic spelling but there are some very special cases. For example in Russian the letter is pronounced v in some words like in yego which is pronounced j vo instead of j o . Unlike the Latin script which adds marks to letters in order to change languages the Cyrillic alphabet often creates new letter shapes instead.

The Amiga was a line of personal computers that Commodore International produced during the s and early s. The first Amiga computer the Amiga possessed a HAM mode enabled the machine to display colors a chip that enabled it to play back four channels of sound at once an operating system with multitasking and a graphical user interface at a time that these features were uncommon or not as developed as Amiga's. The Amiga is often said to have been ahead of its time. Although the Amiga did not sell well in America and Commodore went bankrupt the Amiga developed a fandom and niche in Europe amongst video and graphics enthusiasts and gamers. The Amiga was initially created by computer chip designer Jay Miner and Hi Toro later Amiga Corporation . In Commodore purchased Amiga Corporation. The first Amiga the Amiga was released in after delays and marketing blunders. The second model the Amiga was released in and was the best selling of the Amiga computers. In Commodore declared bankruptcy. After the bankruptcy the Amiga was purchased by german company ESCOM.

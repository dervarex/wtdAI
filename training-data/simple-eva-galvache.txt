Eva Galvache October Cartagena June Pozuelo de Alarc n Madrid was a Spanish radio journalist. She studied at the Universidad CEU San Pablo. In she started at the Cadena COPE network with Faustino Catalina directed by Jos Luis Gago in the religious news program Iglesia noticia. The program had the collaboration of Paloma G mez Borrero and Antonio Pelayo She also hosted the programs Letra y m sica and El espejo de la familia. Through this family radio program related to family and the defense of life. Specialized in religious information she took charge of the news of the COPE network for thirty years being the first secular woman to hold the position of religion editor in the chain. She attended as a special envoy to cover the most important ecclesiastical events worldwide such as World Youth Days various synods conclaves and various canonizations. She died on June in Pozuelo de Alarc n after suffering from breast cancer.

Brewster Kahle born October is an American digital librarian and computer engineer. He is known for creating the Internet Archive and co founding Alexa Internet. Brewster Kahle was born in New York City. He went to Scarsdale High School. He studied at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology MIT and graduated in with a degree in computer science and engineering. After college Kahle worked at Thinking Machines where he helped create the Connection Machine. In he co founded WAIS Inc. which was later sold to AOL. In he co founded Alexa Internet which was sold to Amazon in . In Kahle started the Internet Archive. This is a non profit organization that saves web pages and other digital content. The Internet Archive has a tool called the Wayback Machine which lets people see old versions of web pages. Kahle has received many awards for his work. He was inducted into the Internet Hall of Fame in . He is also a member of the National Academy of Engineering. Brewster Kahle is married to Mary Austin and they have two children.

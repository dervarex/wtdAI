Two other passes give access on foot to Chitral the Broghol Pass and the Dorah Pass both from Afghanistan. Both are closed in winter. The general population is mainly of the Chitrali people who speak the Chitrali language or Chitrali which is also spoken in parts of Yasin Gilgit and Swat. Chitral is also home to the Kalash tribe who live in three remote valleys southwest of Chitral town.

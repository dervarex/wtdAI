Kidtoon Films was an American theatrical distribution program designed specifically to bring safe G rated and educational children's content to the big screen. Operating primarily as a weekend morning matin e showcase the program allowed families with toddlers and young children to experience the movie theater in a comfortable kid friendly environment. Founded in by Jonathan Dern as part of the digital distribution entity The Bigger Picture. Originally operated under Sabella Dern Entertainment SD Entertainment which also produced several of the program's early movies. Vetted strictly by child development experts including Dr. Donna Mitroff to guarantee age appropriate programming. Launched officially on September with its very first theatrical release Tonka Tough Truck Adventures The Biggest Show on Wheels Focused on short theatrical windows utilizing Saturday and Sunday morning time slots. Formed heavy partnerships with major theater chains most notably National Amusements Showcase Cinemas as well as Regal Cinemas Carmike Cinemas and Rave Cinemas. Provided an affordable pipeline for international and independent animation studios to get North American theatrical exposure before transitioning to home video. Grew from screening in just seven initial theaters to a digital satellite distribution network spanning over theaters across states by the late s. Corporate Acquisitions and PBS Kids Partnership Acquisition The Bigger Picture and Kidtoon Films were acquired by AccessIT which later integrated Kidtoon into its expanding digital cinema initiatives. PBS Kids Deal Forged a major long term relationship with PBS Kids. Brought popular television properties like Sid the Science Kid Martha Speaks Curious George and WordGirl to the big screen alongside music videos from artists like They Might Be Giants. The program updated its featured movie every single month. Some of its most prominent theatrical releases included Spookley the Square Pumpkin screened annually every October from to Candy Land The Great Lollipop Adventure Little Nemo Adventures in Slumberland theatrical re release in My Little Pony animated specials including A Very Minty Christmas and The Princess Promenade Tom and Jerry The Fast and the Furry Scooby Doo in Where's My Mummy Various Barbie Thomas Friends and Strawberry Shortcake features The Hiatus Parent company AccessIT merged into Cinedigm Digital Cinema Corp. Following a corporate shift and an acquisition by New Kideo in January Kidtoon Films entered a four year hiatus. Brief Revival The program made a limited comeback in February across Showcase Cinemas locations kicking off with the movie Barbie Spy Squad. Current Status The program gradually waned and phased out of theaters as Cineverse formerly Cinedigm pivoted heavily toward streaming media

Yuriy Vasilyevich Abramochkin normally rendered in English as Yuriy or Yuri Abramochkin December April was a Soviet and Russian photographer and photojournalist. Abramochkin worked with Soviet and world leaders politicians and celebrities making among others the photos of Nikita Khrushchev Leonid Brezhnev Mikhail Gorbachev Boris Yeltsin Charles de Gaulle Willy Brandt Francois Mitterrand Richard Nixon Urho Kekkonen Jacques Chirac Bill Clinton Yuri Gagarin Ronald Reagan Valentina Tereshkova and Elizabeth II.

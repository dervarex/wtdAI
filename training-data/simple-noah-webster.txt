Noah Webster Jr. October ndash May was the author of a famous American dictionary a pioneer of spelling and a political writer and editor. In the s he was the editor of a Federalist Party newspaper. His Blue backed Speller books taught five generations of American children how to spell and read. He made enough money from it to write his dictionary first published in as An American Dictionary of the English Language. Webster worried that his Grammatical Institute might be pirated. He had to seek copyright protection from each state. At last on May President George Washington signed the United States' first general copyright act into law. Later Webster lobbied for an extended copyright law. He wrote By this bill the term of copy right is secured for years with the right of renewal for years more. If this should become law I shall be much benefited.

D.Gray man D Gureiman is a manga series by Katsura Hoshino. It is a Gothic fiction manga first serialized in in Weekly Sh nen Jump. It is an ongoing series and is currently up to chapters. There is also a novel series called Reverse that tells the characters' backgrounds. As of May VIZ Media has released the first five volumes in the United States. An anime version began airing on October in Japan but has yet to be seen in North America. Many characters and their designs were from some of Katsura Hoshino's previous works and drafts such as Zone Book man and Continue and her assistants. Earlier titles considered for the manga were Dolls Chronoa and Zone.

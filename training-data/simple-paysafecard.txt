Paysafecard is a closed loop prepaid online payment method based on vouchers with a digit PIN code. It is independent of bank accounts credit cards or other personal information. Customers can purchase vouchers at local sales outlets such as gas stations or newsstands and pay online by entering the code at the checkout of the respective website e.g. in online games . As of PaysafeCard is issued in approximately countries covering most of Europe and North America Australia and New Zealand and some countries in Western Asia and South America. It was originally established as paysafecard.com Wertkarten AG. IBM Austria served as the technical partner providing the original hardware and expertise. In the company received EU funding under the eTEN program for electronic services. By its subsidiary Prepaid Services Company Ltd. was granted an EU wide e money license by the British Financial Services Authority FSA allowing it to issue electronic money throughout the European Union. Acquired Dutch competitor Wallie. Acquired by Skrill one of Europe's largest payment providers. Skrill acquired British competitor Ukash and merged it into the PaysafeCard brand.

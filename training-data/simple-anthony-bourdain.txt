Anthony Michael Bourdain June June was an American celebrity chef author and television personality known as one of the most influential chefs in the world. Bourdain first became known for his book Kitchen Confidential Adventures in the Culinary Underbelly. His first food and world travel television series was A Cook's Tour which ran for episodes on the Food Network from through . In Bourdain began hosting the Travel Channel's culinary and cultural adventure programs Anthony Bourdain No Reservations and The Layover . In he switched to CNN to host Anthony Bourdain Parts Unknown. On June Bourdain was found dead of an apparent suicide by hanging in his hotel room in Kaysersberg Vignoble Haut Rhin France. He was working on an episode of Anthony Bourdain Parts Unknown in Strasbourg France. He was years old. At the time of his death he was in a relationship with actress Asia Argento.

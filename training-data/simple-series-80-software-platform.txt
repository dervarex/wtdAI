Series formerly Crystal is a mobile software platform introduced in that uses Symbian. It was made by Nokia for their enterprise and professional level smartphones. All devices that use it have a screen resolution of pixels and a full QWERTY keyboard. The large size of the screens are used to good effect but software has to be developed specifically for it and the market for it was relatively small. The last Series device is the Nokia i announced in and shipped in . The last Communicator the Nokia E Communicator released in uses S rd Edition instead of Series .

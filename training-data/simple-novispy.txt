NoviSpy is a spyware program for Android mobile phones. It is used by government agencies in Serbia including the police and the Security Information Agency BIA . The software became known in late after Amnesty International reported that it was used to spy on journalists activists and political opposition leaders. In December Amnesty International published a report about NoviSpy called A Digital Prison Surveillance and the Suppression of Civil Society in Serbia. Researchers found that police used the spyware against people who joined public protests especially protests against lithium mining in Serbia. Most spyware programs try to hack phones from far away. NoviSpy is different because government officers usually need physical access to the phone. Police officers installed NoviSpy on people's phones when they were stopped at border crossings or held in police stations. When installed NoviSpy allows officers to Read private chat messages Track the location of the phone Turn on the microphone to listen to conversations See saved photos and files Reports also show that police used specialized hardware made by the company Cellebrite to unlock phones before installing NoviSpy.

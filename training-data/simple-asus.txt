ASUSTeK or more commonly ASUS is a Taiwanese computer hardware and electronics company. It was founded in Taipei in by T.H. Tung Ted Hsu Wayne Hsieh and M.T. Liao. ASUS is the world's fifth largest PC vendor by unit sales after Lenovo HP Dell and Acer . The name ASUS originates from Pegasus the winged horse of Greek mythology. ASUS' products include laptops tablet computers desktop computers mobile phones personal digital assistants PDAs servers computer monitors motherboards graphics cards sound cards optical disc drives computer networking devices computer cases computer components and computer cooling systems. ASUS Republic of Gamers ASUS ROG is a brand used by ASUS since encompassing a range of computer hardware personal computers peripherals and accessories oriented primarily toward PC gaming. The line includes both desktops and high spec laptops. In September PC Pro discovered through a reader that ASUS had accidentally shipped laptops that contained cracked and unlicensed software. In February a security vulnerability in the AiCloud functions on a number of ASUS routers. In March Kaspersky Lab researchers disclosed a supply chain attack that affected the ASUS Live. In May hardware reviewer Gamers Nexus sent a ROG Ally in for warranty repair due to a faulty thumbstick. ASUS denied the warranty repair. A few days later ASUS made the repair under pressure from Gamers Nexus ASUS also apologised for the poor service.

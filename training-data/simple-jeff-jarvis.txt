Jeff Jarvis born July is an American journalist professor and public speaker. He is known for his ideas about the internet and how people can share information online. In Jeff Jarvis had prostate cancer. He was treated and got better. He is married to a woman named Tammy but her last name is not known. Jarvis started his career as a journalist in at a small newspaper. Later he worked for big news companies like the New York Daily News and as a television critic for magazines like TV Guide and People. He also helped create the magazine Entertainment Weekly. In he became a professor at the City University of New York's Graduate School of Journalism. He teaches about new media and how the internet is changing the news. He has a popular blog called BuzzMachine where he writes about new media. He has also written several books including What Would Google Do . In his work he often talks about the importance of being open and sharing on the internet.

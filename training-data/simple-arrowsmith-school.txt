The Arrowsmith School is a private school in Toronto Ontario. It is for children in Grades to that have learning disabilities The Arrowsmith School was first founded in Toronto in . It was founded by Barbara Arrowsmith Young. A second location was opened in May in Peterborough Ontario. The Eaton Arrowsmith School is modelled on the Toronto school. It was founded by Howard Eaton. That school was opened in in Vancouver British Columbia. It has two other schools in Canada and one in the United States. The school uses a program known as the Arrowsmith Program. This program was created by Arrowsmith Young in . She created it from exercises that she had created for herself in . She has said that they helped her to overcome her own learning difficulties. Her own problems with learning disability are talked about in her book The Woman Who Changed Her Brain. She says that her way of doing things is based on research into neuroplasticity. Her research suggests that the brain is always changing and rewiring itself. The program has been used into other public and private schools in Canada the United States Australia and New Zealand. Several cognitive psychologists and neuroscientists do not think it is a good program. Barbara Arrowsmith Young founded the original Toronto school in with her and her then husband Joshua Cohen. They created it to teach learning disabled children. They taught the children by using the program and exercises that Arrowsmith Young had begun creating for herself in . She said the program helped her to overcome her own learning difficulties. In Arrowsmith Young's book The Woman Who Changed Her Brain she said she used her middle name for the school in honor of her paternal grandmother As a young girl her grandmother had been one of the pioneer settlers of Creston British Columbia. The Toronto school grew slowly over time. In she and Cohen chose to open a second school in Brooklyn New York and stop using the school in Toronto.By the New York school had closed. The marriage of Arrowsmith Young and Cohen had also ended. She returned to Toronto and re opened the school there. The school later moved to its present location in the Forest Hill neighborhood of Toronto. Barbara Arrowsmith Young is still the director and owner of the school. She is also the owner and director of a second branch of the school in Peterborough Ontario. It was opened in . Both branches have had a growing numbers of students from outside Canada. This happened after Arrowsmith Young's speaking tour to New Zealand Australia and the United Kingdom. This tour was for her to talk about her book The Woman Who Changed Her Brain. In October about one third of the students the Peterborough branch were from other countries. Seven students were from Australia one student was from the United Arab Emirates and one student was from the United States . In Howard Eaton opened the Eaton Arrowsmith School in Vancouver. It is modelled on the Arrowsmith School in Toronto. The Eaton Arrowmith School opened branches in British Columbia at Victoria in and White Rock in . Eaton opened a branch in the United States at Redmond Washington. This school the Eaton Arrowsmith Academy opened in September . Eaton is the owner and director of all four Eaton Arrowsmith schools. In his book The Brain That Changes Itself Norman Doidge a Canadian psychiatrist and psychoanalyst wrote an entire chapter about Arrowsmith Young and the Arrowsmith Program. In it he writes about Arrowsmith Young dealing with her learning disabilities and how she created the program. He also includes some small case histories of children and adults who he says were helped by the program. No actual data to support this was presented. He says that what she has done was an important discovery. He also said that it had major implications for education . However he also says that the Arrowsmith Program has been controversial. There has been a large amount of doubt and criticism from several psychologists neuroscientists and learning experts. The main reason for this is the lack of scientific evidence that the program does what it says it does. and on its underlying rationale which its critics say represents an oversimplification and misapplication of neuroscientific concepts. In Canada neuroscientist Adele Diamond and cognitive psychologist Linda Siegel were on a CBC documentary about the Arrowsmith program. It was filmed at the Arrowsmith School in Toronto. Siegel was very critical about the program. Part of what she said about the program was removed before it was broadcast after Arrowsmith Young's lawyers threatened the CBC with a lawsuit for libel. Siegel was wrote a report to the Vancouver School Board VSB about The Arrowsmith program.

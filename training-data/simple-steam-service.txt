Steam is an internet based digital distribution digital rights management multiplayer and social networking program developed and distributed by Valve Corporation. Steam has its own store where the user can purchase and or add video games and other software to the player's game library. It is available for Microsoft Windows OS X and Linux and also has an application with fewer functions for smartphones which is available for Android and iOS. Steam can also be used as a communication software meaning that you can talk with people if they are your friends on Steam.

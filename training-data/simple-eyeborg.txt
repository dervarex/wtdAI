An eyeborg or eye borg is an electronic eye that allows people to hear colors. It is mostly used by blind people or by people with color blindness. It works by using a head mounted camera that reads the colors directly in front of a person and converts them into musical notes. The first eyeborg was made in England in by Adam Montandon in collaboration with colourblind artist Neil Harbisson. The invention won a British award in Innovation Submerge and a European award in Content Tools and Interface Design Europrix . In Peter Kese a software developer from Slovenia made further developments to the eyeborg by increasing the number of colors to and adding color intensity through different volume levels. Matias Lizana a student from Universitat Polit cnica de Catalunya is currently developing the eyeborg into a smaller chip as part of his final year project. The new chip will allow users to hear colors in stereo and to embed the device.

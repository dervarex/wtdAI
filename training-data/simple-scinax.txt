Scinax is a genus of frogs snouted treefrogs in the family Hylidae. These frogs live in eastern and southern Mexico to Argentina and Uruguay Trinidad and Tobago and Saint Lucia. These are small to medium sized tree frogs. Duellman and Wiens brought this genus back into use in . The name originates from the Greek word skinos meaning fast moving or nimble.

Glennys Ramona Rosario De la Cruz born October in Santo Domingo the Dominican Republic is a businesswoman. She studied social communications then co founder of iBAN Online. iBAN Online is a fintech company that manages online credits in the Dominican Republic and Mexico. She studied at the Catholic University of Santo Domingo in the Dominican Republic. In she began her professional career at working for local and international companies dedicated to the import and wholesale sales of natural medicine products and technological equipment. Glennys also worked for a global resort chain. At these companies she developed her knowledge of administration customer service B C and B B sales team management distribution chains and digital marketing. In at age Rosario De la Cruz co founded iBAN Online in the Dominican Republic. In Glennys won The Pitch RD national competition obtaining an initial capital grant from the Republic of China Taiwan. In iBAN Online participated in the constitutive assembly as a founding member of the Dominican Fintech Association ADOFINTECH . In with three years of operation iBAN Online was located in one of the most important business centers in Santo Domingo Citi Tower with more than employees. In iBAN Online expanded its operations to Mexico City with Rosario De la Cruz assuming the CEO role. In with the outbreak of the COVID pandemic iBAN Online Dominican Republic and Mexico launched a social commitment promotion in which the company offered extensions to support entrepreneurs.

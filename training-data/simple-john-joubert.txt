John Pierre Herman Joubert March ndash January was a South African born British composer who worked in choral works. A music academic at the universities of Hull and Birmingham for years Joubert took early retirement in to concentrate on composing and remained active into his s. Though perhaps his best known for his choral music were the carols Torches and There is No Rose of Such Virtue and the anthem O Lorde the Maker of Al Thing. Joubert composed over works including two symphonies four concertos and seven operas. Joubert was born in Cape Town South Africa. He studied at Diocesan College South African College of Music and at the Royal Academy of Music. Joubert lived in Moseley a suburb of Birmingham England for over years at the time of his death.

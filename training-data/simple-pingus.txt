Pingus is an open source video game made by German developer Ingo Ruhnke. It plays like Lemmings but has penguins instead of lemmings. Pingus has levels grouped into 'level sets' plus winter themed tutorial levels. Levels usually must be completed in order. Many other levels some with very different graphics cannot be found in game. The next major version . . released on August moved the game from ClanLib to Simple DirectMedia Layer SDL . This move which started on February made it easier to make non Windows and non Linux versions.

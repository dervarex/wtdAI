The Raaberbahn or GySEV is a railway company from Hungary and Austria based in Sopron. Hungary owns and Austria owns of the company. In Hungarian it is called Gy r Sopron Ebenfurti Vas t GySEV and in German it was called Raab Oedenburg Ebenfurther Eisenbahn ROeEE until when it was renamed Raaberbahn. The first railway line between the Hungarian cities of Gy r and Sopron opened on January . The section from Sopron to Ebenfurth opened for use on October . The company's ownership stayed the same after the end of the Austro Hungarian Empire. The Hungarian State holds and the Austrian State . The electrification of the Gy r Sopron railway finished in and on May seven V locomotives came in place of the old M diesel locomotives. Today Raaberbahn runs six main railway lines in two countries and with its European Economic Area license transports train passengers in both countries and freight across Europe.

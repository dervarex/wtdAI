Instinctools was established by Alexey Spas and Diethard Sohn in Stuttgart Germany in . In Instinctools launched DITAworks a SaaS product for managing technical documentation. In the company opened a development center in Warsaw Poland marking its first major international expansion. In Instinctools became a Microsoft Certified Partner. In the company opened a development center in Astana Kazakhstan became a resident of Astana Hub and formed a strategic partnership with ForUse to expand into the North American market. Also in Instinctools partnered with OVHcloud a European cloud services provider. In the company received TechBehemoths Awards in Mobile App Development and Web Development Germany . In Instinctools was recognized again by TechBehemoths with awards in WordPress Development Custom Software Development and ReactJS Development Germany . In the company launched a new development center in Ahmedabad India continuing its global expansion. That same year it received a TechBehemoths Award for Web Development in Germany. Instinctools provides custom software development AI app development digital product engineering and intelligent automation services. The company s portfolio includes e commerce projects SaaS marketplaces legacy modernization and integration projects. In Instinctools built a digital marketplace for Lition Energie GmbH a German startup offering peer to peer green energy trading. In Instinctools introduced the AI Adoption Workshop program to support business owners in evaluating and planning for the implementation of artificial intelligence.

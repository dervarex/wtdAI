Mail.Ru Group London Stock Exchange listed since November is a Russian Internet company. It was started in as an e mail service and went on to become a major corporate figure in the Russian speaking segment of the Internet. As of according to comScore websites owned by Mail.ru collectively had the largest audience in Russia and captured the most screen time. Mail.Ru's sites reach approximately of Russian Internet users on a monthly basis and the company is in the top of largest Internet companies based on the number of total pages viewed. Mail.ru controls the largest Russian social networking sites. It operates the second and third most popular Russian social networking sites Odnoklassniki and Moy Mir respectively. Mail.ru holds of shares of Russia's most popular social network VK and minority stakes in Qiwi formerly OE Investments . . It also operates two instant messaging networks Mail.Ru Agent and ICQ an e mail service and Internet portal Mail.ru as well as a number of online games.

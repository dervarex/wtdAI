Wikiquote is a sister project of Wikipedia. It is one of many projects run by the Wikimedia Foundation. Wikiquote was based on an idea by Daniel Alston and made by Brion Vibber. The goal of the project is to produce a large reference of quotations from famous people books and proverbs and to give details about them. The project was first created in English. Meanwhile in July more languages were added.

John P. Sall born is an American billionaire and software programmer who co founded SAS Institute and created JMP statistical software. John Sall was born in Rockford Illinois in and was interested in science as a child. He got a bachelor's degree in history from Beloit College in Beloit Wisconsin and then went to Northern Illinois University NIU where he got a master's degree in economics. While he was studying at NIU Sall became interested in statistics and computer science. He went to study statistics at North Carolina State University where he was given an honorary doctorate in . He received an honorary doctorate from NIU in . John Sall worked with programmer James Goodnight at North Carolina State University. In they founded the company SAS Institute with Anthony James Barr and Jane Helwig in founding SAS Institute. The company develops SAS software which is used for machine learning data analytics data management and artificial intelligence. Sall created many parts of the SAS language including procedures for working with time series econometrics categorical data and matrix algebraic manipulations. Sall started creating JMP which originally stood for John's Macintosh Project in the s and it was released in October . He is still JMP's chief architect. He co wrote the book JMP Start Statistics with Ann Lehman and Lee Creighton. Sall lives in Cary North Carolina. He is married and has four children. Sall and his wife work in charity and conservation through the Sall Family Foundation. Sall was on the board of The Nature Conservancy from to and is a member of the board for the Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History. Sall and his wife also work with the World Wide Fund for Nature WWF CARE Pan American Health Organization and other non profits. They contributed to the founding of Cary Academy a college prep school. Sall is a Fellow of the American Statistical Association in and the American Association for the Advancement of Science. He is a member of the North Carolina State University board of trustees. Sall owns about one third of SAS Institute while Goodnight owns the rest of the company.

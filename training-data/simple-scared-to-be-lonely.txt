Scared to Be Lonely is a song by Dutch DJ Martin Garrix and English singer Dua Lipa. It went to the top ten in Austria Belgium Czech Republic Denmark Finland Germany Hungary Malaysia the Netherlands New Zealand Norway Portugal Scotland Slovakia Sweden and Switzerland. It also reached in Australia and the United Kingdom in Ireland and in the United States.

TR is a protocol that is mainly used for the communication with end user devices such as DSL modems. The number of such devices is rising steadily and they are often very difficult to configure. For that reason TR allows to configure these modems remotely. This also poses problems because it allows to change the configuration the managed device as well as other devices which may be behind a firewall. Since the users are not aware of the configuration changes TR can be used to introduce a backdoor. This backdoor might allow a remote search by police or legal authorities of a country.

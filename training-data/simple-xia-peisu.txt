Xia Peisu was a Chinese computer scientist and Teacher. She is known for her research in computer science and technology. She has been called the Mother of Computer Science in China . Xia took the lead on the team that made the Model . This was the first computer designed locally in China. She and her husband Yang Liming were elected to the Chinese Academy of Sciences in . In she was given the first Lifetime Achievement Award from the China Computer Federation. Xia was born on July in Chongqing Sichuan. Her grandfather Xia Fengxun had spent forty years as a teacher. Her father Xia Hongru ran a school in Jiangjin County Chongqing. Her mother Huang Xiaoyong was a teacher at the Jiangjin Girls' Middle School and headmistress of Jiangjin Girls' Primary School. Xia learned ancient Chinese prose and mathematics at a young age. At fourteen years old she was accepted into Nanyu Secondary School now Chongqing Nankai Secondary School . In this school she was at the top of her class. In Xia moved to the National No. Nine Middle School in Jiangjin County. She graduated high school in at the top of her class. After that she started in electrical engineering at the National Central University. She later earned a bachelor's degree. After this she did postgraduate studies at the Telecommunications Research Institute of National Chiao Tung University Chongqing from to . In she went to the University of Edinburgh in the United Kingdom. She earned a doctorate there in . Xia and her husband Yang Liming returned to China in . She then became a researcher in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Tsinghua University. In mathematician Hua Luogeng started work on China's first electronic computer. He recruited Xia and two other scientists to lead the project. Later the two other scientists left the project which left Xia to be the only leader of it. Under her leadership the project made Model in . This was the first electric computer designed in China. Over the years her work resulted in other advances and discoveries. In March Xia taught China's first course in computer theory. She also wrote Principles of the Electronic Computer which was the first computer science textbook in China. When the University of Science and Technology of China was founded in Xia set up its computer science department. She taught more than students from to . She helped to start the Chinese Journal of Computers in and the Journal of Computer Science and Technology in . Xia advised more than graduate students. Two of these won top national prizes for their theses. She taught who led the development of the Sugon supercomputers. She also taught who designed the Loongson CPU. When the first locally designed CPU in China was made in it was named Xia in her honor. In Xia and her husband were elected to the Chinese Academy of Sciences. She also received first Lifetime Achievement Award from the China Computer Federation. In Xia met Yang Liming. They married in when they were both at the University of Edinburgh. Yang later became a theoretical physicist. They had two sons Yang Yuenian and Yang Yuemin. Xia died on August aged . Xia Peisu at the National Museum for Modern Chinese Scientists

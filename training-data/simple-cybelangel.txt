CybelAngel is a French cybersecurity company. It works in external threat intelligence digital risk protection and attack surface management. The company was founded in Paris in by Erwan Keraudy Stevan Keraudy and Matthieu Finiasz. CybelAngel makes software and services that help find exposed data stolen passwords and other cyber risks outside company networks. CybelAngel was founded in Paris in by two brothers Erwan and Stevan Keraudy and cryptography researcher Matthieu Finiasz. Before founding the company Erwan Keraudy worked in finance. Stevan Keraudy worked in technology and machine learning. In CybelAngel's technology was used to find exposed information linked to the Ashley Madison data breach. CybelAngel provides cybersecurity services that find risks outside company networks. Its tools include checking external attack surfaces protecting digital assets threat intelligence third party risk checks monitoring the dark web and finding exposed data. The platform uses automatic systems and human experts to find leaked information stolen passwords and other cyber threats that affect companies.

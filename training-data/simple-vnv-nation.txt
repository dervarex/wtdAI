VNV Nation are an electronic music band originally from Wexford and London now based in Hamburg that combines elements of trance synthpop and electronic body music EBM into what they call futurepop. Earlier works also include some electro industrial influences. The members are Ronan Harris and Mark Jackson. The VNV in the name stands for Victory not Vengeance in keeping with the group's motto One should strive to achieve not sit in bitter regret.

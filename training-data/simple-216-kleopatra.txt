Kleopatra is a Main belt asteroid found by Johann Palisa on April in Pola. It is named after Cleopatra the Queen of Egypt. It is a dumbbell shaped M class metallic asteroid. Kleopatra is an unusual object. Its odd shape was revealed by adaptive optics on the ESO . m telescope at La Silla. By bouncing radar signals off the asteroid a team of astronomers at the Arecibo radio telescope in Puerto Rico were able to develop a more detailed computer model of its shape which confirmed the dog bone like shape. A favored explanation is that Kleopatra is a contact binary two similarly sized asteroids that have hit and stuck together instead of breaking apart. Kleopatra is a fairly big asteroid measuring nbsp km. It is believed to be a loosely packed metallic object based on its radar albedo.

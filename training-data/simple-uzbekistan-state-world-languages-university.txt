Uzbekistan State University of World Languages abbreviated as UzSWLU Uzbek O zbekiston davlat jahon tillari universiteti abbreviated as O zDJTU is a public university in Tashkent Uzbekistan. Islam Karimov established this university by merging two language institutions in . The University has faculties Roman German Philology Journalism Translation Studies Russian Philology and three English faculties and offers bachelor and master degrees in language related fields. It has also the main national responsibility for training and requalification ESL teachers creating and updating language textbooks teaching methods strategies in Uzbekistan. The University also has its own printing service and coordinates the Centre for Development of Language Teaching Methods. UzSWLU is relatively young institution established by Islam Karimov former Uzbekistan's President. In its current form It was created in by the merger of two higher institutes in Tashkent as follows Tashkent Pedagogical Institute of Foreign Languages established in . Russian Language and Literature Institute established in . In the University established a lyceum that prepares middle school students to enter the University and also teaches language and humanitarian subjects in depth. Prior to this the University contained three lyceums but with the introduction of an eleven year educational school this number was reduced to one. This lyceum is on Bobur Street in Tashkent.

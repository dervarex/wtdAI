Text is a Polish software company based in Wroc aw Poland. It was previously called LiveChat Software. The company develops software for customer service business communication and artificial intelligence. Its products include LiveChat ChatBot HelpDesk Knowledge Base and OpenWidget. The company is listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. The company was founded in Wroc aw in as LiveChat. At first it mainly developed live chat software for businesses. In Capital Partners bought of the company. In the shares were sold to Gadu Gadu which made LiveChat part of the Naspers group. In the company's founders and managers bought back a controlling share of the business. They worked with the private equity company Tar Heel Capital to buy the share owned by Naspers. LiveChat Software joined the Warsaw Stock Exchange in April . In LiveChat Software changed its name to Text. The company said the new name reflected its wider product range and its growing focus on artificial intelligence and e commerce. The company has offices in Wroc aw and Boston Massachusetts and serves customers in many countries. Text is a group of customer service and sales products. It can connect with services such as Shopify and WhatsApp. LiveChat is software that allows businesses to communicate with customers through website chat and other communication channels. ChatBot is a platform for creating automated chatbots for businesses. It was first released as BotEngine in . The platform can connect with services including LiveChat Facebook Messenger Slack and other communication tools. HelpDesk is software for managing customer support requests. Messages are collected as tickets which can be given different categories and statuses. Knowledge Base is a tool for creating help centers. Companies can use it for internal information for employees or for public support pages for customers. OpenWidget is a free website widget launched in . It can add contact forms feedback forms product recommendations and FAQ tools to websites. The company has served tens of thousands of paying customers in more than countries. Customers have included companies such as Adobe AirAsia ING Huawei Orange and PayPal. In Deloitte listed LiveChat Software among the fastest growing technology companies in Central Europe in its Technology Fast ranking. In the company was also named as a finalist by Red Herring in its list of European technology companies.

The International Statistical Classification of Diseases and Related Health Problems th Revision ICD is list of codes that classify diseases and medical problems. The ICD is published by the World Health Organization. There have been many revisions or updated versions of the ICD. Work on the latest version ICD began in and was finished in . The ICD gives a special unique code to Every medical condition including mental health problems Specific symptoms if it is not clear what condition is causing the symptom. For example if a person has a cough but his doctor does not know why he would just use the ICD code for Cough . Changes in the body or test results that are not normal if it is not clear what condition is causing these changes . Injuries or illnesses that are caused by things outside of the body for example broken bones burns and poisonings Social issues that may cause health problems for example if a person's job causes health risks or if the person's health is at risk because of poverty .

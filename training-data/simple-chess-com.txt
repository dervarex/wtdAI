Chess.com is an internet chess server where people can go and play chess online. The site is the largest online chess platform in the world. The site has a freemium model meaning that some features are free but a paid subscription is required to access more features such as unlimited puzzles or unlimited game review. Users can go and play time controls such as bullet blitz rapid and more. On December Chess.com stated that they reached million users. As of April it had million daily active users.

D ZB TV channel analog channel digital also known as GMA TV Batangas or GMA Southern Tagalog is a commercial television station. It is owned by GMA Network Inc. Its studio complex is located at PNG Bldg. P. Burgos St. cor. Rizal Ave. Batangas City Batangas Province its transmitter is located at Mt. Banoy Bo. Talumpok East Batangas City Batangas Province.

The Bangladesh Manipuri Sahitya Sangsad is a literary society that works for the development of Meitei language officially called Manipuri language and culture in the People's Republic of Bangladesh. Its name is usually shortened as BAMSAS. However instead of BAMSAS it is also shortened as BMSS in many cases. The Bangladesh Manipuri Sahitya Sangsad was established in the year in Bangladesh. It used to publish a magazine named Meira . In it published the first ever Bangladeshi Meitei language poetry book Basanta Kunnipalaji Leibang . It is the first of its kind in Bangladesh. In it published another book Bangladesher Manipuri Kabita consisting of Meitei language poems authored by Meitei poets of Bangladesh. Later other books of Meitei language poems Myang Mapei Marakta and Wakhalji Nachom were also published. Some books on Meitei cultural topics were published in Bengali language. Bangladesh Manipuri Sahitya Sangsad annually gives awards to notable writers of Meitei literature Manipuri literature and culture. The Nilima Sinha Memorial Award consists of a certificate an Innaphi a crest a cash of Bangladeshi Taka as well as a free round trip travel fares for two people. In the Nilima Sinha Memorial Award for Manipuri Literature and Culture was given to Sahitya Akademi Awardee Kshetri Bir of Kakching Manipur for his contributions to the Meitei literature. In the Sinam Tanubabu Memorial International Award was given to Mutum Ramani Devi from Kwakeithel Thokchom Leikai of Manipur India for her work named Fongngamdraba Cheinakhol . The Rajkumar Sitaljit Memorial Award is given usually as a part of the International Manipuri Short Story Festival organised by the BAMSAS. However it is the lifetime achievement award given by Manipuri Short Story Society India. In the Rajkumar Sitaljit Memorial Award was given to AK Seram from Sylhet Bangladesh. The Bangladesh Manipuri Sahitya Sangsad annually organises the International Manipuri Short Story Festival IMSSF for consecutive days. In it was organised at the Court Point in Sylhet Bangladesh. It was organised in collaboration with the Manipuri Short Story Society India. writers from India Manipur and Assam took part in the event. The BMSS also annually organises the Manipuri Bhasha Utshab also known as Manipuri Language Festival or Meitei Language Festival in Bangladesh. During the event Meitei language article writing contests songs performances traditional Meitei dance performances aes usually done. In October Bangladesh Manipuri Sahitya Sangsad BAMSAS received the representative people of the Patriotic Writers Forum Manipur PAWF from Manipur in the Holly Inn Sylhet for the celebration of Ningol Chakkouba in Bangladesh.

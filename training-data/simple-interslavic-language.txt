Interslavic is a constructed language meant to be used by Slavic people. It is like a modern version of the Old Church Slavonic language of the th century. It is based on words and other things that exist in all Slavic languages. The idea is that Slavs from any country can understand it without learning it first. The first Interslavic grammar was written in by Juraj Kri ani a priest from Croatia. The modern form was started in by a team of linguists. The name of that project was Slovianski. In it merged with another project called Neoslavonic. Since that time the name of the language is Interslavic Med uslovjansky . Interslavic can be written in the Latin and Cyrillic alphabets.

Thongak Lairembi also known as Langol Lairemma is a primordial goddess in Meitei mythology and religion. She is the divine female personification of the death. She guards the door of the entrance to the underworld which is the land of the death ruled by her consort Thongalel. Legend says she gets departed from her husband when Thongalel was lost in the battle between the gods of the underworld and those of the upper world in the hands of Salailen Sidaba. The winner asked the goddess who is the wife of the loser to be the gatekeeper of the entrance gate to the underworld and for Thongalel he gave him Khamnung Kikoi Louonbi in return. The name Thongak Lairembi t . ak.p . lai.rem.bi is made up of two Meitei language Manipuri language words Thongak t . ak. and Lairembi lai.rem.bi . Thongak adjectival form of noun word Thongngakpa t . ak.p means doorkeeper or gatekeeper in Meitei language Manipuri language . Lairembi lai.rem.bi means goddess or a female divinity in Meitei language Manipuri language . Every year the Lai Haraoba festival is celebrated in honor of goddess Thongak Lairembi .

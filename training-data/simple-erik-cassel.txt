Erik Cassel December February was an American computer engineer and software developer best known as the co founder of Roblox. He was very important in helping with the platform's early technology and also helping its engineering team. Prior to Roblox Cassel was a Vice President of Engineering at Knowledge Revolution where he helped with the creation of educational physics simulation software. Cassel's interest in computers began in childhood when he was fascinated by punch card machines and later acquired a TRS from Radio Shack. This passion led him to study computer science at Cornell University graduating in with a bachelor's degree. After graduating Cassel joined Knowledge Revolution in a software company co founded by David Baszucki. The firm specialized in educational physics simulation tools most notably Interactive Physics which allowed high school students to conduct virtual experiments. Cassel quickly rose to the position of Vice President of Engineering. His work was a major factor in the company's success culminating in its acquisition in for . nbsp million. Following the acquisition Cassel and Baszucki began planning a new venture that would merge physics simulation with online social interaction. Inspired by early s social networking trends Baszucki proposed the idea to Cassel in . Initially considering names such as GoBlocks and DynaBlocks they ultimately chose Roblox. The Roblox Corporation was incorporated in March and the platform officially launched on September nbsp . In its early days Cassel and Baszucki managed all aspects of the platform from coding to community engagement. They soon shifted focus to user generated content allowing players to create and share their own games and experiences. As head of engineering Cassel oversaw the introduction of several foundational features including hats the first egg hunt and limited items. He advocated for innovations such as animated avatars and bubble chat which later became defining aspects of the platform. In Cassel was diagnosed with an aggressive form of brain cancer. Despite his illness he continued to work training engineers and ensuring the platform's technical vision remained intact. He made public appearances at the inaugural Roblox Rally in and the Roblox Game Conference in . Cassel died on February nbsp at the age of . In his honor Roblox released his iconic hat as a limited time item. His family including his two sons has remained connected to the company. Cassel is remembered as a super programmer and a central figure in Roblox's history. His influence continues to shape the platform's development and community.

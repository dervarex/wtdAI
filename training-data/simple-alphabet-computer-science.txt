In computer science an alphabet is a finite non empty set. The elements of an alphabet are called the letters or symbols of the alphabet. An example of an alphabet is cdot which may be used for Morse code or begin if else for while which may be the keywords of a Programming language. It is named after the mathematician Stephen Cole Kleene. The Kleene star of the binary alphabet is lambda .

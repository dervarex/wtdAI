Lusophones or Lus fonos in Portuguese are all people who have Portuguese as their native or official language. They are called this because of the Portuguese colonization and maritime expansion that began in the th century. This process spread the language and culture to various continents. The word Luso comes from Lusitania an ancient Roman province that corresponds to a large part of Portugal and fono means sound voice. The Portuguese speaking countries form the so called Community of Portuguese Language Countries or in Portuguese Comunidade dos Pa ses de L ngua Portuguesa CPLP . There are approximately million native speakers and million who have the language as a second language. The official Portuguese speaking countries are Africa Angola Cape Verde Guinea Bissau Equatorial Guinea Mozambique and S o Tom and Pr ncipe. In addition to these nations there are historical communities of speakers in regions such as Macau China and Goa India .

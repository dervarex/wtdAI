Dennis MacAlistair Ritchie September October was an American computer scientist who helped shape the digital era. He created the C programming language and with long time colleague Ken Thompson the UNIX operating system. Ritchie and Thompson received the Turing Award from the ACM in the Hamming Medal from the IEEE in and the National Medal of Technology from President Clinton in . Ritchie was the head of Lucent Technologies System Software Research Department when he retired in . He was the 'R' in K R C.

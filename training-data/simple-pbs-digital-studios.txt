PBS Digital Studios is a non profit organization. It is based in Arlington Virginia. It has original series and partnerships with other YouTube channels. Most of the series are about science popular culture art food news and music. PBS Digital Studios was founded by Jason Seiken in June . They made their first popular video with a remix of autotuned vocals from Mr. Rogers' Neighborhood. It is called Garden of Your Mind. The PBS Digital Studios network has over million views. They have over million subscribers. They have popular series including Crash Course Blank on Blank It's Okay To Be Smart and the multiple Webby Award winning PBS Idea Channel. Each month the shows have more than million streams. Their first series Frankenstein MD started on August . It ran until October . In PBS Digital Studios partnered with the Green brothers' series Crash Course. They made courses in astronomy U.S. government and philosophy. Crash Course Astronomy started on January . Crash Course U.S. Government Politics started on January . Crash Course Philosophy started on February .

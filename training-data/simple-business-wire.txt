Business Wire is a company that sends out full text press releases from thousands of companies and organizations worldwide to news media financial markets disclosure systems investors information web sites databases bloggers social networks and other audiences. It is a subsidiary of Berkshire Hathaway. Business Wire was founded in by Lorry I. Lokey. It started by sending releases to media outlets in California. Business Wire created its website in May . In Business Wire was the first commercial newswire to end the practice of distributing news to financial outlets minutes before anyone else to provide immediate equal access to company information as noted by Reg FD. Business Wire's first wholly owned European operation launched in with the opening of an office in London. On June Business Wire entered the German Ad Hoc market with a disclosure network for companies with primary or secondary listings on the Deutsche Boerse. The service was established in compliance with BaFin Germany's national securities regulator. On September Business Wire established Regulatory Disclosure networks in France Sweden Switzerland and Luxembourg. The move came in planning for the organization of the EU's Transparency Obligations Directive TOD . On January Autorit des march s financiers the French Financial Markets Regulator approved Business Wire to operate as a Regulatory Disclosure Service in France. On January Business Wire established an Asian hub with the opening of its Tokyo Japan bureau and later that year added Japanese language making the website available in seven languages. On January Berkshire Hathaway noted that it was purchasing Business Wire for an undisclosed amount. This acquisition was completed on March . In Business Wire partnered with ImpreMedia to launch LatinoWire a news distribution service that sends out brand content to Hispanic websites media outlets journalists and bloggers. In Business Wire received a patent for the technological process of optimizing and distributing press releases to maximize their ability to be found and tracked in leading search engines. On September Business Wire noted an exclusive distribution partnership with VentureBeat. On November Business Wire opened its nd office in Hong Kong. In January Business Wire launched a new news and content distribution enhancement service.

Materialize NV based in Leuven Belgium is a company active in the field of D printing offering both software and parts manufacturing. Materialize was founded in June in Louvain by Wilfried Vancraen . In the company released the first elements of the Mimics software suite and in the Magics software suite. The first of these suites Mimics transforms stacks of D images eg DICOM files into a D model and calculates the area of these D models. Initial data can be obtained by computed tomography confocal microscopy microtomography or microscopic tomography micro CT or CT or magnetic resonance imaging MRI via image segmentation. The second Magics makes it possible to optimize the printing result to simulate the process to manage the mesh structures to generate supports to import data for example in CAD formats and to export them in particular in D STL files. These software suites then evolve regularly . At the same time the company sets up web services allowing its customers to express manufacturing needs or to pilot D prints.

If We Ever Meet Again is a pop dance song by American producer Timbaland featuring Katy Perry released on February . It is from Timbaland's third studio album Shock Value II and later added to Perry's deluxe third studio album Teenage Dream. This song reached number one in New Zealand and the Czech Republic and also peaked in the top ten in countries.

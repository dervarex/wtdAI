BlaBlaCar is a French online marketplace for carpooling based in Paris. Its website and mobile apps connect drivers with passengers travelling between cities so they can share the cost of a journey. The company earns a commission of between and on bookings. BlaBlaCar also operates BlaBlaCar Bus an intercity bus service. As of the platform had about million active users and operated across Europe and Latin America. The idea for BlaBlaCar came in when founder Fr d ric Mazzella could not find transport to visit his family for Christmas. He noticed that many cars on the road had empty seats and began working on a service to connect drivers and passengers. In he bought the website Covoiturage.fr which had been created in . By Covoiturage.fr had become the largest carpooling website in France. In BlaBlaCar launched in the United Kingdom. In the company introduced online booking and expanded to Italy Portugal Poland the Netherlands Luxembourg and Belgium. In BlaBlaCar launched in Germany and Covoiturage.fr was renamed BlaBlaCar.fr. In the company expanded to Ukraine and Russia. Later that year it reached million users. In BlaBlaCar entered India and acquired several competitors including Carpooling in Germany Autohop in Central Europe and Rides in Mexico. In the company launched BlaBlaLines a service for daily commuting in France. During the same year it closed its offices in India Turkey and Mexico saying it had expanded too quickly in those markets. In BlaBlaCar acquired the French startup Less. Later that year it acquired the long distance coach operator Ouibus from SNCF. Ouibus was renamed BlaBlaBus. In BlaBlaCar acquired the Russian bus booking platform Busfor. In the company acquired Octobus a Ukrainian software company that develops ticketing and management software for bus operators. In BlaBlaCar acquired Klaxit a French company that provides carpooling services for commuters. Following Russia's invasion of Ukraine in BlaBlaCar suspended investment in its Russian subsidiary and stopped financial transfers from its headquarters. The Russian business continued operating under local management.

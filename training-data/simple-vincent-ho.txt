Dr. Vincent Ho Wai fung Chinese born in is a Hong Kong media executive filmmaker and businessperson. He has worked on short digital films for mobile devices. He is widely recognised in mainland China and Hong Kong as the Father of Micro movies . Vincent Ho studied at Hong Kong Baptist College now known as Hong Kong Baptist University. He graduated from its School of Communication in majoring in film and video. He later completed a doctoral program related to business and new media. After graduating Ho worked in Hong Kong television for about ten years. He was a program director scriptwriter and television host. In he produced World Cup programmes for Asia Television ATV featuring Michael Hui. He later worked on variety shows at Cabe TV with directors Pang Ho cheung and Lam Chiu wing. In Ho joined the Navigators web portal division at Hong Kong Telecom. He worked on digital projects including online broadcasts such as Glass Forest . In he led user experience design for online banking projects at HSBC and Hang Seng Bank. In Ho co founded GTV which supplied content to mobile phone operators. In he produced Love a series of short films made for G mobile phones. It heavily popularised the short form storytelling archetype throughout Greater China and led local media to crown him the Father of Micro movies . In Southern Media Corporation's mobile television division acquired GTV's assets. Vincent Ho has held roles in several organisations and industry groups. He is chairman of the New Media Foundation a programme consultant for Radio Television Hong Kong RTHK a judge for the Cyberport Creative Micro Fund and founding president of the HKBU Greater Bay Area Alumni Association. Ho has supported Hong Kong's pro Beijing camp. In he signed a public petition supporting the Hong Kong National Security Law. The petition later received media attention over questions about some names on its list of signatories. Vincent Ho is an appointed member of the Hong Kong Arts Development Council. he chairs the council's Arts Criticism Group and is vice chair of its Dance Group. His work focuses on combining traditional film techniques with digital media and art technology. In December Hong Kong's Culture Sports and Tourism Bureau reappointed Ho to the council for a three year term. His work at the council has included efforts to develop creative talent in the region and expand the use of digital media in Hong Kong's arts sector. He has also supported cultural preservation and art projects involving places in the Greater Bay Area. In Ho entered the China Central Television CCTV short film competition Beautiful China Extreme Shoot Hours. His film Love Xi Shi Tofu was made within hours without a fixed script. It won the National Best Short Film Gold Award. In Ho directed the historical film We Met at Songhua Lake . The film received a Special Contribution Award at the th China Golden Rooster and Hundred Flowers Film Festival.

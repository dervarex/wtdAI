KDE is a community that makes free software including a desktop environment called KDE Plasma for operating systems based on Linux or UNIX. This replaces the console which is simply a black screen with text on it. KDE Plasma is a popular piece of software for users of Linux and UNIX. Plasma uses the X Window System and Wayland which runs on many UNIX like operating systems most notably Linux. Since KDE SC . many KDE programs also run on Microsoft Windows and MacOS.

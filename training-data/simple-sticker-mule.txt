Sticker Mule is an American e commerce and printing company that produces custom stickers labels and related printed products. The company was founded in by Anthony Constantino and is headquartered in Amsterdam New York. It operates mainly through an online platform that allows customers to order custom printed items. Sticker Mule was founded in as an online printing service focused on custom stickers and short run production. Its business model relied on centralized manufacturing combined with online order processing allowing customers to submit and approve designs digitally. In the s the company expanded its manufacturing capacity and broadened its range of custom printed products while continuing to operate through a direct to consumer model. In Sticker Mule acquired Rockin Monkey a Texas based sticker manufacturer marking the company's first reported acquisition. The company has also entered into partnerships including a collaboration with the National Thoroughbred League NTL announced in . Sticker Mule has been listed among sponsors of technology industry events such as the Crunchies Awards. Sticker Mule operates a vertically integrated production model in which manufacturing fulfillment and shipping are handled internally. Orders are placed through an online interface where customers upload designs and approve proofs before production. The company has expanded beyond stickers into other categories of custom printed products while maintaining its core manufacturing model. In the company launched Studio a browser based graphic design tool intended to simplify the creation of custom designs for print and digital use. In Sticker Mule introduced Sticker Mule Stores enabling users to create and manage online storefronts for selling custom merchandise. In Sticker Mule began accepting cryptocurrency as a form of payment for its products. The company has also funded local initiatives including scholarship programs in New York State. Sticker Mule has received national media attention due to activities and public statements by its founder and CEO Anthony Constantino. In the company attracted media coverage after installing a large pro Donald Trump sign at its headquarters in Amsterdam New York. The sign and related messaging prompted public debate and legal disputes involving local authorities. In the same year the company was also reported to have sent mass emails related to the United States presidential elections which drew commentary from media and communications experts. Anthony Constantino has been involved in United States politics including a campaign for the United States House of Representatives in New York's st congressional district. Reporting by Politico and other outlets has covered campaign related activities including fundraising advertising and issues related to signature collection during the candidacy.

Made In The A.M. is the fifth and final studio album by English Irish boy band One Direction released on November by Columbia Records and Syco Music. It was the band's only album without Zayn Malik who left the group eight months earlier and the last released before the group entered an indefinite hiatus the following year. It was the year's sixth best selling album worldwide with . million copies sold and received generally favourable critical reviews. The album was preceded by the three singles Drag Me Down released on July Perfect released on October and History released on November . All three reached the top in a number of national charts. The album itself debuted at number one on the UK Albums Chart with copies and at number two behind Justin Bieber's Purpose on the US Billboard with album equivalent units in its first week including pure album sales. Defying predictions that interest in boy band albums was declining its first week sales topped the sold by the band's previous album Four.

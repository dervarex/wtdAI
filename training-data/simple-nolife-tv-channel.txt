Nolife was a French television channel. It described itself for TV for geeks nerds and otakus . Its main programming focused on video games Japanese culture technology and various other topics. Its slogan was Y a pas que la vraie vie dans la vie There's not only real life in life . Nolife was originally created as an independent television network by S bastien Ruchet and Alexandre Pilot both of them having previously worked for other French television channels through their production company Pocket Shami. Later Pilot said that they wanted to create Nolife because a lot of their projects were refused by other television networks. They took advantage of the possibility to launch a televison channel at a lower cost a few hundred Euros per month on the Free IPTV network. The channel was made for people who love video games Japanese culture and technology. Its slogan was Y a pas que la vraie vie dans la vie . This means There is not only real life in life in English. In Nolife had money problems. A game company named Ankama Games invested money into the channel to help it survive. However by the network had hard times again because it lost advertising money. It could not find new investors and closed down permanently on April . The channel was very important for internet creators in France. It helped many French web series and comedians become famous. Nolife had about one hour of new programs every day. The rest of the time it showed repeats music videos and independent shows. Some of its most popular shows were A daily news show about video games anime and tech culture. Chez Marcus A weekly gaming show hosted by a popular French gaming journalist named Marcus. J Top A weekly television chart show where viewers voted for their favorite Japanese music videos. Noob and NerdZ Popular comic web series that were broadcast on the network.

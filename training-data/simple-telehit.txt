TeleHit is a Mexican cable satellite television network. It has been broadcasting since . Its main programming is music and music videos. It is a part of Televisa Networks an affiliate of TelevisaUnivision Mexico and is also available in various countries in Latin America and United States. Telehit is MTV's second Spanish language music video network. At first it aired Spanish music. Now it airs a fusion of different musical rhythms like pop rock electronic hip hop and reggaeton. Besides music videos the channel also broadcasts concerts TV specials and general interest content for youth. Telehit has also been a platform for hosts and actors to being into Mexican show business. The Telehit Awards were first presented on November . It was made to commemorate years of airing on Mexican television. These awards recognize the most outstanding artists in music.

The iPhone Duo is a foldable smartphone developed by Apple. It is the first iPhone to feature a foldable design and has the largest display in the iPhone lineup. It was announced on September alongside the iPhone Pro and Pro Max. The device was released on October . The iPhone Duo features a titanium frame and a foldable display system. When closed the device can be used through its back display as a regular smartphone. When opened the internal display provides a bigger display. The device can be used in different positions and iOS adapts the interface depending on the position. The iPhone Duo comes in two colors Night Sky and Star White. The iPhoneDuo uses the Apple A Pro chip. The chip provides increased performance and better at graphics processing artificial intelligence and other workloads. The Duo design features a . inch outer display and a . inch inner display when unfolded. The device uses a book style folding design. The two displays have a consistent aspect ratio allowing content to transition between the folded and unfolded configurations. The inner display also incorporates a camera beneath the display for FaceTime. Videos are captured in K at nbsp fps. An under screen camera in the inner display was developed for FaceTime video calls allowing for multi camera use during calls. The iPhone Duo uses Apple's C cellular modem. The device also includes Apple's N wireless networking chip which supports Wi Fi Bluetooth and Thread. The iPhone Duo uses a dual battery system. Apple states that the device provides up to hours of battery life with the use of the two displays. The battery can charge to percent in approximately minutes using a compatible fast charger.
